Privacy

Your privacy at Web Faery.

This website

What webfaery.love collects

This site has no trackers and no cookies for advertising. If you email me, I keep your message so I can answer it. I use your email address to reply and for nothing else.

For people whose site I look after

How you show up on Google

If you are a client, I read the numbers Google Search Console keeps about your own website, once a month. This is on by default for client sites, and you can stop it or delete the numbers any time with the buttons in your portal. The monthly numbers are fetched using read-only access to Search Console data, so nothing on your Google account or your site can be changed that way.

What I read, for your site only:

  • how many times people clicked through to your site from Google last month
  • how many times your site appeared in search results
  • your average position in those results
  • the five search phrases and five pages that brought the most visits

Search Console does not tell me who the searchers were, and I do not receive their names or any way to identify them.

Who sees it

You, and me

  • You see your numbers in your Web Faery portal, on the Visitors page.
  • I (Taya) see them to look after your site.
  • I do not sell this information, share it with anyone else, or use it for advertising.
  • The small monthly totals are kept on Web Faery’s own server. They are not mixed with any other source.

Web Faery’s use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your choice

Stop it, or delete it

It is on unless you turn it off.

  • In your portal, open Visitors, then How you show up on Google, then Privacy. You can stop the tracking there, turn it back on later, or delete the past numbers.
  • Or email me at taya@webfaery.love and I will stop it and delete your numbers.
  • You can also remove my access any time in Google Search Console, under Settings, then Users and permissions. After that I cannot read anything new.
If this changes

I will say so here first

If I ever add another source of Google information, such as your Google Business Profile, this page will say so before I do, and I will ask you first.

Questions about any of this are welcome: taya@webfaery.love.
