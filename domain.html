Web address guide

Your web address, the .com part.

It’s the yourname.com people type to find you. It’s always legally yours, with you listed as its owner. I hold it for you and renew it as part of your subscription, so there’s no account for you to make and nothing to remember.

At a glance

Where it lives, and what it costs

  • Whose it is. Yours, with you listed as its legal owner.
  • Who holds it. I do, in my Porkbun account.
  • The first year. Included in your build.
  • Renewing it. Covered by your subscription.
  • Email forwarding. Mail to hello@yourname.com lands in your inbox. Free.
  • Sending from your domain address. Comes with Color and Advance.

A .com renews for about $12 a year, and some endings, like .co, cost more. Either way, it’s part of your subscription. Prices are estimates as of October 2026.

Day to day: nothing to do

Held for you

Your address lives in my Porkbun account with you listed as its legal owner, so it’s always legally yours. I renew it as part of your subscription and look after its settings. With Color and Advance, your subscription also covers sending email from your domain address, if you want it. One less thing to remember.

Porkbun’s free privacy protection keeps your home address out of public directories.

One small thing: I register it with your own email, so you’re its owner on record. When it’s first registered, an email may arrive asking you to confirm that email address. Please tap its link; it keeps your address in good standing.

Already have an address?

Wonderful. It stays yours.

Tell me where it lives (GoDaddy, Wix, Squarespace, anywhere) and you can choose:

  • Keep it where it is. We point it at your new site, and you keep renewing it there, the way you do now.
  • Or move it in with mine. It joins my Porkbun account with you still its legal owner, and your subscription renews it from then on.

Bought it (or moved it) in the last 60 days, maybe through Wix or Squarespace with an old site? Every registrar locks an address for 60 days after that, so it can’t move yet. No problem: we point it now, and move it once the 60 days are up, if you like.

Keeping it where it is · about 5 minutes

Point it at your website

Where your address lives, find its DNS settings. Add these nine records, one at a time. For the A and AAAA records, the Host (or Name) field stays empty, or @ if your registrar asks for something. For the last one, the CNAME, put www. Everything else keeps its normal settings.

A185.199.108.153
A185.199.109.153
A185.199.110.153
A185.199.111.153
AAAA2606:50c0:8000::153
AAAA2606:50c0:8001::153
AAAA2606:50c0:8002::153
AAAA2606:50c0:8003::153
CNAMEwww → juno-arch.github.io

If there are old A, AAAA or www records pointing somewhere else, those get replaced by these. If you already use email at this address, leave everything else as it is: the email records stay exactly as they are. When in doubt, send me a screenshot and we’ll do it together.

These records are for sites I host on GitHub Pages, which is most of them. An Advance site that takes payments or runs a shop lives on Cloudflare Pages instead, so for that one I’ll email you its own steps.

Last step: one email. Tell me “done!” and I’ll flip the switches on my side. Please don’t send me your password. The address may take a few hours to wake up across the internet; that’s normal. Then your site goes live at your very own address. 🌱

Or move it in with mine · about 5 minutes

Move it in, once it’s free to move

Then there’s nothing to point and nothing to renew. Where your address lives now:

  • Turn off its transfer lock (it might be called a domain lock or registrar lock).
  • Ask for its transfer code (also called an auth code or EPP code), and email it to me. It isn’t a password; it only lets your address move.
  • Your current company may email to ask if the move is okay. Say yes, and it’s usually settled within about 5 days.

Moving it in costs you nothing: it counts as that year’s renewal, which your subscription covers. Still within its 60 days? We point it for now, and move it after.

If you ever cancel your subscription

It’s handed over

Your site is still yours, and so is your address. Handing it over takes about ten minutes of your time, it’s free, and nothing about your website blinks while it moves. I’ll walk you through it by email.

  • Make yourself a free Porkbun account at porkbun.com, with your everyday email and a good password. Do the quick ID check Porkbun asks new accounts for.
  • Email me your Porkbun username. I send your address from my account to yours, and Porkbun does this for free.
  • Accept it within 3 days (if it slips by, no harm; I’ll just send it again). Then add your card and leave auto-renew ON, so your address keeps renewing. Renewal is about $12 a year for a .com, paid straight to Porkbun.

Rather it lived somewhere else? I can give you its transfer code instead, and any company can take it from there. Please don’t send me your password. I don’t need it, and I won’t ask for it.
