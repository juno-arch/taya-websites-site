Color and Advance

Color or Advance: two ways to get your tools

Both plans can take bookings and send a newsletter. The difference is who builds the tools: with Color I set up trusted third-party tools in your name, and with Advance your booking, shop, gift certificates, waivers and portal are hand-built by me, on my own server.

Color

Color: tools you may already know

Built with tools you may already know.

  • Booking. Your Book button opens the booking app you already use, or a free Cal.com I set up for you, in your name.
  • Newsletter. A sign-up on your site, sending through Buttondown in your name. If your list needs Buttondown’s paid plan, that bill is yours.
  • A small shop and gift cards. A shop of up to 5 products with buy buttons, through your own Stripe account (Stripe’s normal card fee applies), and gift cards through Gift Up in your own account (their small fee applies).
  • A DoorDash button. For food and drink businesses, linking to your DoorDash page.
  • Instagram and email. Your Instagram feed fills in on your site as you post, and email comes from your domain address, covered.
  • Your accounts. Every account is in your name. The ones I set up for you are made with your email, and I hand you the logins at launch.

What Color costs

Cal.com is free for one person. Your subscription covers your web address and your email from your domain address. It doesn’t pay for the paid plan of a tool I set up (like Buttondown for your newsletter), for paid plans of apps you already use, or for card fees.

Advance

Advance: hand-built by me

Your booking, shop and more, built by me on my server.

  • Booking. Clients pick a time on your site, with a deposit if you like, plus confirmation and reminder emails. Busy times on your own calendar stay blocked. Made for one-on-one appointments; group classes stay in your class app.
  • A small shop, up to 20 items. Sold-out marking, discount codes, free shipping over an amount you pick, packing slips, a “shipped” email with tracking.
  • Gift certificates. Bought on your site and sent by email, right away or on the day they choose, with no expiry and no fees.
  • Waivers. Clients sign on your site and you both get the signed copy.
  • Your own portal. A private place with its own sign-in, with only the parts your business uses.

What Advance costs

All of it is covered by your $90 a month subscription: booking, the shop, gift certificates, waivers and your portal. There’s no separate app to sign up for or pay each month. Card payments go through your own Stripe at Stripe’s usual rate (about 2.9% + 30¢ a payment), straight to you. I don’t take a cut. If you ever cancel, I help you move these into accounts of your own.

What apps like these usually cost on their own

For comparison

  • Booking apps for one person: about $20 to $35 a month (Fresha, Acuity, Vagaro, Booksy). A few are free for one person, like Square’s free plan and Cal.com.
  • Shop builders: about $15 to $39 a month (Big Cartel, Shopify, Squarespace, Wix), and some add 2% on each sale.
  • Waiver apps: about $19 to $22 a month (WaiverForever, Smartwaiver).
  • Gift card tools: often no monthly bill, but about 2.5% to 3.5% of every card sold, on top of the card fee (Square, Gift Up).

From each company’s own pricing page, October 2026, US prices. Prices change, so it’s worth checking theirs too.
