Welcome aboard.

Here's the whole journey from here to your site being live: what I'm doing, the few things I'll need from you, and what lands in your hands at the end. No surprises, no homework you didn't sign up for.

The path

What happens now

I build your draft

Within about a week you get a link to a real, working version of your site, not a sketch. It'll already sound like you, because it's grown from our conversation.

You react, I shape

You tell me what's off: a list, a voice memo, comments right on the page, however it comes out of you. We go back and forth until it feels like home.

The dress rehearsal

If your site takes bookings or payments, you and I test everything with pretend clients first. You'll book a fake appointment from your own phone and watch it land in your booking book. Nothing goes public until you've driven it yourself.

We go live

Your site opens at your own web address. I watch it closely the first couple of weeks, then we settle into whichever care plan you chose.

Your part

The few things I'll need from you

  • Your words, when you have them Changes, corrections, the story in your voice, any format, any time. There's no wrong way to send them.
  • Photos of you and your work Yours or a friend's. Good window light and a steady hand go further than you'd think, and I'll help you choose.
  • Your web address, bought under your own name About $12 a year. Follow the guide with a cup of tea, or we simply do it together in fifteen minutes, your card in your own hand. It matters that it's registered to you, not to me.
  • If you take payments: your own payment account Ten minutes, together. Your clients' money must flow straight to you. It never touches me.
  • If you take bookings: your session types, prices, hours, and how close to a session someone may cancel Four small decisions. I set up everything else.
The handover

What lands in your hands

  • Your live site, at your address
  • A login to your own booking book (if you have booking) Change your hours, block off a vacation, see your week, all from your phone, no website edits ever.
  • Your site's ingredients, on one page Your exact colors, fonts, and wordmark, so flyers, cards, and posts can always match your site.
  • Every account that should be yours, in your name Domain and payments, with the passwords in your hands.
  • The standing promise: if we ever part ways, everything comes with you Every file, handed over whole. That's the deal, in writing, forever.
And breathe

What you never have to do

  • Learn code, hosting, or any tech vocabulary
  • Create accounts on platforms you don't care about Only two accounts are truly yours to hold: your web address and (if you take money) your payment account. Everything else lives under my roof, included in your plan.
  • Keep the machinery running. That's my whole job
  • Wonder what's happening. You can always just ask me, in person-words
