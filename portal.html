                          Your services

                            Questions on the booking form

                            Anything you’d like to know before you meet. The answers come to you in the booking email and show here, and go nowhere else.

                              Busy times from your calendar

                              Connect the calendar you already keep, and the times you’re busy there close for booking here. Only the busy times are kept: no titles, places or notes.

                                Where to find that address

                                Google Calendar (on a computer): Settings, then your calendar’s name on the left, then Integrate calendar. Copy the “Secret address in iCal format”.

                                Outlook: Settings, Calendar, Shared calendars, Publish a calendar. Pick your calendar and “Can view when I’m busy”, then copy the ICS link. That one shares only busy times.

                                iPhone or Mac (iCloud): in Calendar, share the calendar and turn on Public Calendar, then copy the link. Anyone with that link could see the calendar, so a calendar kept just for these busy times is a good idea.

                                Keep the address to yourself, like a password. If you ever share it by mistake, make a new one in your calendar and paste it here.

                                Your bookings in your calendar

                                Each booking email to you has a calendar file. Open it to add the booking to your calendar; the emails when it moves or is canceled carry a file that moves or removes it.

                                Or copy your private calendar link and add it as a subscribed calendar. It holds every booking, with the address for home visits. Calendars look for changes on their own schedule: an iPhone every so often (on a Mac you can pick every 5 minutes), Google every several hours, sometimes a day. The emails are the quick way. Keep the link to yourself.
