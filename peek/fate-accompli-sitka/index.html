    Ken, Rachel & Olivia Every build

    Sitkans for thirty years, following their bliss

    Ken and Rachel Fate have studied and played with art throughout their lives. Rachel works with paint on canvas, concrete and wood, as well as digital formats. Ken creates mosaics, graphic design and audio art. They each moved to Sitka some thirty years ago, and they try to walk the coast or hike in the woods every day, with their muse Olivia.

    Olivia works as a greeter in the gallery. She’s a rescued village dog, a “Bethel Special” from up north along the Bering Sea Coast, and she’s the star of a whole series of Rachel’s paintings.
