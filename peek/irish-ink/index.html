    Aftercare Tended + In Bloom· from $600

    Heal it right

    Wash, dry, Whipp

    Always have clean hands before you touch your new tattoo. Wash it with warm to hot water and an antibacterial soap 3 times a day, using moderate pressure, and rinse all the soap off. Let it air dry for about 5 minutes, then apply Tattoo Whipp until the white turns clear. Use it for about 5 days, and as often as you need. You can keep using it after it heals to keep your tattoo fresh and bright.

    Please don’t

    • Use A&D, Vaseline or pure petroleum jelly. Petroleum and mineral oil clog pores and slow healing.
    • Touch it with dirty hands.
    • Scratch it. If it itches, slap it to relieve the itch.
    • Pick or pull at the flakes. Flaking is normal.
    • Soak it in tubs, pools, hot tubs or the river until it’s fully healed.

    Good to know

    • Stay out of direct sun, and always use sunscreen once it heals, so it doesn’t fade.
    • Wear loose clothing.
    • A dry ice pack helps with swelling.
    • It will seep a little fluid, blood or ink for the first few days, so wear old clothes you don’t mind staining.
    • Healing time depends on you and where the tattoo is. Any worries, call Casey or come see him.
