Thursdays 6 to 7:15 pm · with Hanna

The building blocks that get you into the air: beginning climbs, knot work, strengthening, beginning inversions and fun sequencing. Grab a friend.

You need: nothing yet. We all start at the beginning.

Tuesdays 6:45 to 8 pm · with Sam Veres

Build off your foundations into mini fabric sequences: footlocks, knot sequences, inversions, from the ground and in the air.

You need: standard climb, footlock from the ground, inversion from the ground.

Wednesdays 5:30 to 7 pm · with Sam Veres

Learning and sequencing skills in the air: drops, footlock sequences, hip key skills and transitions.

Rope Strong

Wednesdays 5:45 to 7 pm · with Maureen Monroe

Drills, climbs, beats and conditioning. Dynamic movement in the air, how to do beats and how to use them. Better suited to intermediate and advanced aerialists.

You need: Intro to Silks done, comfortable inverting in the air, a 30 second hollow body hang.

$120 / $100 members4 weeks, or $33 / $27 a class

Coaches who’ve toured the world

Levity Circus Collective was started in 2018 by four friends who wanted a place to build community, train circus arts somewhere safe and inspiring, and make art together. Barbra Kenyon now cheers the collective on from Southern California, and the studio keeps growing on Hersey Street.

The coaches bring a wealth of knowledge, a lot of different perspectives and an infectious enthusiasm for helping people take flight.

Ty Vennewitz

Founder and coach

National Circus School in Montreal. Toured China, Canada and the US. Juggling, gymnastics and Juggle Jam.

Maureen Monroe

Founder and coach

Flying since 2012, after 14 years of rock climbing. Toured with the New Old Time Chautauqua. Rope Strong.

Anna Thomas-Henry

Aerial and handstand coach

2015 U.S. Aerial Champion on silks. Former Principal Aerial Coach at SANCA Seattle. Handstands.

Rosie Rogmans

Aerial rope coach

École Nationale de Cirque graduate who toured with Cirque du Soleil and Circus Monti. Rope.

Sam Veres

Aerial coach

Shalako Lee

Over a decade of aerial performance, bold shapes and seamless transitions. Sling.

Hanna Moore

Questions before the first climb

Still wondering? Email levitycircuscollective@gmail.com anytime.

I’ve never done circus. Where do I start?

Intro to Silks, our six week series for brand new aerialists. We all start at the beginning and get strong as we go, and lots of people repeat it before moving up to Silks 1. Handstands and Juggle Jam are open to every level too.

Can I drop in?

Our coaches run four week series but are often open to drop ins, and Handstands and Juggle Jam welcome them every week. Drop in prices are on each class above.

What’s open studio?

Time to train on your own, on skills and drills you already know and can do safely. A host is there to help with rigging. Please don’t teach new skills to people who don’t have the vocabulary yet. You’ll need aerial experience to be on equipment.

How do memberships work?

A studio membership is $50 a month and a keyholder membership is $65, with a card on file for the monthly payment. Cancel anytime, one week before your payment is due.

Do you teach private lessons?

Yes, every coach teaches private and semi-private lessons, for focused coaching or when group times don’t fit your schedule. your private rate
