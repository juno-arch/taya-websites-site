Before you come in

The things people ask, answered once, so the phone can ring a little less.

Do you take reservations?

No reservations. On a busy show night, come a little early for a table.

Are you open for lunch?

We open at 3, Wednesday to Saturday, and stay open until 10.

Do the shows cost anything?

No, every show is free. Most start at 6 pm.

Is there outdoor seating?

Yes, there’s a patio, with heaters for when the fog rolls in.
