      Before you come

      Do you have rope climbing or autobelays?

      Far North is a bouldering-only gym, meaning that all climbing is done without ropes or harnesses. Our routes reach anywhere from 7 to 16 feet tall and we have thick padding throughout the gym for safe landing.

      Do I need any special equipment?

      The only things you’ll need are climbing shoes and a chalk bag. Climbing barefoot or in non-climbing shoes is not allowed on the walls, for sanitary reasons.

      Do you have rental shoes and chalk?

      Rental shoes are $5, and chalk bag rentals are free with purchase of a day pass.

      How often do you reset your routes?

      The routesetting team resets a different wall every 2 to 3 weeks, and the Slab of the Week every Friday.

      Do you have weights, a Kilter Board, a Tension Board or a treadwall?

      Yes to all four. Children are not allowed in the back weight area, on the Kilter Board or on the Tension Board.

      Are children allowed in the gym?

      Children 12 and under are only allowed in the main climbing area. Rock climbing is inherently dangerous, so parent or adult supervision is required.

      Do you host birthday parties?

      No. The gym does not provide instruction or supervision for groups of children outside of the Spider Monkeys After School Program. Parents, guardians and chaperones must accompany kids 12 and under and are responsible for enforcing the gym rules.

      Can I pause or cancel my membership?

      Come in any day, 9 AM to 9 PM, or call the gym at (707) 826-9558 to make any membership changes with a staff member.

      Do you offer lessons or coaching?

      Yes, both individual and group lessons can be scheduled with Coach Eli. Eli’s contact, as you’d like it shown
