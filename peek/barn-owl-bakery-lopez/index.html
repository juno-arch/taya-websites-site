        From the oven Every build

        Wood fired, wild sourdough

        Every bread and pastry is leavened with our own wild sourdough culture, with no commercial yeast or chemical leaveners, and baked with island wood in our Bassaninna Tubix 420, a steampunk oven from Italy.

        • Midnight Rye

          100% stoneground whole rye flour. A dense and rich bread we’ve been perfecting for 8 years.

        • Mountain Bread

          A dense danish style rye with sunflower, sesame, pumpkin, millet and poppy seeds, and plump raisins.

        • Sourdough Gluten-Free Bread

          Our original gluten free loaf: a simple set of ingredients, no gums, no commercial yeast, no soy.

        • Pastries and flatbread

          Fresh pastries and Sicilian flatbread in the morning at the farmstand, Thursday and Friday.

        • Heritage grain cookies

          Double Chocolate Pecan Rye, made with whole sprouted rye grown and milled right here on Lopez.

        • Sourdough Butter Crackers

          Naturally fermented spelt crackers with rosemary and dulse, topped with salt from the Salish Sea.

        • Rosemary Sea Salt Shortbread

          Buttery shortbread made with sprouted Chiddam Blanc de Mars wheat grown on Lopez.

        • Trail Cookie

          Packed with protein and sprouted Lopez grown heritage grain, sweetened with maple syrup.

            Our story Every build

            Grown here, baked here

            Cover crop, plow, disc, seed, roll, harrow, rogue, reap, bind, thresh, clean, store, mill, sift, mix, shape, bake, eat. For eight years we’ve been baking with grain grown right here on Lopez Island.

            • Heritage grainsDiverse heritage and landrace varieties are at the heart of what makes it flavorful and nutritious.
            • Organic ingredientsFrom the butter to the sesame seeds, everything is certified organic or comes from a farmer you know and trust.
            • Soil firstCarbon in the fields is the bottom line: no inputs, minimal tillage, rain watered, cover crop cycles.
            • Right livelihoodA living wage, healthcare, and a business that actively regenerates the earth. The journey continues.

            Good to know Tended + In Bloom· from $600

            Questions, answered

            When is the farmstand open?

            Thursday, Friday and Saturday, 9 to 6. It is closed Sunday through Wednesday. Hot fresh bread comes out of the oven from 3:30 on Thursday and Friday.

            How does the Lopez pre-order work?

            Pre-orders go live Monday morning. Your order is bagged and waiting at the farmstand: pick it up Friday from 9, or Saturday until 6.

            Do you ship?

            Yes, through the mail order shop, to the mainland. Orders can take a while depending on season and schedule, so get in touch if you’re worried about timing.

            Is anything gluten free?

            The Sourdough Gluten-Free Bread and the Lemon Oat Shortbread use gluten free ingredients. They are made in the bakery alongside, and on shared equipment with, wheat.

            My map points to the wrong house

            Maps can send you to the neighbor’s. Keep going down the road one more address and you’ll see the bakery at 108 Grayling Ln. Payment at the farmstand is on the honor system, based on what you picked.

            I’m a farmer or a seed grower

            Get in touch about foundation seed for any of the varieties. We believe strongly in sharing, cooperation and food system diversity.

            Can I show work, hold a class or a meeting at the Gallery?

            Please get in touch about exhibiting work, classes, meetings or other ideas. We want the building to serve the community.

            Every answer comes from your own pages, and one Yelp review mentioned the map pointing next door (and the honor system). If that’s no longer true, tell me and I’ll take it out.
