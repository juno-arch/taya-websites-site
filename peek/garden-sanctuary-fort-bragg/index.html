Meet your host Every build

John, host and gardener

John left a big-city career in architectural design behind in the mid-1980s and moved to a mountaintop to run a retreat center. In 1998 he moved to Fort Bragg, with the idea of creating something on a small scale where guests could feel the healing power of nature in an intimate, personal way.

He brings his talents as an architect, a landscaper, a lover of Spirit and an innkeeper to the garden, and plays what he calls an unobtrusive host. Guests find him easy to reach, and he’s glad to point you to Glass Beach, the Skunk Train, the coastal trails and the Victorian village of Mendocino, 20 minutes up Highway One.

Before you arrive

When are check in and check out?

Check in is after 3 pm and check out is before 11 am.

What is included with the studio?

All of the eight acres of gardens, meadows and forest, the wet and dry cedar sauna, free weights, an outdoor shower, the hammock, a fire table, free Wi-Fi and a complimentary breakfast of bagels, fruit, coffee and teas. Nothing is shared: only one guest booking stays on the property at a time.

How do I book a massage?

Massage is offered in a separate massage studio on the property, by prior appointment, with a talented local therapist, Paul, for a separate fee paid to him. Once your stay is confirmed, message John for the contact details.

Is there a hot tub?

No. The hot tub has been permanently removed from service. The cedar sauna is there for you, wet or dry.

What happens in a power outage?

John has a generator that provides the essentials, except the sauna and the garden fountains and waterfalls.

Can I hold a wedding or event here?

The gardens have hosted many weddings, but John no longer hosts weddings or events.

Do pets live on the property?

Yes, pets live on the property, so it’s worth mentioning if anyone in your group has allergies.
