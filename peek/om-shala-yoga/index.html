First time in the space? Every build

We like to keep things simple

  1. Come about 5 minutes earlySo you can put your things away and use the bathroom.
  2. Shoes off at the doorYour teacher will greet you, show you how to sign in, and help you find a place for your mat.
  3. Tell your teacherMention any injuries or special concerns before class.
  4. Grab some propsYou’re welcome to set props by your spot, so they’re easy to reach.

Questions Mother + Crone · from $600

Do I need to sign up ahead?

Drop ins are welcome! Just come. To secure a spot, we encourage you to sign up online, especially for aerial yoga and sound baths.

How do I pay?

Sign up online, bring cash, or pay by Venmo. Change for cash may not be available in the studio.

What if I need to cancel?

Cancel up to 24 hours before a class or special event for a refund. Cancellations within 24 hours aren’t refundable, and there are no refunds for no shows. Workshops and class series can be canceled up to 7 days ahead for an account credit.

Guidelines for using the space
  • Please remove your shoes at the door.
  • Enter and leave the practice room quietly.
  • Only water in the practice room, please.
  • Please skip perfumes and essential oils before class; many people are sensitive to them.
  • It’s a small space, so please come fresh and mindful of scent.
Is there a scholarship?

If you’re facing a financial barrier, email the studio at your studio email to talk about scholarship options.
