Good to know Every build

Our policies

Tuition

Tuition is due at your child’s first class of each month. It’s a 4-week flat rate. If the gym is closed and your child will have fewer than 4 classes, we adjust that month. Extra 5th days in a month are our gift to you: use one as a make-up or just a free class.

Pay ahead for 3 months and save 15%. Brothers and sisters after the first child also save 15%. Discounts don’t combine.

Make-up classes

Make-ups are offered in the same month as the missed class, up to 2 a month, as space allows. Missed classes aren’t taken off your bill or moved to the next month, so be sure to schedule your make-ups. A no show, no call class can’t be made up.

Arriving and what to wear

Please arrive 5 minutes before class. Warm up and stretching are a must, and a child who misses the first half of stretches may not join class that day. Hair tied up, please.

  • No jeans, or pants and shorts with snaps, buttons or buckles
  • No jewelry, gum or food
  • No shoes, socks or tights
Watching class

Parents are welcome to watch from the observation area only. Please don’t enter the gymnastics area. For everyone’s safety, keep noise and coaching from the seats to a minimum, and no flash photos.
