Since 1995

Excellent espresso, cool jazz

Dave, Mary and Peter Gealey opened Headlands with one goal: an upbeat, comfortable headquarters for delicious light fare, excellent espresso drinks and cool jazz. Thirty years later, the music still plays every weekend.

Friday & Saturday

Live music from 5 to 7:45 pm.

Sunday

Afternoon sets from noon to 2:45 pm.

Always free

All ages, no cover. A food or drink purchase keeps the music going.

Eat & drink

Fresh, local, and made here

Morning food is served all day (except the waffles), with bagels from Roland’s, sourdough from Fort Bragg Bakery and focaccia from Costeaux. Everything to go, too: just call ahead.

Morning

  • Our world famous Belgian wafflesWith maple syrup. Add fresh fruit and vanilla yogurt.
  • Bagels from Roland’s BakeryPlain, garlic, sesame, jalapeño cheddar, cheddar, onion, everything or multigrain, with cream cheese, peanut butter, tomato, cucumber, onion, lox and capers.
  • Breakfast burritoEgg, cheese and black beans. Add salsa and sour cream.
  • Breakfast sandwichEgg, sausage and cheese.
  • Headlands’ nut & seed granolaMade for us by GoodLife Café. Gluten free, vegan and organic.
  • Fresh fruit saladIn a cup or a bowl, with yogurt or granola.

Panini & more

  • From Costeaux French BakeryAlmond tart (frangipane), lemon tart, princess torte, chocolate torte, brownies and chocolate macaroons, and other rotating treats.

  • Espresso drinksMr. Espresso’s oakwood roasted Neapolitan espresso, and fine coffees from Big River Coffee Company. Any coffee drink decaf or over ice.

Our story

Thirty years on Laurel Street

  • 1995

    Dave, Mary and Peter Gealey open Headlands. Peter’s drawings still grace the menu and the walls.

  • 2006

    Juan and Lenore Naal buy the coffeehouse. Juan was the Gealeys’ very first employee, working his way up from dishwasher to night manager. Lenore worked summers here while she became a 4th grade teacher.

  • Now

    Heather and Morgan met working here and married soon after. Heather started in 2003, back when she was just a kid hanging out in front. Long before anyone thought of selling, customers kept saying they’d be the perfect couple to take it over one day. They did.

“The heart and soul of Fort Bragg.”Toby Lurie, poet, painter, musician and beloved customer
