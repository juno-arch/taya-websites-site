Before you arrive

How far ahead should I book a massage or the chef dinner?

Massage is scheduled at least 72 hours before check-in, and the private chef dinner at least a week ahead. Both depend on therapist and chef availability, so if your dates are closer, call and ask.

Can I ask for a male or female therapist?

Yes. Add your preference in the guest notes when you book. Therapist assignments depend on availability.

What is breakfast like?

A beverage bar with fair trade coffee, tea from around the globe and fresh water from the retreat’s artesian well comes with your stay. A premium hot breakfast, or breakfast in bed for two, is an upgrade. Order it 48 hours ahead.

Are pets welcome?

Every room is pet friendly, at $35 per pet per night. Four small dogs live here too, so you will be greeted.

Can we add another guest to a room?

An extra bed is $50 per adult or $25 per child 12 and under. The Syringa Suite sleeps up to five, and the whole lower level around the Nooma Bedroom sleeps up to eight adults.

Where are you?

291 Syringa Heights Road in Sandpoint, Idaho, about a mile from downtown.
