    Meet Cayla

    Hello, my name is Cayla McCutcheon, and I am the creator and owner of Woven in the Waves, based in Sitka, Alaska.

    I am Lingít and a proud member of the Dakl’aweidí tribe of Angoon. A lifelong Sitkan.Cayla, on her website (Cayla, please check this wording and the spelling)

    At Woven in the Waves, I blend the art of metal and fiber to create intricate micro macrame jewelry. Every piece I make involves hand-texturing and shaping all wire components, and I handle 100% of the silversmithing myself.

    My journey into macrame began in 2016 with wall hangings, but it wasn’t long before I was drawn to creating smaller, more delicate art. This eventually evolved into jewelry. A year later, I transitioned to working full-time as a jewelry artist, a path I’ve followed ever since.

    Rooted in generations of artists before me, I founded Woven in the Waves, a jewelry business crafted by local indigenous hands and inspired by the sea.Cayla, on her Airbnb Experience (Cayla, please check these words)

    Good to know Tended + In Bloom· from $600

    Before you order

    Straight from your shop’s own shipping and returns page, your listings and your Class Info page.

    How long until my earrings ship?

    Made to order styles could take up to a week to process. The Alaskan Wildflower Hoops have a production time of 2 to 4 weeks, so each pair is uniquely yours.

    Can I ask for a custom color or design?

    Cayla is not taking custom requests. You do choose your own thread color from 37 colors, your metal, and on some styles your beads.

    What are the ear wires made of?

    Antique copper hoops come with niobium ear wires, which Cayla describes as a 100% pure metal, and the sterling silver pair has 935 sterling silver ear wires. The Dipper hoops have 14k gold-filled ear wires.

    Can I return my order?

    Cayla does not take returns, but is open to exchanges within 30 days of purchase with a receipt. You pay the shipping charges for an exchange, and it can take up to 3 weeks to process. Sale items are always final sale.

    Who can come to a workshop?

    Public workshops are limited to 6 people, 18 years or older. Private workshops are the same, for groups of friends and family, with no age requirement, and still limited to 6.
