The work Every build

Trash, cast in porcelain

Turning everyday materials + environmental concerns into art.Heather, on Instagram

Heather’s work responds to environmental degradation, rampant consumerism, and the waste of single-use plastic packaging. Working mostly in clay, she casts discarded plastic into enduring ceramic forms, using plaster mold-making and slip casting. The pieces are part artifact and part warning, and an invitation to think about what we truly value.

Commissions are welcome: ceramic installations for homes, hotels and commercial spaces.

      About Every build

      Heather Law, ceramic sculptor and teacher

      Heather grew up in rural Mendocino County, and the natural world still shapes her work. She earned a B.A. in Art at Cal State Chico in 2004, then apprenticed at Hoyman-Browe Pottery Studio, and in 2009 she received an M.F.A. in Ceramic Sculpture from the School for American Crafts at Rochester Institute of Technology.

      She has held national and international artist residencies, including recent work in Jingdezhen, China, the historic city of porcelain. Plaster mold-making and slip casting are part of her practice, and she teaches them in classrooms and in the community. Today she is an adjunct professor at Mendocino College and a studio artist in Ukiah.

      Before your party

      The answers come from your offerings page.

      How far ahead should I book?
      Please schedule a private party at least two weeks before the day you want.
      How many people do I need?
      A private event needs a minimum of eight participants to run.
      What is included?
      Clay, tools, glazes, firing and all materials are included, and aprons too. At the end, each guest chooses a glaze color for the finished piece.
      When do we get our pieces?
      Finished pieces are ready for pickup in about 3 weeks.
      What if I can’t make it?
      You can take a 50% refund, or full credit toward a new reservation. You are also welcome to send someone in your place. Just let Heather know before the class so the roster is right.
      Can we bring food and drinks?
      Yes, bring beverages and snacks to enhance the experience. The studio is spirits- and cannabis-friendly.
      Where is the studio, and what should we wear?
      The studio is in a warehouse at the Rose Business Park in Ukiah. Please dress in layers, since the temperature inside can vary with the weather.
      Can the party be at our place?
      Yes. Off-site events include an additional setup and takedown fee for transport and preparation. Ask for a quote based on the location.
