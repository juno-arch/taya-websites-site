The gallery Every build

A gallery the artists built together

It began in 2013, when a committed group of artists wanted a place to inspire each other, support each other, and sell their own art right here in Sitka.

They found a downtown space with high ceilings, beautiful natural light and built-in sections for different artists, and took the leap. Every year the co-op grows: new artists join, some retire or move on to new ventures, and some have stayed since the very first season.

Good to know Tended· $600

Questions visitors ask

Are you open in winter?

The gallery is open year-round. Like the hours of daylight in Sitka, the schedule shortens during the winter months. (this winter’s hours)

Can I ask about a piece before I come in?

Yes. Call (907) 747-6536 or email artsitka@gmail.com to ask what is on hand today. Your personal shoppers can send pictures and help find the piece that feels “made for you.”

Do you ship?

Orders go by US Postal Service Priority Mail or UPS, and large or fragile pieces are arranged with you first. Shipping from a small island costs more, so the gallery suggests ordering two weeks or more before you need it. Free local pickup is available in Sitka.

Do you have gift cards?

Yes. Gift cards are honored in the Sitka store and online, and cannot be redeemed for cash.

What about returns?

Online purchases can be returned or exchanged within 30 days, in perfect condition. Please let the gallery know first.

Can I reach an artist directly?

Each member can be contacted by email, and some share their own websites, listed with the members above.
