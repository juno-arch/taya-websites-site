About Haven Every build

Room to breathe, right in Old Town

After 19 years behind the chair, Lisa looked for a salon with this kind of culture and couldn’t find it. When the space at 311 E Street came up, she could see where the chairs would go, which mirrors would hang on the walls and how the plants would bring it to life. After a week of planning and a grounding trip to Zion, she signed the lease. Haven opened in August 2025, and welcomed its first stylists in January 2026.

FAQs Tended + In Bloom· from $600

Good to know before you come

How do I book an appointment?

Each stylist manages her own booking. Find her card above: most have a Book button that goes straight to their booking page that opens her booking right there on her card. Not sure who to see? Get paired and we’ll help.

Do you take walk ins?

Haven is by appointment only.

How far ahead should I book color?

It depends on the week and the stylist. Usually look about a week ahead, but once in a while there’s a cancellation and you can come in the next day.

Should I wash my hair before color?

Yes! Clean hair means a smoother application and an even color. Please arrive with your hair mostly dry, so we don’t need to adjust your timing and price.

How much notice to cancel?

Each stylist has her own policy, but a good rule of thumb is 24 hours.

Can I bring my kids?

We suggest making other arrangements if you can, so everyone gets a peaceful visit, and we understand care can be hard to find. If they come along, please keep them close and away from chemicals and scissors, and bring something quiet for them to do.

Where should I park?

Right out front is free 2 hour parking, like most of Old Town. Free 4 hour parking is on 3rd Street between E and D, and on D between 2nd and 3rd. The free gravel lot is 3 blocks away by the boardwalk on 1st Street. The lot across the street and the one on the corner are paid, through the Passport Parking app.
