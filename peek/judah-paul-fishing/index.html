The boat

The Judah Paul

The boat and the business carry the name of Captain Steve’s son, Judah Paul. After losing him suddenly, the family decided life is too precious for what-ifs. So they named the boat after him, chase their dreams, and fish every chance they get.

Size
31 feet by 10.5 feet, solid fiberglass, very stable
Power
Twin Yanmar diesels
At the helm
Dual Garmin displays, fish finder, GPS mapping, radar, autopilot, VHF radio
Comfort
Lots of room on the rail, a big cabin, a private enclosed flush toilet and fresh water sinks
Safety
All gear meets Coast Guard rules: life jackets, EPIRB, life raft and more
Gear
Daiwa Saltist reels on Penn and Okuma rods, Cannon electric downriggers, a live bait well

Good to know

I’ve never fished the ocean. Can I come?

Yes. Beginner to expert, child to adult, everyone is welcome. Captain Steve will show you how, and all the gear is on the boat.

Where do we meet, and when?

At Woodley Island Marina in Eureka, D Dock, ready to leave at 6:00 am. Trips are back by about 2 pm, or sooner if everyone limits out.

Do I need a fishing license?

Yes, everyone who fishes needs a valid California fishing license. You can buy one online from California Fish and Wildlife before your trip.

What if the weather turns?

Safety comes first. If we cancel for weather or equipment, you can reschedule your trip or get a full refund.

What’s the cancellation policy?

Cancel more than 7 or 14 days ahead for a full refund. Later than that, the trip is charged in full, unless we can fill your seat. Book early for the best days.

How many people fit on the boat?

Up to 6 anglers. Book the whole boat for a private trip: $1,500 for salmon or halibut, $1,800 for rockfish, $2,400 for tuna.

Can you clean my fish?

Yes. Filleting is $15 per rockfish limit.

Is food included?

Bring your own food and drinks. (anything you provide on board)
