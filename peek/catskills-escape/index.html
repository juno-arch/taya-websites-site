The Lodge Every build

A home on a hill, with the land to itself

The Lodge is a 1960s mid-century home, updated, on a private drive between Ellenville and Mountaindale. It sits on 105 private acres of woods and open fields, with views from every room and a door from every room to the outdoors. Town is about seven minutes away in either direction.

Families and friends come for a weekend or a week, and small groups come to retreat. Light moves through the house the way it moves through the trees.

  • Sleeps up to 8, with four bedrooms, five beds and three bathrooms
  • A wood-burning fireplace, a fire pit with unlimited firewood, hammocks on the hillside and a large covered porch
  • A full kitchen, fresh coffee and herbal tea blends on arrival, yoga mats and studio props
  • Solar-powered, with high-efficiency heating and cooling, and linens, towels and eco bath products provided
  • Check in after 4 pm with a smart lock, check out by 11 am
  • 15% off weekly stays, with savings for monthly stays

Meet your host Every build

Lianna Willoughby

Lianna founded Retreat East Co around something she believes deeply: spending time in nature is one of the simplest and most powerful ways to feel better. Life moves quickly, and time outdoors has a way of slowing us down, so we breathe more deeply and think more clearly.

She is a mother of two and a certified Jin Shin Jyutsu practitioner, and her home on the hillside is her way of sharing that belief. Guests arrive with something different each time, and there is no prescribed experience here.

Before you arrive

How do I book the Lodge?

Stays are booked on Airbnb, where the Lodge sleeps up to 8. The planner or an email to Lianna then arranges any experiences for the days of your stay.

When can I check in and out?

Check in is after 4 pm and check out is before 11 am. Check in is self-service with a smart lock.

How do the experiences work?

They are optional, and each one is arranged in advance. Lianna connects you with local practitioners, guides and chefs, and confirms the days and times with you.

Can I stay longer?

Yes. Weekly stays are 15% off, with savings for monthly stays. Write to Lianna for details.

Are children and pets welcome?

Children are welcome. Pets are case by case, so mention yours when you write.

Can I bring a group to lead a retreat?

Yes. Groups of 3 to 6 stay overnight in the house, and one-day workshops welcome up to 20 outdoors. Weekend retreats have a three-night minimum, and rates are custom, so send your program, group size and ideal dates.

Do I need a car?

A car is the easiest way to arrive and get around. Ellenville and Mountaindale are each about seven minutes away.
