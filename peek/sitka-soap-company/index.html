        Questions, answered

        Which bar is right for me?
        The Original bar, also called the Everything bar, washes you, your dishes, your boat or truck and your gear. The Coffee Sugar bar scrubs, and the Adventure Buddy bar is for dogs and cats. Try the finder above and the bear will point the way.
        What sizes does the soap come in?
        The Original bar comes in a 3.5 oz full size, or a 2 oz travel size in a reusable container.
        Does it have a scent?
        The Original bar has no added fragrance, dyes or chemicals. The candles come in many scents, listed on each tag.
        How do I order?
        Today you text (907) 738-3434 or send a message on Instagram or Facebook with what you want, and a Square invoice arrives by email. On this site you can tap a tag instead, whichever way you like to order.
        Do you deliver or ship?
        Free local delivery in Sitka. Shipping elsewhere: shipping details, please.
        When is the Ape open?
        Thursday to Saturday, 10 AM to 7 PM, from May to September. The rest of the year, text to order.
        Can my shop carry your soap?
        Yes, wholesale inquiries are welcome. Choose Wholesale on the form below.
