    Good to know Tended + In Bloom· from $600

    Before you call

    Do I need any experience to start?

    No. Swedish is our introductory class and the first step for everyone. You’ll need to be 18 or older, with a high school diploma or GED (or college transcripts), and a personal interview.

    Can I take the classes at my own pace?

    Yes. After Swedish, you can take the rest of the classes in any order, at your own pace (Intro to Neuromuscular Therapy comes after Deep Tissue and Acupressure). The full program is finished within three years.

    Do you offer financial aid?

    We don’t take part in federal or state financial aid, but all of our programs offer flexible payment options. For Swedish, you can pay in full or in three payments, two weeks apart.

    Do you have night classes?

    Not right now. Most classes run Monday to Thursday, 10 am to 2 pm. If you’d like a night program, call the school and we’ll put you on our contact list for when night classes are back.

    I studied somewhere else. Can I transfer?

    Loving Hands accepts transfer students. Call the school and we’ll talk through your hours.

    What should I wear to class?

    Clean clothes that aren’t restrictive: no denim or heavy canvas, and nothing that shows your shoulders or middle. If your hair is long, bring something to tie it back, and keep a nail kit with your class supplies.

    Where do I park?

    Free parking is right in the Sunny Brae Center lot, and the school has an ADA bathroom.
