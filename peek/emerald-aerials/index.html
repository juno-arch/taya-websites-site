What to know before you fly

The things first-timers (and their parents) tend to wonder about.

Do I need to be strong or flexible to start?

No. Everyone starts low to the ground, and the strength builds while you’re busy learning. Tawnie customizes every session to what your body can do today.

What should I wear?

Leggings or fitted pants that cover the backs of your knees, and a shirt that covers your underarms and tucks in. No zippers, buttons or jewelry (the silks snag). Bare feet or grippy socks are both fine.

How old do kids need to be?

Kids classes are for ages your age range. Younger or older? Text Tawnie and she’ll find the right fit, or a private lesson.

What happens at my first class?

You’ll sign the waiver, warm up with the group, and learn how to climb and wrap safely before anything else. Expect to leave a little tired, a little sore, and very proud of yourself.

Is it safe?

Classes are small, there’s a crash mat under every rig, and Tawnie spots every new skill. Nobody is asked to do anything they’re not ready for.
