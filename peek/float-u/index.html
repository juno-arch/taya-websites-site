About Float U

A recovery space for real people who need real rest

You don’t need to look the part, know the lingo, or fit a wellness mold.

What happens on my first float?

An attendant shows you your room and how everything works. You take about 10 minutes to shower and settle, then float for a true 60 minutes. A gentle alarm lets you know when it’s time, and you have 10 more minutes to rinse, hydrate and re-orient.

Can two people float together?

Yes. The XL float suite, the only one in Humboldt County, has room for one person or two. Add a second guest for $20. The Fire & Ice room works for two as well.

What should I bring?

Just yourself. Towels, slides, water, earplugs and hair protection are all included.

Who should check with a doctor first?

None of these services are medical treatments. If any of these apply to you, or you’re not sure, please consult a physician before you book:

  • Float: uncontrolled high blood pressure, severe kidney disease, open wounds, active infections, or conditions affected by heat or magnesium.
  • Sauna, cold plunge and Fire & Ice: pregnancy, cardiovascular conditions, and others.
  • Red light: pregnancy, photosensitivity.
  • Halo: severe or uncontrolled respiratory conditions.

Each service has its full list on its own page.

What if I need to cancel?

Please give 24 hours’ notice to cancel or reschedule. Late cancellations may be charged 50% and need to be made by phone. No-shows are charged in full, and prepaid sessions or memberships may be forfeited.

Is Float U accessible?

Yes. The space is ADA compliant, the infrared sauna is ADA accessible, and an attendant walks you through everything. If you have a question about your needs, call (707) 460-2002 before you book and they’ll talk it through with you.

Where do I park?

Parking is free. Float U is at 426 B Street in Eureka.

¿Hablan español?

Sí. English and Spanish are both spoken here.

How can I pay?

Visa, Mastercard, Discover, American Express, debit and cash. Affirm is offered when you book on Vagaro.
