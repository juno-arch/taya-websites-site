      Good to know Tended + In Bloom· from $600

      Before your first class

      Do I need to bring a mat?

      Students typically bring their own, but a few are kept on hand if you forgot yours or are visiting the area. There are blocks, bolsters, blankets and straps for everyone.

      When should I arrive?

      At least 10 minutes before class, so you can find a nice spot and get comfortable.

      Is it hot yoga?

      No. The studio is warm and cozy but not considered hot yoga. Socks or bare feet only, please, in the practice space.

      Is there water and tea?

      There’s a filtered water dispenser in the practice space, and complimentary Five Mountains tea. Bring a reusable bottle or mug.

      What if I need to cancel a class?

      Cancel at least 30 minutes before class starts to avoid a penalty, online through your Mindbody. To stop an auto-renew pass, email or call the studio up to 24 hours before the next billing cycle.

      Where do I park, and is it accessible?

      Street parking and a small back lot are available. The lot spaces facing the building are for Radiate clients; the covered spaces across the lot are for residents. Enter the back lot from Gravenstein Highway via Redwood Avenue. Every area of the studio is wheelchair accessible, with accessible parking, ramps and an elevator to the second story entrance.

      What if I can’t afford a pass right now?

      Email hello@radiate.studio to hear about discounted pass options. Memberships also come at three rates, so yoga is open to everyone: Community, Actual and Pay it Forward.
