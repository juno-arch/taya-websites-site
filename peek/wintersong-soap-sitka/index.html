Our story Every build

A kitchen outgrown by more soap than meals

WinterSong Soap Company is a small family business in Sitka, on Baranof Island, surrounded by wilderness, beaches and wildlife. It began in a small kitchen, which was quickly outgrown when more soap was being made than meals.

The soaps, salves and bath products combine the old-fashioned art of soap making with herbal wisdom, using local plants gathered with respect for nature. Everything is cut, wrapped and packaged by hand, in recycled paper, locally made wooden crates and simple packaging.

  • Alaskan soaps, inspired by the wilderness, in 4 ounce bars
  • Natural and botanical soaps in large 5 ounce bars, made with essential oils and natural mineral pigments
  • Holiday soaps, made for a short time each year as the days grow short
  • Devil’s Club salve, other salves, lotions, body butters and bath salts
  • Gift sets on custom wooden trays, and locally made gifts

FAQ Tended + In Bloom· from $600

Good to know before you order

These answers come from your own shipping policy, return policy and About page. Your return page still shows Network Solutions’ default wording, so tell me if that’s really your policy.

How much is shipping, and how fast?

Orders are processed within 1 to 3 days and shipped anywhere in the United States by USPS Priority Mail: $12 for orders up to $61, and $21 for orders over $61. For shipping outside the US, send us a message.

Can you ship my order as a gift?

Yes. Choose “Ship this order as a gift” and it goes to the other address, with a gift receipt in the box.

Is there a deal on a lot of bars?

Yes. Buy 12 bars at the regular price and choose one free bar.

How is a bar of soap made?

From vegetable oils, from scratch, poured into a wooden mold that holds 20 pounds of soap. Each batch takes 24 hours to make, is cut by hand with a piano wire, and then cures for 3 to 6 weeks before each bar is trimmed, buffed and labeled. Try it on the soap bench.

Do the soaps contain animal products?

No animal products are used in the Alaskan soaps.

What if I’m not happy with my order?

You can return your items for a refund or an exchange. Refunds for items returned within thirty days of the purchase date go back the way you paid, and after thirty days they’re a credit for your next purchase. Original shipping isn’t refunded.

When are the holiday soaps made?

For a short time each year, as the days get shorter and the dark comes earlier, in small quantities with the familiar smells of the season.

Do you sell anything besides soap?

Yes: salves, lotions, body butters, bath salts and gift sets of your own, and at the shop, gifts made by Alaskan makers, like jewelry, pottery and tea towels.
