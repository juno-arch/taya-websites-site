    The kilns Every build

    Cone 6 or cone 10? Every class says its clay

    Each studio fires one clay, and your teacher tells you which one to buy. Cone 6 and cone 10 clays and glazes are made for different firing temperatures, so they are not mixed: a clay or glaze fired at the wrong cone can melt or slump and ruin a kiln shelf. That is why each clay has its own studio and kilns. (General explanation, not from Fire Arts’ own pages.) Inside the kiln, a little witness cone bends over when the firing reaches its heat. The cones on each class time work the same way: a bent cone is a spot that’s taken.

    Cone 06

    About 1828°F

    The bisque firing, before any glaze goes on.

    Cone 6

    About 2232°F

    Studio #10 and Phoenix. Glaze fired in the electric kilns.

    Cone 10

    About 2345°F

    The main studio. Glaze fired in the gas kilns, in reduction.

    FAQs Tended + In Bloom· from $600

    Good to know before your first class

    These are your own answers. I lined them up with your September rate sheet and the Fall schedule, so the hours, prices and kiln space say the same thing everywhere.

    When can I sign up?

    Registration opens on a set day at 9 am, online, so it’s fair for everyone. For Winter, that’s Monday, Nov 16. Classes fill quickly, so the start of registration is your best chance. Want a nudge? Get an email when it opens.

    Do I need my own clay and tools?

    Classes don’t include clay or tools. Beginners don’t need to bring anything to the first class: your teacher will have clay, and will tell you which clay and tools to buy. Phoenix Ceramic & Fire Supply, right next door at 550 S. G St. #35, has both.

    Can I come in outside of class?

    Yes! Students are encouraged to practice. An 8 week class comes with up to 6 hours a week of extra studio time, the glaze room by appointment, and up to 3 cubic feet of firing a month for your first class.

    What if the class I want is full?

    Email director@fireartsarcata.com with your name, phone and the class number, and we’ll contact you if a spot opens.Tap Join the waitlist on its time. If a spot opens, you’ll get an email right away. Waitlists don’t carry over to the next session.

    How do I use a gift certificate?

    Call us before registration day, and we’ll give you a discount code to enter at checkout.Type its number into the sign-up form, and it comes off your total right there. Gift certificates work for classes, for anything in the gallery, and for our recycled clay. Certificates Fire Arts donated are good for what’s written on them.

    How do I become a member?

    For ceramics: take a class first, to get to know the studio, its glazes and its ways. Members need some clay experience. Then join the member waiting list. For fused glass, write to us.

    Can I drop in, or take a one night class?

    We don’t have drop-in studio time or one night classes. We have 8 week classes each session, a few short workshops, and long term memberships.

    What’s the refund policy?

    If we cancel a class, you get a full refund. If you withdraw, a $20 administration fee comes off every refund. At least 14 days before the first class: a full refund, less the fee. At least 7 days before: half, less the fee. Within 7 days of the first class, or once class has begun, we can’t refund, and we don’t prorate missed classes.
