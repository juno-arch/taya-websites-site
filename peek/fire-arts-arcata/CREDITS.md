# Fire Arts Center mockup: photo and graphics credits (Kiln Night rework, Oct 12 2026)

Every photo is Fire Arts Center's own, from their public Instagram (@firearts_arcata) and their Square Online site (fireartsarcata.com), taken at the largest size offered. No stock, no stand-ins, no faces. Photos with words on them (class flyers, schedule slides, price stickers) were cropped to the clean part, cleaned up, or left out. Instructor selfies from their class tiles were left out on purpose. Every graphic below the photos was drawn in code, and no graphic has text in it.
