Family owned, on Miner Street

A soda fountain with paint on its hands

Bella Art Works began as a ceramics studio and ice cream parlor in one, a family project on historic Miner Street, and it’s been a mash up of a retro soda fountain and a paint your own studio ever since. Our second home, Bella of the Valley, is up Highway 3 in Fort Jones, all art, all weekend.

If you’re ready to step back into a time when the internet and apps weren’t the focus, grab someone you love and come sit a spell.

Good questions, plain answers

What people tend to ask before they head down to Miner Street.

Do I need a reservation to paint?

No. Walk in artists are welcome anytime we’re open. Groups of 6 or more, please call ahead so we can save you a big enough spot.

When can I take my piece home?

Canvas goes home the same day. Pottery and glass need to be fired first: how many days, and how pickup works.

Is it for kids or grown ups?

Both. Some events have an age limit, and we always note it on the event.

Do you have gluten free or dairy free options?

Yes. Every sandwich can be on gluten free bread, and we try to always have a dairy free sorbet and a sugar free ice cream in the case. Heads up: sandwiches are toasted with mayo for crunch, and we’ll use butter if you ask.

How do I save a spot at an event?

Pre-register with a small deposit, since events often sell out. The rest is due the day of.

What if I need to cancel a party?

The $45 deposit is non refundable. (how rescheduling works)

Do you sell gift certificates?

Yes, for painting, parties and treats. Get one online (opens in a new tab).
