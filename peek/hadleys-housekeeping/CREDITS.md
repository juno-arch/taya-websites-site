# Hadley's Housekeeping mockup: photo and words credits

Every photo on this page is Hadley's Housekeeping's own, from their public Facebook page (facebook.com/HadleysHousekeeping), except the hero, which is one free stock photo since Oct 13 2026 (see the Hero sample photo section below; Taya's note on the mockup: "use stock photo"). Captured Oct 12 2026 at the largest size Facebook serves, exported near native size with a gentle sharpen. Labels and words on their before and after collages were cropped out; nothing with text on it is shown as a photo. In review (Oct 12) each before and after pair was lined up (the after photo gently scaled and shifted to match the before, then both cropped to the shared area) so the scrubbed patches in the signature line up; the after photos were resampled once, at about 1x.
