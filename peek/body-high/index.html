Studio rules preview

Sample lines drawn from your public first timer and booking info. On your real site, your own studio rules, policy and waiver open here as PDFs (and the waiver is still signed in FitDegree at booking).

Do I need to be strong already?

Nope, you’ll build it. Beginner classes start with more conditioning, and the strength shows up faster than you’d think.

Is pole for my body?

Yes. Anyone can pole dance: every gender, age, shape and size. Conditioning helps every body get there, one class at a time.

How often should I come?

Three classes a week is the sweet spot if you want to progress quickly, but any amount is a great workout.

Will I bruise?

Probably a little at first, plus the odd pole burn. It’s normal, and it fades as your skin adjusts and your technique gets smoother.

Do I have to dance in heels?

Not at all. You’ll be barefoot until walks, spins, climbs and inverts feel comfy. After that, heels are optional (and sold at the studio).

My hands get sweaty. Help?

Totally normal. The studio sells Dry Hands grip to keep you on the pole.

What if I need to cancel?

Cancel more than 3 hours before class and it goes back on your pack or membership (drop ins are refunded). Within 3 hours, it can’t be refunded.

Is there a deal for first timers?

Yes! Your first class is $20, good for 30 days after you buy it. Here’s how to get ready.

Do I have to book ahead?

Yes, classes are reserved in advance on FitDegree. Booking opens at least 5 weeks out and closes 2 hours before class, and a class with no sign ups may be cancelled, so book early.
