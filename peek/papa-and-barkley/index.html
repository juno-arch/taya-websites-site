    Good to know, both lines

    These are your own answers from both help centers, now in one place and saying the same thing. Right now one answer says the cannabis line is California only while another says California and New York, and one says the hemp line ships “no matter what state you live in” while the shipping page lists state limits. Here they agree.

    What’s the difference between the two lines?

    We make two lines of Releaf products. Our cannabis line has both CBD and THC, with the ratio on the package (like 3:1 or 1:3 CBD:THC), and it’s sold only at licensed dispensaries and delivery services in California and New York. Our hemp line is made from hemp, with less than 0.3% THC by weight, and ships to your door.

    Where do you ship?

    The hemp line ships within the US, but each state has its own rules about some products:

    • CBD Releaf Balm and CBD Releaf Body Oil: every state
    • The 3:1 balm, body oil and stick: every state but Colorado, Idaho, North Dakota and Virginia
    • CBD drops, softgels and Releaf Gummies: every state but California (a California law, AB 8)
    • Sleep Releaf Gummies: every state but California and Oregon

    We don’t ship outside the US. The cannabis line doesn’t ship at all: find it at a licensed shop near you.

    Can I travel with it?

    The hemp line, yes, you can take it across state lines. The cannabis line, no: it’s against federal law to take it out of the state where you bought it.

    Will it show up on a drug test?

    Our full spectrum formulas include both THC and CBD, and they may cause a positive urine drug test.

    Do I need a medical recommendation?

    In California, adults 21 and older can buy the cannabis line at any adult use shop. Some shops are medical only, so it depends on the shop you choose.

    Where are your lab results?

    Every product has a Certificate of Analysis (opens in a new tab). The batch label on your product tells you which one is yours.

    What if it’s not right for me?

    Every hemp order comes with our 30 day satisfaction guarantee. Write to us within 30 days of getting your order, and we’ll take care of the return.

    Can my dog have some?

    Our products are made for people only, so we don’t recommend them for pets. Ask your vet first.
