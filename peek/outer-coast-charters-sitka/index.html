One boat, one captain, your day on the Sound

Outer Coast Charters runs out of Sitka, on the western side of Baranof Island in Southeast Alaska, and it is owned and operated by year-round resident, adventure outfitter and fishing fanatic Captain Christopher Paul Jones.

Every adventure is a private charter for a small party, custom designed around you and carried out aboard Suzy Q, a Coast Guard approved safety boat with a heated cabin. You pick the start time, and you pick how long to stay out.

    Meet Suzy Q

    Built in New Zealand, Suzy Q is a double-hulled aluminum Stabicraft 2500 Ultracab XL safety boat. Her ride and proven seaworthiness come from an Arrow-Pontoon System, a ring of ten separate air chambers, and her positive buoyancy and heated cabin keep everyone safe and comfortable.

    Ten separate air chambers.

    • A fully fueled, equipped, Coast Guard approved vessel
    • An onboard safety briefing before the adventure
    • Snacks, soft drinks and bottled water

    • Local history, and the captain’s choice of music

    Questions guests ask

    How many people can come?

    Your charters are private, for parties of 1 to 6. Each Viator tour lists its own group size (4 or 5 guests), so for a party of six, just ask.

    What should we bring?

    Rain gear, waterproof boots or shoes, a hat and sunglasses. For fishing, bring your own rubber boots. Snacks, soft drinks and water are on board, and the fishing gear is too.

    Do we need a fishing license?

    Yes. The Alaska license for residents or non-residents is listed on Viator at $15 per person, paid on the day. (confirm the current amount)

    Is it good for kids?

    Yes, the tours are very kid friendly. Infants sit on laps.

    Where do we meet?

    At Crescent Harbor, Row 6, Slip 27, a 5 to 10 minute walk from the cruise terminal shuttle. The directions are in the Plan your day section above.

    What if we need to cancel?

    On Viator, you can cancel up to 24 hours before for a full refund. (your cancellation and weather policy for requests made here)

    Is the boat wheelchair accessible?

    No, the boat is not wheelchair accessible. Service animals are allowed.

    Will we see wildlife?

    Past guests have seen whales, sea otters, bears, bald eagles and sea lions, and Saint Lazaria is visited if seas allow. Wildlife can’t be promised, so Chris plans each day around what the Sound is doing.
