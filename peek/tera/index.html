First visit Mother + Crone· from $600

Questions, gently answered

The things first-time clients tend to wonder about, before they arrive.

What should I wear?

Whatever feels comfortable. You undress only to the level you’re comfortable with, and you stay covered by a sheet, with only the area being worked on uncovered.

What happens at my first session?

You’ll start with a short conversation about what your body is carrying today and what you’re hoping for. If you’ve filled in the intake form, bring it along. Then you’ll have a quiet moment to settle onto the table.

How do I tell Tera about pressure?

Just say so, any time: lighter, deeper, or a spot to skip. Even in a deep tissue session, the work follows your breath and your pace, and there’s no wrong thing to ask for.

Can Tera come to my home?

Yes, mobile massage is available in your service area, plus any travel fee. Home visits start with a call or text, so Tera can hear about your space first: 707-932-0037.

What can I expect afterward?

Many people feel loose, a little sleepy, or deeply calm. Drink some water, move gently, and give yourself time to settle before you head back out.
