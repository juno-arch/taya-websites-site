A deep love for all life, and the ground beneath it

Tera is a Holistic Health Practitioner and a massage therapist. She has a deep love for all life and nature and draws from that connection and grounding energy that guides her in her work.

Over the last 24 years as a mother, a farmer, and a backyard gardener, she has learned the art of nurturing. She brings that knowledge, compassion and gentle care to the table, holding space for you to relax, unwind and allow healing to happen.

This summer she opened her own practice, a quiet studio in Arcata, something she had long been manifesting. For anyone who would feel more comfortable at home, she also makes home visits by request.

    First visit

    Questions, gently answered

    The things first-time clients tend to wonder about, before they arrive.

    New client? Get the intake form (PDF)

    What should I wear?

    Whatever feels comfortable. You undress only to the level you’re comfortable with, and you stay covered by a sheet, with only the area being worked on uncovered.

    What happens at my first session?

    You’ll start with a short conversation about what your body is carrying today and what you’re hoping for. Then you’ll have a quiet moment to settle onto the table.

    How do I tell Tera about pressure?

    Just say so, any time: lighter, deeper, or a spot to skip. Even in a deep tissue session, the work follows your breath and your pace, and there’s no wrong thing to ask for.

    Can Tera come to my home?

    By request, yes. Tera makes home visits mostly for older folks, and for people who feel more comfortable in their own home. Reach out first: call or text 707-932-0037, or email . She’ll talk it through with you, so you both know what to expect.

    What can I expect afterward?

    Many people feel loose, a little sleepy, or deeply calm. Drink some water, move gently, and give yourself time to settle before you head back out.
