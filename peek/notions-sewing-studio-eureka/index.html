    It started as a wild notion

    Having spent a good part of her life sewing, Rebecca Hubbard opened Notions Sewing Studio in December 2016. It was born out of a love for teaching, a passion for fiber arts, and the challenge of being a small business owner, and she began teaching classes in 2012.

    Good to know Tended + In Bloom· from $600

    Before the first class

    Do we need to bring anything?

    No. Everything you need for the first class is provided, and I’ll tell you if anything is needed later. For advanced and clothing classes, it’s nice to bring your own fabric.

    Does my child need a sewing machine?

    No. The studio provides every machine, and I’m happy to suggest which ones are good for beginners. You’re welcome to bring your own: just tell me ahead of time, since there’s little room to store machines during the week. A machine in working order, with its manual, is a must.

    How old do students need to be?

    Most classes start at 8. Seven year olds who focus well, follow directions and are ready to sew are welcome in the very beginning camp, and needle felting starts at 6 for students who can stay focused.

    What if a camp is full?

    Email me to join the waiting list. Schedules change, and sometimes I can add seats with an assistant teacher.

    What if we need to cancel?

    Cancel up to three weeks before camp starts and you’re refunded, minus a 10% processing fee. After that, a refund (minus the fee) is possible if your spot can be filled. A student who doesn’t show up isn’t refunded or credited. Private lessons can be refunded up to a week ahead, and cancelled 48 hours ahead for a studio credit.

    How do I set up a class?

    Email, text or call, and we’ll find a time that works for both of us. Camps and after-school sessions are bought online.

    On O Street near Henderson Center in Eureka. (the address, and where to park, shared once you register)

    Giving back Every build

    Help a young sewer

    Notions is dedicated to teaching the skills of sewing and crafting, and to helping young people become self reliant and confident. Most donations go to the children’s programs: you can sponsor a child by covering the cost of a class, help pay for supplies, or give materials.

    Gladly accepted:

    • 100% cotton fabric, at least a fat quarter (18 by 21 inches), in children’s or neutral colors and patterns
    • Thread, in every color, in a general sewing weight
    • Buttons, zippers and basic notions
    • Polyfill and batting
    • Monetary donations

    The studio has very little storage, so please skip small scraps, stretchy, heavyweight or polyester fabrics, old or musty fabric, and clothing. To donate, call (707) 601-9804.
