About Bowen

A few gentle moves, then a pause

Bowen is a series of light, precise moves across muscles and soft tissue, with short “wait” times in between, so your body can take its time. It was developed in Australia by Tom Bowen in the 1950s, and it’s still new to a lot of people here in Humboldt.

It’s done through loose clothing, so there’s nothing to undress for, and most people find it deeply relaxing.

Take your time

Get up slowly, have some water, and let the rest of the day wait a little longer.

Your practitioner

Hi, I’m Christine

I graduated from Loving Hands Institute of Healing Arts in 2002, trained in Swedish massage, and over the years I’ve grown an eclectic style, using many different modalities. Two decades of massage have taught me a lot about our bodies and how adaptable they are to the work we put them through.

A few years ago I felt there was more to this work than massage, and found Bowen. I finished my Bowen training in 2021, and I’ve loved learning all it can do ever since.

I truly enjoy people, and helping them come to a place of peace.Christine, in her own words

Questions, gently answered

The things people tend to wonder about before their first session.

What is a Bowen session like?

You lie down, fully clothed, and Christine makes a few light rolling moves across your muscles, then pauses for a minute or two before the next few. It’s quiet and slow. Many people drift off.

What should I wear?

For Bowen, loose, comfortable clothes, since it’s done through your clothing. For massage, you undress to your comfort level and stay covered by a sheet.

How many Bowen sessions should I book?

Christine recommends starting with four, once a week for four weeks. After the four, you check in together and go from there. Booking all four is $80 a session.

What if I need to cancel?

Please give 24 hours’ notice, so someone else can have the time. Cancellations within 24 hours, and missed appointments, are charged for the full session.

What if I’m running late?

Your session may be shortened so the next person can start on time, and the full session is still charged. Out of kindness to everyone, please plan to arrive a few minutes early.

Yes, for any session or any amount. Buy one right here on the site.Call or text (707) 498-9762 and Christine will set it up with you.
