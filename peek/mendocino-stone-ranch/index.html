A ceremony in the redwoods, a weekend among the stones

Perched on the Gualala ridge between Gualala and Point Arena, the ranch spans 50 acres of redwood forest with about 35 large stone installations, plus lawns, patios and viewpoints that feel made for vows, dinners and dancing.

Ceremony sites include a forest “redwood cathedral,” a small meadow, the pergola, and the Fish Rock Watchtower. Your guests can stay on the property too, in three homes, so the whole weekend unfolds at an easy, unhurried pace.

          Good to know Tended + In Bloom· from $600

          Before you come up the ridge

          How many guests can a wedding have?

          Up to 100 are included in the venue packages, and up to 160 can come at $25 a guest past 100. Elopements are for up to 15 guests, Sunday to Wednesday.

          Can we have a party at one of the rentals?

          No parties are allowed at the rentals unless the entire property is rented for an event.

          Does rain cancel a tour?

          Light rain won’t cancel a tour. If it’s actively storming on tour day, the tour is cancelled.

          Can someone with limited mobility come on a tour?

          Yes. Ask ahead, and we can arrange a ride on the Polaris Ranger, the “Stone Ranch Express,” to reach the installations.

          Can we bring a pet?

          Pets are allowed for a $75 fee for up to two, and service animals are free. Pets shouldn’t be left unattended at any time. (which homes allow pets)

          How does check-in work?

          The houses have self check-in with a keypad lock. (check-in and check-out times)

          Is there yoga at the ranch?

          Yes, on Tuesdays at 10 am in the Watchtower Yurt with Shayla Workman, $20 a person. RSVP with Shayla directly.
