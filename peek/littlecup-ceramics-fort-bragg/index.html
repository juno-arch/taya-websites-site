        Good to know Tended + In Bloom· from $600

        Before you visit

        When is the shop open?

        Tuesdays to Saturdays, 12 to 5, or always by appointment.

        What are the classes like?

        Private creative workshops at the studio, two hours each, with all materials provided and every skill level welcome. Introduction to Letterpress is for up to 4 people, Poster Printing for up to 2, and the Linocut Class is a private class.

        Can I get personalized cards?

        Yes. Personalized flat notecards come in 25s with an A6 envelope, in black, red or blue ink. Each card is hand printed, so there are slight variations in color. If you would like more cards printed, send a message.

        Do you do custom designs or wholesale?

        Yes. If you are interested in custom designs or placing a wholesale order, just send a message.

        How does shipping work?

        Shipping is free on orders over $50. (your shipping rates under $50)

        Can I give a class as a gift?

        Yes. The Littlecup Press and Vintage gift card is letterpress printed on card stock and can be used for a class or anything else in the shop.
