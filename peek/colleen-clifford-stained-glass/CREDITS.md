# Photo credits: Colleen Clifford Stained Glass mockup

Every photo on this mockup is Colleen Clifford's own, from her website colleencliffordart.com (Squarespace; gallery, classes, about and home pages), captured Oct 12 2026 at the largest size her site serves (?format=2500w), then exported as WebP near native size with a gentle UnsharpMask (1.4, 75, 2) on the large files. No stock photos. No photos with words on them were used (her College of the Redwoods and Art of the Burl class flyers and her North Coast Open Studios flyers were left out).

Her Instagram (@colleenclifford_stained_glass) and Facebook were read for facts and captions only; no images were taken from them. The Stained Glass Glimpses stills (movie and TV screenshots on her blog) are not hers and were not used: the page only lists her post titles and links to her posts.
