About Colleen Every build

A self-diagnosed puzzle person

I fell for stained glass in a high school Art Metal class in Madison, Wisconsin: the colors, the tools, the piecing together of the glass. I bought my own tools and kept making it in my parents’ basement, dorm rooms and shared houses, and spent one summer selling window hangings, boxes and kaleidoscopes on State Street.

I graduated from Humboldt State in 2002 with an Art Studio degree, with an emphasis on jewelry and fine metalsmithing, then spent 13 years making fine stained glass lamps for a local lamp company. In 2016 I started my own business in my garage turned studio, here in the dunes of Manila.

“Glass is a fragile yet sensuous material, and I enjoy making art that can play off these elements.”Colleen, in her own words

    Good to know before class

    These are your own answers, from your classes page, including the Intermediate FAQ (yes, the “Nope” stays).

    Beginning classes

    Do I need any experience?

    Not at all. All levels of students can take a Beginning class, and you learn every step of the stained glass process in a day.

    What do I need to bring?

    Just yourself. Supplies, materials and designs are all provided. No outside patterns please, unless you’ve taken my Intermediate class. I’ll email you 3 days before class with more details.

    How old do I need to be?

    Classes are for ages 16 and up.

    What if I need to cancel?

    Sorry, I can’t hold seats. There are no refunds in the week before class, unless I can fill your seat.

    None of the dates work for me. Now what?

    If you and a couple of friends can match one of my open days, I’ll save the day for you, and we’ll spread the word and fill it up. It takes 3 to grab a day, and up to 6 can come. Ask for a day.

    The Intermediate class

    Who can take it?

    If you’ve taken my Beginning class (or the equivalent) and have a solid grasp of stained glass skills, you can take my Intermediate class. If you struggled in a class before, take the Beginning class again to build your abilities.

    What are we making?

    Suncatcher ornaments, and I show you how to design your own, with durability and originality in mind, then how to turn your design into a pattern in glass. Designing your own is entirely optional: you can keep using my designs. Designs should be around 6 by 6 inches, with 5 to 15 pieces.

    When does it end?

    We start at 10 am and end when we finish. If you need to be out by 4, we can make it happen.

    Can I make something bigger?

    Unfortunately not in this type of class. People have asked about bigger, framed window hangings, but I don’t have the setup and materials for that this year. Maybe next year!

    Why is the Intermediate class fee non-refundable?

    Only a small group of students is at the level for my Intermediate classes, so there’s a smaller pool to fill a seat if someone drops out. If you need to cancel, I’ll try to fill your spot and refund you (minus Square fees) if someone fills it.

    Can my Beginning level friends come to an Intermediate class? I swear, I’ll teach them everything!

    Nope.

    Custom work and visits

    Do you take custom orders?

    Yes! Windows, doors, sidelights, panels, logos and gifts. Please be in touch with your ideas and inspirations, and we’ll set up a time for you to visit the studio.

    Can I visit the studio?

    Yes, by appointment. Contact me to set up a tour of my home studio and to talk about your vision. It’s also open to everyone during North Coast Open Studios in June.

    Do you do repairs?

    I haven’t been taking on many repairs lately, but feel free to ask.
