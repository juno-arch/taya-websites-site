Before you walk in

Pole can feel intimidating at first, so comfort comes first here. Here’s everything to know before your first class.

  1. Reserve your spotText (541) 901‑3541. There are no walk in classes, and each person books separately.
  2. Bring your IDDiamond is 18 and over. Bring a valid government issued ID to your first class, like a driver’s license, a passport or a DMV ID card.
  3. Dress to gripStretchy short shorts that show your thighs and a sports bra or fitted stretchy top. Skip lotion and oil for 24 hours, and leave rings, bracelets, necklaces and watches at home.
  4. Bring cash, water and socksSocks help with stretching and cool down. Grip is optional, and it’s sold at the studio.
  5. Come 5 to 10 minutes earlyTimes on the schedule are when class begins, and the door is locked right before class starts.

good to know Tended + In Bloom· from $600

Questions before your first spin

Still wondering? Text (541) 901‑3541 anytime.

I’m brand new to pole. Can I come?

Yes. Every class is all levels: everyone warms up and cools down together, and your tricks, spins and conditioning are picked for where you are. Pole dance is for any body type and any strength level.

Can I just walk in?

There are no walk in classes. Classes are kept small for more focused teaching, so text to reserve your spot, and each person books separately. Classes usually fill about 3 weeks ahead.

What should I wear?

To grip the pole your skin needs to show, with everything private covered. Stretchy short shorts that show all of your thigh, and a sports bra or tight stretchy top. Pole clothing is welcome too. Skip lotion or oil for 24 hours, and leave jewelry and watches at home.

What should I bring?

Water, socks for flexy and cool down time, and grip if you like (it’s sold at the studio). For Low Flow and Dancer 101, bring pole heels and knee pads.

Do I need ID?

Yes, for your first class. Diamond is 18 and over, so bring a valid government issued ID, like a driver’s license, a passport or a DMV ID card.

How do I pay?

Cash only, at the studio. A class is $25, or $200 for a bundle of 10 (just for you, not shareable). Open Pole is $15, or $150 for 10.

What if I can’t make it?

Let Lindsay know at least 24 hours ahead, and the sooner the better, so she can fill your spot. A same day cancel or a no show uses one class from your bundle, or a $25 fee without one, brought to your next class. Emergencies happen, and the fee can be waived.

Can I bring my partner?

Yes, in the Couples Class: tricks and transitions for couples of every kind, all levels. It’s the only class with shared poles.

Where do I park?

Drive down the alley at 617 N. Holly St. There’s a large gate right behind the building where you can pull in and park. The studio is upstairs.
