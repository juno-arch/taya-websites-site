    Good to know Tended + In Bloom· from $600

    Before your first class

    Do I need to book?

    Not for regular classes: walk-ins are welcome. Pay when you arrive, with cash, card, check or coupon. Space is limited, so a few minutes early is a good idea.

    Is my first class really free?

    Yes. Your first class is free.

    What should I bring?

    Just yourself. Mats, bolsters, blocks and blankets are all in the studio.

    I’m a beginner. Is that okay?

    Very much so. The studio has classes for beginners, with gentle movement and meditation, and advanced classes for people who want to deepen their practice. Teachers adapt to the person in front of them.

    Is there a sliding scale?

    Morning classes are sliding scale, $0 to $15, for elders over age 60. Wednesday night classes are sliding scale, $0 to $15, for healthcare workers.

    Can I have a private session?

    Yes. A 60 minute one to one session is $100, with intake and planning forms and a phone call included.
