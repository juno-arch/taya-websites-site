The hall

Concerts under stained glass

Ferndale’s majestic 115+ year old former Methodist church, next to the town cemetery. Ferndale Music Company opened it to the public in 2015, and the first show sold out.

Inside: stained glass memorial windows, rich redwood details, 24 foot Italian tin ceilings and a barrel wood stove for winter shows.

225seats, and professional sound
1901the year of the big remodel
24 ftItalian tin ceilings
ADAaccessible, all ages shows

Upstairs, the owls

Barn owls have nested in the bell tower since it was built in 1902. The Owl Cam’s new feed comes this fall.

As seen on PBS

Stay the night

The Green Room at The Old Steeple

Once only for musicians playing the hall, the Green Room is a private suite inside the church: a vintage kitchen and dining room with a gas fireplace, a high ceilinged parlor, a bedroom and a bath, with instruments on the walls. Up to 3 guests, a few blocks from Main Street.

Before the show

Where are my tickets?

Tickets are paperless. Your name goes on the Will Call list at the door, and you’ll get an email confirmation within 24 hours of buying online. Need help? Call (707) 786-7030 or email ferndalemusic@yahoo.com.

Can I bring my kids?

Yes. Shows at The Old Steeple are all ages.

Is the hall accessible?

Yes. The Old Steeple is ADA accessible, with accessible restrooms.

When do doors open?

Shows start at 7:30 pm. Doors open at your doors time.

Where do I park?

(your parking note here)

Where can I stay?

Right here, when there isn’t a show: the Green Room is a private suite inside the church. Ferndale’s Victorian Village has more inns a few blocks away.

How old do you have to be for lessons?

Lessons are for all ages. Vicky teaches ukulele to everyone from 4 to 94.

When are you open?

On concert dates, and by appointment. Call (707) 786-7030.
