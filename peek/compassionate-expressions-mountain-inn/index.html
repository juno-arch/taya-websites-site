Before you arrive

How do I reserve a room or a treatment?

Rooms can be booked on Airbnb or by phone at (828) 683-6633. Spa services are by appointment, so please phone or write at least two days ahead, and more if you can. It’s not a walk-in spa.

What if I need to cancel?

Lodging needs 7 days’ notice, or 14 days for holidays and October. Spa services need 72 hours. Cancellations are made by phone, by speaking with Reva or her staff directly.

Is there internet, television or cell service?

The inn rooms are a low-stimulus, low-tech space, so they have no TV or internet, and cell service doesn’t reach the grounds. A landline phone is in the room for your convenience. (please confirm whether guests find wifi in the rooms)

Can I bring children or pets?

The inn rooms don’t take children under 15, and the retreat houses ask that you check about children first. Pets, tent camping and RVs aren’t possible.

Who can use the hot tubs and the sauna?

The two outdoor hot tubs and the private infrared sauna are for lodging guests. Each one is reserved just for your party and sanitized after each use.

How far are you from Asheville?

About a 30-minute scenic drive northwest, in the quiet farm country of Leicester.
