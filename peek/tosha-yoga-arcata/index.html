    New to yoga Every build

    You are welcome here

    If you are brand new to yoga, welcome. Most of us avoid starting new things because we are afraid of looking ridiculous, but here you won’t be. And if you have done yoga in the past and know it’s time to come back to a friendlier relationship with yourself, welcome too.

    • Classes are drop-in: no pre-registration necessary.
    • Wear or bring comfortable clothes. There is space to change.
    • Aim to arrive 10 minutes early to get settled.
    • Bring a mat if you like, or rent one of theirs for $1.
    • Enjoy some tea while you wait for class to begin.
    • You can rest whenever you need to and pace the class to your own needs.

    Good to know Tended + In Bloom· from $600

    Before your first class

    The answers come from your own pages, so you don’t answer the same email twice.

    Do I need to book ahead?

    No. You are welcome to book online or just show up: there is always space for you. If you do book ahead, pay online or with the Acuity client app.

    Can I get a refund if I book ahead?

    Once you have paid for a class ahead of time, it can’t be refunded or moved to another class, so if there’s a chance you won’t make it, please just drop in. The only time a class is refunded is when the studio cancels it.

    What if my teacher is away?

    There is always a chance the teacher listed can’t teach, and a sub takes the class. Subs now go up on this page and the banner, so you can check before you come.

    How do passes work?

    A 5 class pass is good for 3 months and a 10 class pass for 6 months. Passes can’t be shared and are only good for drop-in classes. A code is emailed when you buy one: use it to book, or tell the teacher your name or code at the door.

    Where do I find the studio?

    In the tower, directly above the Arcata Playhouse, at 1251 9th Street. Look for the outside stairs: you’re up there. The studio isn’t open regular hours, and opens 10 to 15 minutes before each class.

    What do I bring?

    Comfortable clothes and a mat, or rent one of theirs for $1. There is tea while you wait, and space to change.

    Can I rent the studio or host an event?

    Yes. Tosha co-produces events with partners whose teachings fit its values, and the roughly 1100 square foot space is available for weddings, blessings, rituals and other non-yoga events. Write through the form below.
