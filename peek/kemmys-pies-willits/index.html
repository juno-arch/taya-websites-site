Where is the shop?

At 299 E Commercial St in Willits, by the Skunk Train depot. There’s street parking.

When are you open?

Monday 6 to 10 am, Tuesday to Friday 6 am to 6 pm, Saturday 10 am to 6 pm, and closed Sunday. For a holiday, check the banner at the top of this page.

Do you have savory pies?

Yes, savory pies, including a sage mushroom pie, and quiche for breakfast when it’s in.

Do you do group or event orders?

Yes. Customers have ordered assortments of pies, mini pies and scones for groups and events. Send a note below with how many and for when.
