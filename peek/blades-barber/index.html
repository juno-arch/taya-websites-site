Quick answers

The things people ask before they come in.

Do I need an appointment?

Walk-ins are welcome. Roman is appointments only, so if he’s your barber, book him ahead.

How do I book a certain barber?

Tap Book with on their card in The chairs. You’ll land on their own Booksy page: pick a time and you’re set.Tap Book with on their card and book right here, with their own days and times.

Do you cut kids’ hair?

Yes. Roman, Chris, Eric and Oscar all do kids cuts. Chris’s kids cut is for 10 and under, and there are student and senior cuts too.

Do you do perms?

Yes. Roman does a partial perm on top or a full head perm, each with a cut, and Judith does perms too.

Nails too?

Yes. Judith does acrylic full sets and fills, nail art, manicures and pedicures. Most of her prices depend on the design, so her Booksy page has the details.

What about color?

Esme does custom color, blondes and brunettes, and cuts for her regulars. She isn’t taking new clients right now.

After hours, or at my place?

Roman takes appointments after hours by message, at a different rate. Oscar does house calls: message him the address, the names and how many need cuts.

Running late, or need to cancel?

Chris, Eric, Oscar and Judith book with Booksy’s cancellation policy: a no-show or a late cancel can carry a fee. Cancel before the deadline on your confirmation and there’s no fee. (your late policy for walk-ins here)
