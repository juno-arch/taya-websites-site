Quick answers

The things people ask before they come in.

Do I need an appointment?

Walk-ins are welcome, and most days you’ll get a chair fast. A few chairs are appointments only, so if you’ve got your heart set on one barber, book ahead with them.

How do I book with a specific barber?

Tap Book with under their chair. You’ll land on their own booking page, pick a time, done.Call or text the shop and ask for them by name.

Do you cut kids’ hair?

Yes. Kids cuts and seniors cuts are both on the menu.

Do you do women’s cuts and color?

Yes. Our stylist does cuts, color, highlights, root touch-ups and perms. Book ahead for color so there’s time to do it right.

Nails too?

Yes. Our nail tech does manicures, pedicures, acrylics and fills, right down the row.
