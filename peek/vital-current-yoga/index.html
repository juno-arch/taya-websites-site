Meet your teacher Every build

Heather Carpenter

Yoga teacher, Licensed Massage Therapist and doula, with almost twenty years on the mat. Heather calls herself a student first and a teacher second, and she has, in her words, “a bit of an obsession with busting bodywork and yoga myths.” She loves the poses, and the much bigger world of yoga beyond them.

Instilling a sense of safety by making people feel heard and supported is of utmost importance to me.

Questions Tended + In Bloom· from $600

Before your first class

I’ve never done yoga. Can I come?

Yes. Every class is beginner friendly and open to all levels. Rise and Shine is a lovely place to start, and you’ll learn how to use props to fit your body.

What should I bring?

A mat, and any props you have: blocks, a bolster, a blanket, a strap. are there studio props to borrow?

Do I need to sign up ahead?

No, just drop in. $15 a class, or save with a 5 or 10 class card. Pay in class, any form of payment.

How long do class cards last?

Three months from when you buy them. They aren’t transferable or refundable.

Where do I park for a massage?

On the back side of the building. Come in through the rear door.

What if I need to cancel a massage?

With 48 hours’ notice there’s a 50% fee. With 24 hours or less, it’s the full fee. Heather appreciates you honoring it.

Can I practice from home?

Yes! Heather posts free classes on her YouTube channel.
