        FAQ Tended + In Bloom· from $600

        Good to know before you come

        What areas can be waxed?

        Almost any area with unwanted hair: legs, arms, underarms, the bikini area, back, chest, brows and upper lip, and more. The whole menu is above.

        How long does it last?

        Longer than shaving or creams. Hair usually starts growing back in 4 to 6 weeks.

        Does it hurt? How can I make it easier?

        There can be some discomfort, and it depends on you and the area. Skin Deep uses high quality Berodin wax to keep it as gentle as it can be, and offers an optional lidocaine numbing spray for $5.

        How often should I come in?

        Every 4 to 6 weeks, to match your hair’s growth cycle and keep you smooth. You can rebook after your visit, or Fresha will send you a reminder when it’s time.

        How should I get ready?

        Let the hair grow to at least a quarter inch, and no longer than three quarters, for the best result and the least discomfort. Exfoliate beforehand to clear dead skin and help prevent ingrown hairs. There are great exfoliating products in the studio.

        What about aftercare?

        For 24 hours, avoid hot baths, saunas, tanning beds and hard workouts. Then moisturize regularly to keep your skin soft. The studio carries good aftercare to take home.

        I have sensitive skin, or a health condition. Can I still wax?

        Many people with sensitive skin wax safely with a few precautions and gentle products. With some conditions, like diabetes or skin disorders, check with your doctor first. Tell Mary your concerns, and she’ll tailor your wax to keep irritation low.
