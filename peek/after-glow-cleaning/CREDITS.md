# After Glow Cleaning Service mockup: photo credits (Oct 12 2026)

Every photo on the page is their own, from their public Instagram, @_afterglow_cleaningservice_ (https://www.instagram.com/_afterglow_cleaningservice_/). No stock anywhere, so nothing is tagged "Sample photo". Taken at the largest size Instagram serves (1080 px wide), never upscaled. Images were read with Pollen's signed-in browser pane and downloaded from the public Instagram CDN. No photo with words on it is used (their flyers, Canva posts and the badge-on-photo were left out, or only the clean part cropped).
