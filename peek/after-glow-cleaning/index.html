    Questions, answered

    The little things people ask before their first clean.

    How much does a cleaning cost?

    Every home is different, so every new client starts with a free in-home consultation. We look at your space, learn about your needs and give you a customized quote. You’re welcome to ask about the price list, too.

    Do you bring your own supplies?

    Yes. We bring all of our own high-quality cleaning supplies and equipment, so you don’t have to worry about a thing.

    Is there an extra fee for pets?

    No extra fees for homes with pets. Your pets are family, and we treat them as part of the household, not an upcharge.

    My place is a real mess. Is that okay?

    Leave it a mess and leave it to us! Our cleaning is judgment-free, and we treat your home, and everything in it, with respect.

    What is the first clean like?

    If you’re starting regular cleanings, the first visit is a thorough initial deep clean. It sets the foundation for ongoing maintenance and brings your home up to our standards from day one.

    How often can you come?

    One time, weekly, bi-weekly or monthly, whatever fits your home.

    Do you use harsh chemicals?

    Safe products are available, just ask. We even clean ovens the eco-friendly way, with no harsh chemicals.

    Are you licensed and insured?

    Yes, After Glow is licensed and insured.

    Do you offer any discounts?

    Yes: 15% off for first responders and veterans.

    Do you clean rentals and Airbnbs?

    Yes. Move-out cleans before the walk-through with your landlord or property manager, rentals ready for the next tenant, and Airbnbs ready for the next guest.
