How to get started Every build

From an idea to the chair

Brainstorm

Describe your idea in full detail and where on your body it goes. Send reference photos and a photo of the spot. No pictures of other tattoos, please: Sean won’t copy another artist’s design.

Design

Your design is usually drawn before your first session. You’ll finish it together at the start of your appointment, about an hour or two, and that part is free.

Deposit

A $100 deposit holds your dates and comes off your final session. It’s non-refundable. Need to move? Give 7 days’ notice, and you can reschedule once.

Your session

Sessions usually start at noon and average about 4 hours. The shop minimum is $150, which covers up to an hour. Payment is due at the end of each session.

Come in ready, heal right

Before you come in

  • Get some rest and eat a real meal first.
  • Shave the spot, carefully.
  • Wear clothes that leave it easy to reach: a tank top for an arm, shorts for a leg.
  • Bring lunch, snacks and a water bottle. There’s a fridge, a microwave, refills, and free coffee, tea or cocoa.
  • Bring a photo ID.
  • Running late? Text the shop.
