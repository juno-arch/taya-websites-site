The gallery Every build

A house on Trinity Street, and the artists who fill it

A local artist cooperative gallery and shop, filled with original art and gifts by some of Humboldt County’s finest artists.

The artists founded it in 2012 in the Riecke House, built in 1900 and one of only five structures still standing in Trinidad from that turn-of-the-century era. What hangs and stands inside reflects the ocean, the wildlife and the redwoods outside: photography, ceramics, textiles, beadwork, woodwork, cards, prints, paintings, jewelry, blown and stained glass, sculpture and metalwork.

        Questions visitors ask

        Is the gallery open every day?

        Yes, every day. From April to December it is open 10 am to 5 pm, and from January to April 11 am to 4 pm. It is closed on Thanksgiving and Christmas.

        Who will I meet there?

        The gallery is run by its member artists, who take turns welcoming visitors, so you can often chat with someone who makes the work. Call (707) 677-3770 between opening and closing to speak to a member.

        Can I ask about a piece before I come in?

        Yes. Call or email trinidadgallery@gmail.com and mention an artist or a kind of art, and a member will tell you what is on hand.

        Can I return something?

        Certain items may be returned to the original form of payment up to 5 days after purchase, if in original packaging and condition with proof of purchase. Some artwork may not be returnable.
