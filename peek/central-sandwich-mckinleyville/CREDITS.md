# Central Sandwich photo credits
All photos are Central Sandwich's own, from their Instagram account @central_sandwich_mck (posts from 2018). Instagram would not let me download the files, so each was captured from the screen and cropped (no overlaid words, no packaged-food labels, no people).
- images/smokestack-neon.jpg: the neon Central Sandwich clock with a Smoke Stack sandwich ("What time is it? Sandwich time!" post). Cropped to remove the menu board on the left.
- images/pastrami.jpg: hot pastrami on a Dutch crunch roll ("Feeling extra hungry, try our hot pastrami" post).
- images/bacon-beast.jpg: Bacon and the Beast, the staff special (crop from that post; can and chips removed).
- images/bubba-subba.jpg: a Bubba Subba (crop from that post; chip bag removed).
Reviews: Google (Eleine, Raybies F.) and Tripadvisor (a guest), quoted as written with light punctuation fixes.
