Good to know Tended + In Bloom· from $600

Before you come to the shop

What should I wear?

Close toed shoes. Safety glasses, hearing protection (over ear, with foam plugs also available), dust masks and welding gloves are all provided.

How do I get there from my ship?

Round trip transportation from the Sitka cruise terminal is included in your class fee, by the Sitka Tours shuttle. You choose that option when you book, and a representative greets you at the dock. It’s about 15 minutes each way. You can also walk: the shop is exactly 1 mile from the complimentary bus drop off, a 20 to 30 minute stroll past the Sitka Sea Walk and the Sitka National Historical Park.

How long is the class?

About 2.5 hours. With the shuttle each way, plan on 3 hours dock to dock. Classes are scheduled around cruise ship arrival and departure times.

How old do you have to be?

12 or older. Guests 12 to 15 come with a parent or guardian, and everyone signs a release of liability waiver. There are no rate reductions for children.

What if I cancel?

A full refund if you cancel 24 hours before the class starts, and none within 24 hours. If a class is cancelled by the shop for any reason, you get a full refund, and full refunds go to guests on any ship that cancels its port call.

How many people does a class need?

Classes run with a minimum of 4 guests. Groups of 8 or more can ask Mike about a whole class discount. Because the workshop is compact, spectators can’t join without a class registration.

Can the class be wheelchair accessible?

At this time the class is not wheelchair accessible. It’s a moderately active class with power tools and torches.

Can you ship my piece home?

Yes, guests have had their pieces shipped home instead of carrying them for the rest of the trip. (how it works, and the price)

I live in Sitka. Can I take a class?

Yes. Local groups are welcome, and Mike can talk about a local rate for your group. (your note on local class days)
