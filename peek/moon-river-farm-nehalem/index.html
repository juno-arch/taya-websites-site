Join the CSA

A CSA is a relationship between community members and our small farm. Members commit to a whole season, which lets us plan the crops with confidence. Then you get as much produce as you want, whenever you want it, at any of our sales events. Only want a bag of salad one week? No problem. Visitors in town and you need to stock up? Shop on!

Meet the farmers Every build

Lily and Kayleigh

Two farmers who met on the Oregon coast in the spring of 2012, at R-Evolution Gardens, and started Moon River Farm together in 2017.

Farmer Lily

Grew up in Southern California and caught the gardening bug after college. In 2012 she started WWOOFing in New Zealand and was hooked after two months on a small biodynamic farm. She came back to Oregon looking for small, sustainable farms, fell in love with the coastal community, and has been farming on the coast since 2015.

Farmer Kayleigh

Was born in Wisconsin with farming in the family, and grew up with summer visits to her grandparents’ dairy farm. She worked on a small diversified farm in Tennessee through high school, then followed her passion for food and for how cultures connect through it. A six week WWOOFing trip to the Oregon coast in 2012 became the next big adventure.

  • Women-owned and operated
  • Certified Organic
  • Farming since 2017

How we grow Every build

Certified Organic, and proud of it

We believe in growing food in a way that supports all of the living systems around the farm. That means no synthetic pesticides, herbicides or fungicides, limited tillage, and cover cropping, composting, interplanting and integrated pest management to grow the healthiest crops we can. We feel strongly about providing our coastal community with locally grown, fresh food, and we’ll have fun doing it.

Good to know Tended + In Bloom· from $600

Questions before you join

Do you like vegetables? Do you like to cook, or want to cook more? Is a relationship with a local farm important to you? Then the CSA may be for you.

How does the CSA work?

You pay $600 and receive $650 of produce, or pay $400 and receive $430. Then you shop at any of our sales events and use your credit as you like. (2027 prices)

Can I share my membership with a friend?

Sure thing! Just know that you’ll be responsible for tracking separate balances.

Do you accept SNAP for the CSA?

Yes. SNAP members pay a reduced price, through our partnership with the Farmers Market Fund. (your current rate: half price, or 66 percent off?)

What if I don’t use up all my credit?

Your membership is good through the end of the year. No refund or credit is available after the end of December, and any balance left is added to our Neighbor Fund, to support people in our community who are food insecure.

Can I visit the farm?

As a CSA member or customer you’re welcome to come see what we’re up to. We just ask that you call or email first, so we’re sure to be here to show you around.

What are the terms of the CSA?

Joining means committing to the farm for one season, which runs June to November, with about 22 weeks to use your CSA. Pay on time, pick up your veggies each week (or let whoever picks up for you know what they need), and tell us if anything isn’t up to our standard. (your full agreement)
