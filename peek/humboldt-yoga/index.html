Welcome

Holding space for you, in a Victorian house in Old Town

The classes

26 postures, 2 breaths, the same order every time

Also known as the Classic 26 & 2, Original Hot Yoga is one of the most widely practiced styles of yoga in the world. The postures are always practiced in the same order, so students of every level can track their progress over time.

The room is heated to 105°F to safely relieve muscle resistance. By the end of class, you will have worked every muscle, tendon and joint, from head to toe.

New to hot yoga Mother + Crone· from $600

Your first class, gently answered

What should I bring?

A mat, a towel and clothes you can sweat in. Water, or a bottle to refill at our filling station, is a good idea too. Bring an extra towel if you sweat a lot. Mats and towels are here to rent, and there are products to buy.

When should I arrive?

At least 5 minutes before class. Doors open 15 minutes before, and we lock the door when class starts.

Should I eat beforehand?

Avoid eating for 2 hours before class, and remember to hydrate.

Can I shower after?

Yes. We’re open 30 minutes after every class, with showers and dressing rooms. To be kind to the next person, please keep showers to about 5 minutes.

How hot is the room?

Original Hot Yoga is 105°F, Intermediate is 103°F, and the Ghosh & Ashtanga Fusion flow is warmed to 90°F.

How do I book or cancel?

Register with WellnessLiving, then book and cancel online or in the app. Cancel at least 2 or 4 hours ahead to avoid fees.
