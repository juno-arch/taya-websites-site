# LAVA mockup: photo and art credits

Pollen's own vintage and thrift shop (working name LAVA, her idea), built Oct 12 2026 and reworked the same night (disco plus lava: a molten wax hero, molten bands, wax filling the tiles that fit). She hasn't sent her real pieces yet, so every product photo here is a stand-in, tagged "Sample" on the card and "Sample photo" on the piece page. No faces, no readable text, no brand labels in any photo.

## Drawn for this page (no outside source)

- The wax: the molten fill that rises in each fitting tile, the blobs that float off it, the wax pool art beside "On the floor tonight" and the questions, the thank-you art, the face-down Next drop tickets, the hard wax shapes in the Next drop band, the shipping band's drips and the band's wavy top: SVG and CSS made for this page. All abstract wax, no lamp shape anywhere (LAVA is the lamp brand's registered mark).
- The free-shipping meter in the bag: a plain glass tube (a rounded capsule, not a lamp) that fills with wax.
- The mirror-tile strips (a disco ball laid flat) and the one mirror ball, the footer Easter egg ball, drawn in the page's script with its facets on a real sphere so it turns.
- The sparkles in the hero, the "Just dropped" sunburst, the 70s stripe: SVG and CSS made for this page.
- The LAVA wordmark is set in Bagel Fat One (Google Fonts, SIL Open Font License) with a molten gradient fill and a stacked offset shadow. No lamp shape in the logo.
