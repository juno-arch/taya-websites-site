        Little questions

        How does shipping work? Can I pick up?

        Orders of $75 and up ship free. Under that, it’s a flat $8. I pack everything up in a day or two and email you tracking when it ships.

        In Humboldt? Choose free pickup at checkout and I’ll email you a time and a spot.

        What does “on hold” mean?

        When someone checks out, their piece is held about 30 minutes while they pay. If they pass, it comes right back to the floor.

        Your own bag isn’t held until you check out. Then it’s yours for about 30 minutes while you pay.

        Can I return something?

        Every piece is one of one, so sales are final. If I missed a flaw, email me and I’ll make it right.

        How do you measure?

        Laid flat, in inches, twice. Clothes get pit to pit, length, waist and inseam. Records get a grade and lamps get a height.

        The easy trick: measure something you already love that fits, and compare.

        How do you describe condition?

        Honestly, flaws and all. If there’s a mark, it gets its own close-up photo, and the condition line tells you which photo to look at. Vintage has lived a life, and I’d rather you know before it shows up at your door.
