How it works Every build

Simple, from the first message to the final invoice

  1. Tell us what you need

    Send us a message by email, a DM, the form here or one of our ads. Tell us about your space and the kind of cleaning you’re looking for. We’ll take it from there.

  2. Get your estimate

    From what you share, we give you an initial estimate. Want something more exact? We’ll set up a free 10 to 15 minute walkthrough to see the space in person.

    3-hour minimum for all cleanings

  3. We get to work

    Your estimate is based on the time the job should take, but every space is different. If we need more time, we’ll ask you first. No approval? No surprise charges. We simply stop once the approved time is reached.

  4. We follow up

FAQ Tended + In Bloom· from $600

You’ve got questions, we’ve got answers

Choosing a cleaning service for your home is a big decision. Here’s what people ask us most. Still wondering? Call (707) 599-5668 or email us.

How do I request a cleaning?

Fill out the quote form on this page, send us a DM, or give us a call. Tell us the type of service, your preferred date and any details, and we’ll tailor the cleaning to you.

What types of cleaning do you offer?

A wide range of home and commercial cleaning: regular house cleaning, deep cleaning, move-in and move-out cleaning, carpet and floor cleaning, window cleaning, janitorial services, maid services and more. We can customize the service to suit your needs.

How do you work out the price?

It depends on the size of your space, the type of service and any extra requests. We give you an estimate first, and a free 10 to 15 minute walkthrough if you’d like a more exact one. All cleanings have a 3-hour minimum.

What if the job needs more time?

We’ll ask you first. If you’d rather not add time, there are no surprise charges: we simply stop once the approved time is reached.

Do I need to be home?

It’s entirely up to you. Many of our clients give us access and instructions while they’re away. Our cleaners are trained to work efficiently and independently.

Do I need to provide supplies?

No. We bring all the tools, products and equipment, and our products are eco-friendly.

Is it safe for kids and pets?

Yes. Our products are chosen with your whole family in mind, pets and children included: non-toxic and gentle on your home and the environment.

One-time, or recurring?

Both. Book a one-time clean, or set up daily, weekly, every other week or monthly visits.

Regular clean or deep clean?

A regular clean keeps your home looking good day to day. A deep clean goes after the neglected and hard-to-reach spots, like baseboards, inside appliances and behind furniture.

How fast can you turn over an Airbnb?

Usually within a 24-hour window, so the space is clean and ready for the next guests.

How do I pay?

Credit and debit cards, online payment and cash. Payment is typically due once the service is complete.
