About Every build

Donuts have always been in my blood.

Late night donut runs with Mom and Dad in Las Vegas, then twenty years in tech in the San Francisco Bay Area. I kept looking for the hottest, freshest donut in every town, and I still love watching them pop out of the fryer.

A few summers running a donut camp at Burning Man were a highlight of every job I’ve had. Then my spouse and I moved to Sitka, where they grew up, and now I make donuts here.
