# Lakeport Dance Center mockup photos and art

Every photo and drawing on this page is the studio's own. No stock photos. Photos reworked Oct 5 2026 (Pollen: the old crops were "out of frame" and the curtain hero was a poor photo).

This is a kids' dance studio. Rule: no child's face close up, and no cropping kids into headless legs. So every photo is one that is naturally fine whole: a class seen from across the room, dancers from behind, adults (teachers), or an empty studio.
