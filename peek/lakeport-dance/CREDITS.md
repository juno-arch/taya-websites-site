# Lakeport Dance Center mockup photos and art

Every photo and drawing on this page is the studio's own. No stock photos. Captured Oct 4 2026.

This is a kids' dance studio, so every photo is cropped so that no child's face shows.

## From their Facebook page, facebook.com/LakeportDanceCenter (logged-out photo pages, largest files, 2048 px)
- hero-stage.webp / .jpg: the top of the June 29 2026 showcase group photo (fbid 1475600471038488): the black stage curtains and the light above them. Cropped above everyone's heads, so no people at all. Warmed slightly and softened a touch to quiet the flash dust. 2048 x 790. The .jpg is kept for link previews.
- g-stage.webp: a lower crop of the same curtains (2048 x 550).
- studio-barre.webp: their studio barres and wood floor, from the right edge of a July 21 2026 royal dance camp photo (fbid 1493909592540909). Cropped to the empty wall; 463 x 820, shown framed, not full width.
- g-slippers.webp: the floor row of the July 21 royal dance camp photo (fbid 1493909805874221): ballet slippers, jazz shoes, tulle and a balloon. Cropped below the children's shoulders.
- g-balloons.webp: the floor row of another royal dance camp photo (fbid 1493909592540909): two dancers holding pink balloons, a tiara on the floor. Cropped below the balloons, no faces.
- g-tulle.webp: knees and princess skirts on the floor (fbid 1493909622540906 page, image 752341395). Cropped below the faces.
- g-skirts.webp: purple and pink skirts on the wood floor (fbid 1493909839207551). Cropped below the faces.
- All gentle sharpen (UnsharpMask 1.4, 75, 2), no upscaling.

## Their artwork (used as masks, so it can be colored)
- dancers.png: the row of eleven dancer silhouettes from their Facebook cover banner (851 x 315), traced to a clean transparent mask at 3x. Used in the curtain call, the 36+ card, the tuition card, the newsletter card and the address card.
- ldc-logo.png: their full LDC / LAKEPORT DANCE CENTER logo, from the top of their 2025 "Dancing for Love" showcase flyer (discoverlakeportca.com), made into a transparent mask. Footer wordmark.
- ldc-mark.png: just the "LDC" letters from that logo (the dancers removed). Nav wordmark and the search-result icon.
