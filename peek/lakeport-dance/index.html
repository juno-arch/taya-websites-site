The studio Every build

A home for dance in Lake County

Lakeport Dance Center is home to Antoinette’s School of Dance, directed by Antoinette Goetz. For over 36 years, Lake County kids (and grown-ups) have learned to dance here, from their very first Movement class to Pointe, Production and the Spirit Dancers.

The regular dance year runs September to June, with camps and classes all summer. Our dancers perform in the Christmas Show and the June Showcase, and take part in dance conventions too.

Good questions, plain answers

What parents tend to ask before the first class.

How young can my child start?

At two! Movement is for ages 2 to 4, and Pre Ballet, Little Tappers, Junior Jazz and Fresh Step Hip Hop start at 4. Classes for ages 4 to 6 and Movement are 25 minutes long.

Can my child try a class first?

(your trial class policy)

What if we miss a class?

Come to a make-up class that’s comparable to your dancer’s level. The same goes for classes cancelled for holidays.

When is tuition due?

The first week of each month, by check, cash, or Venmo (@AntoinetteLDC). To dance in the June Showcase, tuition is paid through June.

Is there a sibling discount?

Yes, $10 off for each additional sibling. It doesn’t apply to Charter Home School students.

Do you take charter school funds?

Yes. We’re an approved vendor for Compass Charter School and Lake View Charter School. The registration fee is waived, with a $5 handling fee per PO.

How do the performance groups work?

Production Dancers, the Spirit Dancers, Advanced Contemporary and Advanced Hip Hop are by invitation, and need two ballet classes a week.

What does the showcase cost?

Tickets are $15 each with reserved seating, and ages 3 and under are free. Every dancer needs tan tights ($10 at the studio). Costumes: (your costume fee)
