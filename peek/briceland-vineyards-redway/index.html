The wines Every build

The wine speaks for itself

Handcrafted in small batches, from distinctive local farms. Andrew makes wine that a novice will enjoy and a true wine geek will appreciate, in about 2,000 cases a year.

Single Vineyard Pinot Noirs

Humboldt’s cool climate suits Pinot Noir. Most of the red is Pinot, grown by neighbors in tiny hillside vineyards of a few acres each, then aged up to two years in oak barrels.

Alderpoint, Phelps and Ronda’s vineyards, and a Reserve

Crisp, No-Oak Whites

Sauvignon Blanc, Chardonnay, Arneis and Gewurztraminer, fermented in stainless steel in small batches pressed from one to three tons of grapes.

The 2025 Humboldt Sauvignon Blanc is back in stock

Famous Humboldt Brut

Sparkling wine made in the traditional way, Methode Champenoise, from the same small lots.

Brut, and a pet nat poured on the patio this summer

Cool Climate Humboldt Bordeaux

Noir d’Orleans, a Bordeaux blend from Ishi Pishi Ranch, with Merlot and Cabernet Sauvignon in the lineup too.

Gold Medal, 2012 Noir d’Orleans, East Meets West

Red Fruited, Lower Oak Zinfandels

Zinfandel from Ishi Pishi Ranch in Humboldt and Dark Horse Vineyard in Mendocino County, and a Syrah from Dark Horse too.

Double Gold, 2013 Dark Horse Syrah, Mendocino County

Humboldt grown

Over 75% of the wine is from Humboldt grown grapes, most of them farmed organically in tiny hillside vineyards, some planted in the late ’70s and early ’80s. Red wines are made in 2 to 3 ton batches, fermented in small bins and hand punched.

    The winery Every build

    A family winery, forty vintages in

    Briceland Vineyards was founded in 1985 by winemaker Joe Collins and Maggie Carey. Joe is widely credited with helping establish commercial wine production from Southern Humboldt grapes, through his consulting and support of most of the early vineyards in the area.

    Maggie’s son Andrew Morris trained and apprenticed with his stepfather, and became Humboldt County’s first second generation winemaker. Today Andrew and his wife Rosie run the winery, working closely with the small growers nearby.

    • Founded 1985
    • Celebrating the 40th vintage
    • About 2,000 cases a year
    • Best in State, California State Fair
    • 94 points, 2013 Alderpoint Pinot Noir, PrinceofPinot.com
