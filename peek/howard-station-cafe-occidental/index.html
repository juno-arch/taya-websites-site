The old station Every build

A railroad station, now with waffles

Howard Station Cafe sits in a Victorian train station building in the heart of downtown Occidental, and the name comes from the train station that once ran through town. The cafe has been family owned since 1982, and three generations have made the food with love.

Jacki Wilson, whose father started the cafe, is the owner now. “I grew up working here,” she told ABC7 San Francisco.

“Our food has been made with love by three generations of family, and is just right for every generation of yours.”From your website
