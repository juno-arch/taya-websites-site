Who you’ll find here

Mary, and the clay

Lotus Studio is Mary Bancud-Bettis’s studio. In 2009 she signed her kids up for a city pottery class, and on the first day the teacher asked if she could stay and help. It was the first time she ever touched clay.

She’s been creating with clay ever since, and in 2014 she opened Lotus Studio in Old Town.

“It’s all about having fun!”

Mary photographs Humboldt too: her prints are in the shop below.

Is it okay if I’m brand new to clay?

Yes. Lessons are for all ages and all levels, and instructions are personalized to your level and your comfort zone. Mary’s first time with clay was helping at her kids’ class.
