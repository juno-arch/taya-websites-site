      About Every build

      Deeply rooted, since 2001

      We are located in Humboldt County, California, nourished by the strong, wild beauty of the redwood forests and the rhythm of the Pacific Ocean. Out of that has sprung a renowned healing arts community, and Wholistic Heartbeat is a resource center designed to simplify your access to it.

      It began in July 2001 with the Isis Institute of Integrated Healing Arts, the dream of three intuitive mothers: Maya Cooper, Carol Dirck and Joan Dixon. The Isis Scrolls newsletter came out that October, and under Maya’s creative leadership it grew into Wholistic Heartbeat. The first Healing Arts Guide followed in 2003.
