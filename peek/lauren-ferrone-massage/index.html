About Lauren Every build

Meet Lauren

Lauren is a massage therapist located in Asheville, North Carolina. She studied Exercise Science at Ithaca College and graduated from the Finger Lakes School of Massage in 2014. Her massage school experience served as a major catalyst for personal growth and reinforced her lifelong passion for natural medicine and holistic therapies.

Since then she has worked in Kauai, New York and Asheville, trained as a holistic health coach, and today she’s studying classical Chinese medicine at Jung Tao School. She particularly enjoys working on individuals who request specific attention on areas of pain or injury, and is highly knowledgeable regarding the human musculoskeletal system.
