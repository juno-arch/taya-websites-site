Questions, answered ahead

The things first-time clients tend to text about, answered before they need to.

How do I find you?

531 E Street, on the corner of 6th and E in Eureka. There is no sign: look for the 531 posted over my door, directly next to Glow Salon. There is a small lot, and you are welcome to park there if there is a space.

Is there a waiting room?

No, it is just the one quiet room. If you arrive early, text me to let me know you are here. If it is not quite your time, I may still be with my previous client, and I will come get you as soon as I am free.

Sugaring or waxing?

Sugaring is a warm paste of sugar, lemon and water that comes off in the direction the hair grows, so many people find it gentler, especially on sensitive skin. Waxing is here too. Tell me the spot and your skin, and I will say honestly which I would choose.

What should I do before a facial?

Come as you are. Skip strong exfoliants or new products for a couple of days before, and let me know about anything your skin is reacting to. We start with a short chat about what your skin needs that day.

How do I pay?

Visa, Mastercard, Discover, American Express, debit, cash or check.

What if I need to cancel or reschedule?

I ask for a full 24 hours’ notice for any service appointment. With less notice, a fee applies. (your fee here)
