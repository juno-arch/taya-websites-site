Our mission Every build

At this practice we are dedicated to one aim

Trauma can be understood as an experience that threatens one’s vitality or sense of safety. Wholeness is a state of being complete within oneself, the integration of all parts and experiences.

We recognize that transformation is a non-linear, often spiral-shaped process. Healing unfolds as we reconnect with the innate capacity for growth and balance that exists within all beings.

Our model of harm reduction honors autonomy and meets people where they are, recognizing the wisdom in the ways they’ve learned to survive.

Started in 2020 by physician Carrie Griffin as a secure, supportive space for the Humboldt community to receive comprehensive ketamine therapy, the Center for New Growth has greatly expanded our offerings over the years as like-minded practitioners committed to providing holistic healing support have joined our team.

Our offerings at a glance Every build

Four pillars of person-centered care

Our providers are deeply committed to providing truly person-centered care, ensuring that each client’s unique needs and experiences are prioritized at every step of their healing journey. We do so through these four pillars:

Medical Care

Patients of Center for New Growth can access a wide-range of medical care services including holistic sexual and reproductive care, palliative care, integrative and functional medicine and much more.

Psychotherapy Services

Our skilled and experienced practitioners at Center for New Growth offer a variety of psychotherapy healing modalities, including NARM, somatic therapy, EMDR, grief counseling, and more.

Neuromodulation

Center for New Growth offers neuromodulation services, including NeurOptimal® Dynamical Neurofeedback® and Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation (TMS), to support mood, focus, and emotional regulation.

Psychedelic-Assisted Psychotherapy

Our psychedelic-assisted therapy offerings include SPRAVATO® and ketamine-assisted therapy (KAT), used to treat conditions like depression, PTSD, chronic pain, addiction, and anxiety.
