FAQ Tended + In Bloom· from $600

Good to know before you order

Your own answers, from your site and your posts. Your site shows no prices, so the price answer says you’ll write back with one. A price guide can go here anytime you’d like one.

How far ahead should I order?

A typical lead time is 2 weeks. Need it sooner? Ask anyway, and I’ll let you know what I can do.

How do I hold my date?

A 50% deposit holds your date. The other 50% is due at pickup or delivery. You can pay the deposit right here, by card.

Can you put our logo on chocolate?

Yes! Send your logo, a photo or a design, and I’ll turn it into an edible image for your order. You can see it in chocolate first.

Can you put a person or a pet on them?

Yes, any image, text or photo, even someone’s favorite furry face.

Do the drink toppers change the taste?

No. They’re crisp, vibrant and completely flavorless, so they look great without changing the drink.

What comes in a paint your own set?

Chocolate covered Oreos, edible watercolor paints and a food safe paintbrush. Themes: Halloween, Humboldt, Thanksgiving and fall, Christmas, Chanukah, or create your own.

Do you deliver?

Pickup is in Eureka, unless we agree on something else.

How do I pay?

Venmo, Visa, Mastercard and Discover. Deposits and weekend treats can be paid right here, by card.

How much does it cost?

Every order is priced by what goes into it: how many, which treats and the design. Send me your details, and I’ll write back with your price. Weekend treats are listed with their price when they’re ready.
