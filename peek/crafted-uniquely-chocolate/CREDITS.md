# Crafted Uniquely mockup: photo credits (Oct 12 2026, hero photo updated Oct 14)

Every photo is Crafted Uniquely's own (Michelle's work), from her own site (crafteduniquelychocolate.netlify.app, taken at the size it serves, which tops out around 600px) and her public Instagram (@crafteduniquelychocolate, taken at the full size the post serves, up to 1440px). No stock, no stand-ins. Upscaled no more than 1.2x, with a gentle unsharp mask.

Left out on purpose: her "After Dark (18+)" gallery (family friendly mockup), the Winnie the Pooh treats and the Wicked pops (licensed characters), the bottom shelf of the book display (adult and gag titles), and her flyers and text slides. Her product photos carry words that are part of the product itself (edible images like "Thank you!", a client's logo, a work anniversary disc); those stay, because the printing is the work.
