Aftercare

Clean hands, always

  1. The most important rule: only touch your new tattoo with clean hands.
  2. Wash with non-scented soap, pat dry with a paper towel, and moisturize with a non-scented lotion.
  3. Got second skin (Saniderm)? Leave it on up to 3 days and take it off in the shower. Redness or itching around it? Take it off right away and heal as usual.
  4. Tender, warm and a little swollen the first few days is normal. Spreading redness, red streaks, fever or drainage can mean infection: please see a doctor.
  5. Touch-ups: your touch-up policy.

Questions, answered once

Your own answers from your FAQ page, gathered in one place so the phone rings a little less.

General

Do you take walk-ins?

Tattoos: if our artists have availability, yes! Piercings: there may be gaps between appointments where Jackie can fit in a walk-in, but your best bet is to book.

What do I need to bring?

For tattoos and piercings, a valid state ID, driver’s license or passport. For a minor’s piercing, see what to bring.

Can my friends watch?

Yes! You’re welcome to bring a friend or two. Please no more than that, to be mindful of the other clients at the shop. For a minor’s piercing, a parent or legal guardian must be in the room.

Can I get tattooed or pierced while pregnant or breastfeeding?

No. For the safety of you and your child, we don’t. An infection during healing could be passed on to your child.

What aftercare products do you recommend?

For piercings, NeilMed Piercing Aftercare ($5 or $10). For tattoos, Ohana organic vegan tattoo butter and foam cleanser. But the best aftercare products are non-scented soap and water! No product works if you skip washing as your artist directs.

Tattoos

How do I book with a specific artist?

Find them in the artist list and tap Request, or DM them directly. Call the shop at (707) 476-8282 to book an appointment.

What is your shop minimum?

The shop minimum is $100, but tattoo pricing varies between our artists. The best way to find out what yours will cost is to reach out to an artist.

Do you have flash I can look at?

Come by the shop and look through the artists’ books, check out their social media, or reach out to them directly to see their designs.

Is it customary to tip my artist?

Tips are never expected but always appreciated!

Do I need a deposit? What if I cancel?

your deposit and cancellation policy. Call the shop or your artist as early as you can.

Piercings

Can I use my own jewelry to be pierced?

We guarantee the quality of our piercing jewelry, so Jackie only pierces with jewelry that meets APP and industry standards: implant grade titanium (ASTM F-136), implant grade steel (ASTM F-138), niobium and 18k solid gold. Once your piercing is fully healed, we’re happy to help change your jewelry.

When can I change my jewelry?

Once the swelling goes down you’ll see the jewelry hanging a little long. That’s when to downsize, usually after 4 to 6 weeks. Changing to something new depends on the piercing, so check with Jackie first.

How soon can I go in the water?

Not until the piercing is fully healed, and every piercing heals on its own time.
