Aftercare

Look after it, it’s yours now

  1. Keep the wrap on for your artist’s timing, then wash gently with warm water and unscented soap.
  2. Pat dry with a clean paper towel. A thin layer of your recommended ointment, nothing thick.
  3. No soaking, no sun, no picking. Loose clothes over it for the first week.
  4. Flaking and itching are normal. If anything looks off, call the shop or message your artist.
  5. Touch-ups: your touch-up policy.

Good to know Mother + Crone· from $600

Questions, answered once

The things people ask the shop every week, so the phone rings a little less.

Do you take walk-ins?

Yes, whenever an artist has an opening. Tuesday to Saturday, noon to seven, at 527 F St in Old Town. If you want a specific artist, send them a request ahead so they can plan the time.

How do I book with a specific artist?

Find them in the roster and tap Request. Each artist handles their own books, so you’ll land on their own form or inbox.

Do I need a deposit?

Most artists take a deposit to hold your appointment, and it goes toward the final price. your deposit policy.

How old do I need to be?

For a tattoo, 18 with a valid photo ID. That’s California law. For a piercing under 18, a parent or legal guardian comes along with original documents: your list of what to bring.

Do you do piercings?

Yes. Our resident piercer does body, ear and nose piercing. your piercing hours and what to bring.

How should I get ready?

Eat a real meal, drink water, get some sleep, and wear something that makes the spot easy to reach. Skip the alcohol the night before.

What if I need to cancel or reschedule?

your cancellation policy here. Text your artist or the shop as early as you can.
