Our story

From a sidewalk to Main Street

  • It began

    Eric Ryberg started Grain Street Bakery with a passion for sourdough, selling his homemade bread right on the sidewalk in Etna.

  • 445 Main

    He moved into 445 Main Street, a building that had been a barber shop and a hair dresser’s before it smelled like bread.

  • 2022

    The bakery became part of Rockside Ranch and became the Farmhouse Bakery. We’ve kept pouring our hearts into it: handmade pastries, high quality coffee and sourdough breads.

“We love being a part of the community and a spot for folks to come in and enjoy some tasty treats with good friends.”The Farmhouse Bakery
