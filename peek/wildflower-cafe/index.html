    Questions we hear a lot

    Is everything vegetarian?

    Yes. The whole menu is meat free, and every dish can be made vegan on request. Look for the Vegan mark for dishes that come vegan as they are.

    What about gluten free?

    Lots of dishes are gluten free as they come (look for GF), and we can swap in gluten free toast, bread or a bun from Arise Bakery for $2.

    How does ordering work?

    Order at the counter, find a seat, and help yourself to water, silverware and sauces at the station. When you’re done, just bus your table.

    Can we come in as a big group?

    Of course. For parties of 6 or more, it’s one check, and an 18% gratuity is added.

    Do you deliver?

    Yes, through DoorDash, for pickup or delivery.
