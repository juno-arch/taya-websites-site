about Every build

Heya, I’m Haydyn.

My style is slow, graceful, artful, and meditative. Approaching the body like sculpture, I bring a multidimensional quality to the session so that my clients can feel truly held.

I’m a highly attentive listener, and I’m very interested in humanity, the body, collective well-being, beauty, and the constellation of elements that make up each individual’s holistic wellness. I approach deep tissue work intuitively, listening to what you describe during intake, noticing the non-verbal communication in session, and responding to where the body is asking for deeper work.

I work with all kinds of people, especially folks in the music & visual arts industries, dancers, and creatives in general. I have a strong commitment to providing accessible bodywork for black, BIPOC, and queer folks, and working artists.

your session Every build

Long, slow, fluid strokes

During an Esalen® Massage you can expect long, slow, fluid strokes that encompass vast surface areas of the body at a given moment; gentle holds, gentle rocking; and meditative pauses.

  1. Tea, and a conversationEach session starts with a cup of herbal tea and a 10 to 15 minute preliminary consultation, to get a full sense of what will feel excellent for you, plus any questions before we begin.
  2. Your massageEsalen® Massage, with added elements if you like: high-quality Pranarōm essential oils, sound & frequency instruments, burned herbs, hot towels and/or hot stones, music or silence. All or none of this, depending on your preferences.
    • Essential oils
    • Sound & frequency
    • Burned herbs
    • Hot towels
    • Hot stones
    • Music or silence
  3. A closing conversationWe finish payment together, give and take feedback, and I witness any reflections you may have.

The carrier oil I use is a personal blend of organic jojoba, apricot seed and avocado oils; it is nut-free and allergen-free.

Before your first session

What is Esalen® Massage?

Cut from the cloth of the 1960s Human Potential Movement, Esalen® Massage is a style of bodywork that takes your whole state of being into consideration, as well as the relational presence shared with the practitioner. There is a meditative quality that gears you towards internal self-exploration during your session.

How long should I plan for?

Sessions are 75, 90 or 120 minutes. Please arrive 10 to 15 minutes early for intake, and plan for up to an additional 30 minutes in the studio for the consultation and closing conversations.

How does the sliding scale work?

It’s a 75% to 100% sliding scale, based on the honor system. Please consider your socio-economic position and pay what you can, what feels right for you. If you can’t afford these rates, email me about an equity contribution.

Should I tip?

Added tips are not expected. If you’d like to add one to help someone else afford a session, let me know and I’ll find a match for your contribution.

What oils do you use?

A personal blend of organic jojoba, apricot seed and avocado oils. It is nut-free and allergen-free. Essential oils are only added if you’d like them.
