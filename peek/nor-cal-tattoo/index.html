Our most asked question Every build

How do I book a tattoo?

  1. 01Find your artist

    Tell us your design idea, and we’ll help you find the right artist for it.

  2. 02Quick consult

    We connect you with your artist for a quick consultation. It’s always free, and some designs don’t need one.

  3. 03Hold your date

    A nonrefundable deposit sets your date. It holds your spot, covers prep time, and shows you’re committed, just like we are.

  4. 04Get tattooed

    Show up, settle in, and leave with new work. Or skip the line entirely and walk in.

No-shows and last minute cancellations cost the artist time and money. Your deposit protects both you and your artist’s schedule. If you no-show, the deposit is forfeited, and another will have to be paid to rebook. Thank you for respecting our time and art.From the shop

Before you come in

Do you take walk-ins?

Yes. Walk-ins are welcome for tattoos and piercing, when an artist is free. (your best walk-in times)

Yes, the shop minimum for tattoos is $150. Flash days can have their own prices.

Does the consult cost anything?

No, the consult is always free. Some designs don’t even need one.

No. Your deposit holds your spot and covers prep time. If you no-show, the deposit is forfeited, and another will have to be paid to rebook. (does it go toward the final price?)
