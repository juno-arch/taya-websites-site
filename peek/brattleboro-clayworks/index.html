    Good to know Tended + In Bloom· from $600

    Before you come down the stairs

    Do I need to have worked with clay before?

    No. Both fall classes are open to beginners, and to people with experience too.

    Can I practice outside class time?

    Yes. Practice time in the studio comes with both classes, on most Fridays and Saturdays, 10 to 5.

    How big are the classes?

    Small. The Tuesday wheel class is limited to 6 students, and the Monday and Wednesday class is limited too.

    What does a class cost?

    Wheel throwing is $350 for 10 weeks, and More Options in Clay is $400 for 6 weeks. Clay is extra in both.

    How do I become a renter?

    Take a class first, then apply. There’s a waiting list. Renters use the studio any time except class times, and the price of clay covers firing and glazing.

    Are the pots for sale?

    Yes. The gallery sells functional pottery, sculpture and ceramic tiles made by our members and working renters. It’s open Fridays and Saturdays, 10 to 5, and by chance or appointment.

    What are “seconds”?

    Pots with small imperfections, sold at discounted prices at our Seconds Sale on the first Saturday of May.

    Where do I park, and which door?

    We’re on the bottom floor of the building at 532 Putney Road. The entrance is on the right side of the building, down the stairs, below Friends of the Sun. (your note on parking)
