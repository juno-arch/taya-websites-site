    Good to know Tended + In Bloom· from $600

    Before you come looking for us

    Where exactly are you?

    At 104 Barracks St, behind Old Harbor Books. Walk through the bookshop, or go around the back of the building to our door.

    Are you open on Sundays?

    We’re closed Sundays. Monday to Friday we open at 7, take food and drink orders until 1:30, and the dining area closes at 2. On Saturdays we open at 7, take orders until 1, and the dining area closes at 1:30. We take a break over the holidays, and post it first.

    What’s the hot lunch?

    It’s ready around 11:30, Monday to Friday, dine-in or to go: soup and house bread on Mondays, our (kinda famous) pot pie on Tuesdays, burritos on Wednesdays, baked pasta on Thursdays and pizza on Fridays. Sandwiches are ready then too.

    Can I phone ahead?

    You can call us at (907) 747-8856. (your note on phoning in a lunch or a sandwich)

    Is there something vegetarian?

    Yes: the veggie sandwich, with house hummus, provolone, lettuce, tomato, bell peppers and cucumber on our fresh baked bread. (your note on allergies and other diets)

    Do you have gift cards?

    Yes, in any amount. You choose.

    Can I hang my art on your walls?

    We feature a new local artist each month, and you can sign up at the counter for a month of your own. Or send a request with the form above.
