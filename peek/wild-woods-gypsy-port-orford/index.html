A little farm on the southern Oregon coast, open all year

Wild Woods Gypsy is Port Orford’s own year round farm stand and gift store, with over 15 local artisans and their handcrafted products on the shelves.

Come on a Saturday for eggs, raw milk pick-up, local beef and uncured salami, bread, pastries and gluten free baking, jam, jelly and salsa, beeswax candles, myrtlewood, jewelry and produce. The weekly emails come with recipes, priority ordering and farm news.
