Good to know

How do I book?

Use the Book button on this page, or text between 9 am and 6 pm, Monday to Friday, to set up an appointment.

What is sound healing?

It uses the sounds and vibrations of instruments such as singing bowls, tuning forks, a drum, a rattle, chimes, a gong and the voice, to help the body and mind relax. It is a complementary therapy and not a cure all.

Could it have side effects?

It is generally safe. For some people it may bring on a headache, dizziness, an emotional release or a little discomfort, and that is temporary.

Who should check with a doctor first?

With any health condition, it is wise to talk with your doctor before changing your routine. That goes especially for anyone who is pregnant or has a history of epilepsy, a severe mental health condition, a heart condition, an implant such as a pacemaker, DBS, stent or shunt, or a recent whiplash.

Can I do a session online?

Yes, the 30 minute Balance Energy Centers session is offered in person or online. The longer sessions are in person.
