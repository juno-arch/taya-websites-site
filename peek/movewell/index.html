04Meet your founder Every build

Tamra James-Stocum

Tamra is fascinated by all things human kinetics. She grew up in Tucson, Arizona, chasing her two brothers through the desert, playing every sport under the sun and climbing any tree she could find. She came to Arcata in 2011 on a full ride soccer scholarship, fell in love with Pilates alongside college soccer, and graduated from Cal Poly Humboldt in 2014 with a BS in Kinesiology, Exercise Science.

She started as an intern at the Pilates studio in downtown Arcata, and in 2019 she took it over and opened Movewell. When the pandemic hit, she took her teaching online, and Pilates on the Vine was born.

We must move through the world to survive, so why not make it effortless and optimal?!

10Questions Tended + In Bloom· from $600

Before your first class

I’ve never done Pilates. Where do I start?

Movewell Mat on Tuesday or Thursday at 10 is a great first class, or book a private session. For The Pilates Circuit on the machines, some Pilates experience or a private first is highly recommended. Ask about the new client special.

What should I wear and bring?

Comfy clothes you can move in. grip socks? bring your own mat, or borrow one?

How big are the classes?

Small, so your teacher can spend more time focused on you. how many people, at most?

What if I need to cancel?

Cancel at least 24 hours before class. Later cancels and no shows are charged in full.

What if I’m running late?

Let your teacher know! If you’re more than 8 minutes late without a heads up, your spot may be given to someone else.

The class is full. Now what?

Join the waitlist on Mindbody. When a spot opens, the first person on the list is added automatically, up to 2 hours before class.

Do class packs expire?

Yes, in-person packs last 3 months from the day you buy them. As a small business there are no refunds, but you can transfer a pack to someone else or exchange it for another pack of the same value.

Where do I park?

street parking on I Street? a lot nearby?
