For someone you love who can’t make it to us

Our mission is to increase access to massage for people with limited mobility, or whose needs don’t fit the traditional massage model, by bringing it to them: wherever they are from Rio Dell to Trinidad, in their home, assisted living or senior housing.

How do we start?

  1. A first visit, usually 60 minutes, to meet the therapist we select and see where the massage will be most comfortable.
  2. We ask about how they’re feeling and get to know them a little, and a few things they love.
  3. With their okay, an intro massage, starting with the hands or feet.

Good to know Tended + In Bloom· from $600

Before your massage

What do I wear?

Comfortable clothing may be worn for every massage. You decide which areas need massage, with the guidance of your therapist.

Do I need a massage table at home?

No. We can bring a table if you have a space of at least 6 by 9 feet, or you can sit or lie in your place of comfort with no table at all.

Which towns do you come to?

Rio Dell to Trinidad for massage at home. Couples at home, Fortuna to Trinidad. Workplace massage reaches from Southern Humboldt to Del Norte County.

How far ahead should I book?

Try to request 24 hours in advance. Days and times depend on the therapist and your schedule, with evening and weekend appointments available. Don’t see the time you want? Text us at 707.832.2929.

I have a referral from my provider.

Make sure you have authorized visits left, then book in the Insurance Referral section of our Jane booking page (Consociate and PRC referrals, at the studio).

What if I need to cancel?

Your time is reserved just for you, so please give us 24 hours notice. Cancelling the day before is 25% of the appointment, the same day more than 3 hours ahead is 50%, and less than 3 hours ahead is the full cost.

How can I pay?

By card, check or cash at the appointment, or we can email you a secure invoice. Gift cards and coupons are welcome too: add a note when you book.
