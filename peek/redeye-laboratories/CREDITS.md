# Redeye Laboratories mockup: image credits (Oct 12 2026)

Every painting, drawing and product photo is Shawn Griggs's own, from his public Instagram (@redeyelaboratories), his Art Storefronts site (redeyelaboratories.com) and his Shopify apparel shop (redeye-laboratories.myshopify.com). One photo of Shawn at work comes from Visit Ferndale's listing for the gallery. No stock, no stand-ins. Nothing with words laid over it (show flyers and the "sat inside the barn" reel cover, which has a caption on it, were left out).

Instagram images were captured at full size from the post's own image (the grid image Instagram serves is the full file, 1440 to 2785 px wide), shown 1:1 in the browser and captured in tiles where wider than the screen, then stitched (the overlap matched pixel for pixel). Exported near native size with a gentle UnsharpMask (1.4, 75, 2). Art Storefronts images are the 1000 px product images his site serves; Shopify images are the 2000 px Printful mockups his shop serves, saved at 640 px.
