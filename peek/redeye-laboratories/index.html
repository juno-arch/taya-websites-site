SGNº 05 Meet Shawn Every build

Painter, surfer, lifelong explorer

I grew up in Ferndale. It wasn’t until 1999 that I finally embraced the paintbrush, and found how forgiving painting was compared to the tight rigidness of drawing with pen and ink.

After running a restaurant for ten years, my wife Maggie and I moved back to my hometown. In 2007 Maggie found a space on Main Street, and after a few weeks of remodeling, 425 Main became Redeye Laboratories. In the summer of 2011 we moved three storefronts down, tripled the space, and it’s the Lab to this day: where I paint, and where the work hangs.

I work between plein air sessions along the coast and deeper studio explorations, where life experiences, dreams and imagination meet. Owls, skeletons, cosmic patterns and glowing campsites keep turning up: totems from my own personal mythology. You’ll find my art on local craft beer labels, and on tees, hoodies and hats around Humboldt.

SGNº 08 FAQ Tended + In Bloom· from $600

Good to know before you buy

Your own FAQ from your site, in your words where I could, plus your hours and the hoodie sizing note from your apparel shop.

How do prints ship?

I choose the most cost-effective carrier for your order and where it’s going. Prints ship in 1 to 7 business days, and you get a tracking number as soon as yours ships. Limited editions ship free within the continental US, in 3 to 9 business days. Very big pieces, over 40 inches on the long side, may need a crate or freight, and I can quote that for you.

Can I return or exchange a print?

All sales are final. I package every print carefully, and if yours arrives damaged, keep all the packaging and write to me with your order number, and we’ll go from there.

Can I cancel my order?

Print sales are final, but write to me as soon as possible if you need to cancel, and I’ll see what I can do. Refunds can take 7 to 10 business days to process.

How do I buy an original?

Ask me about any original, and I’ll send you pricing and such. Shipping and tax are included in the price of most originals.

Do the hoodies run small?

They do. For the perfect fit, order one size larger than your usual. Every shirt and hoodie is made to order, so it takes a little longer to get to you.

Can I visit the studio?

Yes! The gallery and studio are at 405 Main Street in Ferndale, Thursday to Monday, 12 to 5 (hours may vary during bouts of good surf). If you visit on a day I’m closed, write to me, and if I’m around I’d be glad to come down and show you around.

How can I keep up with new work?

Sign up below to be the first to see new work, and follow along on Instagram, where I’m always posting.
