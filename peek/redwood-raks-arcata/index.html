The rooms Every build

Two rooms, hardwood floors

Redwood Raks is available at very affordable rates for classes, rehearsals, workshops, meetings, parties, performances and other special events.

The Jade Studio

The first studio when you enter the building: 40 by 60 feet and seats about 150. Hardwood floors, a stereo, a 6 by 40 foot mirror with a curtain to close over it, a long ledge, tables and chairs, remote control party lights and a mirror ball. It’s the room shows happen in, with live drumming and live music. The Mariposa Patio out back adds optional outdoor space.

The Bella Studio

A fantastic 40 by 40 foot room with hardwood floors, a 6 by 40 foot mirror, a stereo, a TV, tables, chairs, remote control party lights and a lobby that can be a dressing room for shows. No loud drums in this room.

How renting works

Private rehearsals and meetings need no application: book any time there is space on the calendar. Ongoing classes and special events each have a short application, and there are copies just inside the door of the Jade Studio.

Renters carry insurance for teaching classes and for public events, with Redwood Raks Collective as additional insured. Events that serve alcohol need an ABC permit, and larger public events need a dance permit from the City of Arcata. Everyone keeps the floor clean and avoids shoes that would mark it. The rental rates sheet is on your current page: your rates, in your words.

There’s a sound system, theatre lights, monitors, risers, folding chairs and tables to use, and a trained tech person may be needed. Ideas from your page: rehearse, choreograph, video a class, practice drumming, or hold a private dinner-dance date.

To check the calendar and reserve, call 707 296 1648 or write rakscollective@gmail.com.

    Gatherings Every build

    More than classes

    • Folk Dancers’ party

      First Fridays, 8 to 10 pm

      A live music party with local musicians. All ages and levels, no partner needed. Bring a snack to share if you like.

    • Caliente Latin Dance Night

      Second Fridays, 8 to 10:30 pm

      With the Bailando Dance Team in the Jade Studio. Latin dance socials also fill other Fridays, including a Mambo on2 salsa practica.

    • Monday Night Swing

      Mondays, 7 pm

      A beginning class, then a social dance to DJed swing tunes. Sliding scale, or a punch card for four nights.

    • Shows, milongas and haflas

      Tango milongas, belly dance haflas, Monday Night Swing specials and Artist in Residence showcases all happen here. The dates go here as you set them.

    • Summer dance and music camp

      Every July and August, ages 6 to 12

      With Miss Julie: breakdancing, hip hop, Latin styles, drumming, crafts, hula hoops, yoga, roller skating and more. 10 percent off for siblings, and partial scholarships are available. 5 and 13 year olds are welcome with the director’s approval.

    • Artist in Residence

      Since 2008, free rehearsal space for choreographers and new dance companies to develop new work, in exchange for a showcase performance. Past artists include hoop dance, modern, swing and samba.

    • Donation Depot

      Gently used dance shoes, clothing, jewelry and accessories, for sale at suggested donations in the Bella Studio lobby, to raise half scholarships for local dancers and musicians to attend summer camps.

      Good to know Tended · $600

      First time? Start here

      Do I need a partner or experience?

      Not for the beginner classes. Monday Night Swing has a friendly beginning class and Salsa @ 6 says no experience or partner is required. Dance Mix welcomes all levels and ages.

      What does a class cost?

      It depends on the teacher. Dance Mix asks a $5 donation, Salsa @ 6 and Bachata are $10, Monday Night Swing is sliding scale from $5 to $15 (or $30 for a four night punch card), and Bioenergetic Exercise is $15 or $60 for five classes. Each class card shows its own price.

      How do I find the studio?

      We’re at 8th and L Streets, at the end of the courtyard off the parking lot of the Old Creamery Building, right next to Bang Bang, with an east-facing entrance. Map apps can have trouble, so follow the courtyard.

      Can my child dance here?

      Yes. There are kids’ capoeira classes on Mondays and Thursdays, and a summer dance and music camp for ages 6 to 12 every July and August.

      Is there help with cost?

      The Scholarship Fund helps local dancers and musicians pursue training. You can apply with a short form, returned to the studio or emailed to rakscollective@gmail.com.

      Can I rent a room for a party or a show?

      Yes. Both rooms are available, with sound, lights, risers and tables. Call 707 296 1648 to check the calendar.

      Is the studio accessible?

      Your answer: step-free entrance, restrooms, parking
