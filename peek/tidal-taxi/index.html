Alaska Raptor Center

Get up close with bald eagles, owls and hawks at Alaska’s best-known raptor rehabilitation center. A quick stop, about 30 minutes, and a favorite with kids and photographers.

Fortress of the Bear

Sitka’s bear rescue sanctuary. Watch brown bears play, fish and roam in a spacious natural habitat. Tours run in 40-minute slots from 10 AM to 4 PM. Advance tickets are encouraged, so buy them on their website to guarantee your time.

fortressofthebear.org (opens in a new tab)

Waterfalls along the drive

Sitka’s 14 miles of road wind past waterfalls and lush temperate rainforest. What’s flowing depends on recent rain, and Sitka gets plenty, but on a wet week the cliffsides are alive with cascades. We stop at the best viewpoints along the way.

    Before you come to Sitka

    How do I book a tour?

    Call or text us. Let us know when you’re arriving, how many people, and what you want to see, and we’ll handle the rest. A deposit secures your reservation. Same-day booking is fine, but availability isn’t guaranteed once cruise ships have arrived, so earlier is better.

    What are your hours?

    Our regular hours are 7 AM to 7 PM. Reservations outside those hours depend on driver availability, so call or text and we’ll do our best to make it work.

    How much does a tour cost?

    Every tour is private and customized to your group, so the cost depends on how long you go and what you’d like to see. Call or text us and we’ll put together a quote just for you. A deposit of (your deposit amount) secures your reservation and goes toward your total.

    How long can a tour be?

    There’s no maximum. Half-day, full-day, or anything in between, and we’ll fit the length to your visit.

    What’s your cancellation policy?

    Cancel any time before the day of your tour and your deposit is refunded in full. Plans change, especially in Alaska, and we get it.

    Can you pick us up from the cruise ship dock?

    Yes. We pick up at the cruise ship terminal, the airport, hotels, and anywhere in town. Tell us where you are and we’ll be there.

    We’re only in port for a few hours. Can you get us back to the ship?

    Yes. Tell us your ship’s all-aboard time when you book and we’ll plan the tour to get you back with time to spare. Getting you back on schedule is our top priority.

    What should we bring?

    Dress in layers and bring a rain jacket, since Sitka weather changes fast, even in summer. Comfortable shoes, a camera, and any cash you’d like for attraction entry fees. We take care of the rest.

    Are your tours wheelchair or mobility accessible?

    Our vehicles are standard SUVs, so let us know about any mobility needs when you book and we’ll talk through what works. Many of Sitka’s best sights are viewable right from the vehicle.

    When is tour season?

    We operate from April through October, which covers the full Sitka cruise and tourism season. The best wildlife viewing is typically May through September.

    How many people fit in a vehicle?

    We can take up to (how many each vehicle seats). With two vehicles on the road daily through the season, larger groups can ride together in both. Contact us and we’ll set it up.
