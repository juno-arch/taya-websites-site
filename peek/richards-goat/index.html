    Why “Richards’ Goat”? Every build

    Named for a goat with a past

    In 1901, the “Plaza Improvement Committee” of Arcata began developing ideas for beautifying the Plaza green. A center bandstand was completed in the spring, and Charles Richards’ goat was evicted off the premises. Our little tavern and tea room is named after that mischievous goat who was kicked off the Plaza in Arcata’s early years.

    Aimee Hennessey and Merrick McKinlay opened Richards’ Goat in November 2014, and The Miniplex next door in July 2015.

    FAQs Tended + In Bloom· from $600

    Good to know before you come

    These are your own answers, from both of your sites, now in one place.

    At the tavern

    Do you take reservations?

    We don’t normally take reservations for tables, and there are usually no waits for a table before 9 pm. If you’re bringing a large party or having a birthday and would all like to sit together, we may be able to reserve the large table for you if you write us at richardsgoat@gmail.com with advance notice. We also do venue rentals for parties!

    Can I bring a cake for a birthday party?

    Yes, you can bring a cake if you’re ordering drinks, but you’ll have to bring your own paper plates and forks. You may not bring any outside beverages, and will be asked to leave if you do. This is the law, as we have a full liquor license here (not even outside water bottles are allowed).

    Do you have gift cards?

    Yes! Our gift cards can be purchased for any amount over $5 and work like a debit card. They can be purchased in person while we’re open.

    I left my card, phone or coat at the bar

    You can pick up things you left at the bar during our open hours, or call (707) 630-5000 during open hours.

    At The Miniplex

    How do I get tickets before they sell out?

    We send out an email newsletter once a month with links to tickets for upcoming shows, including limited early bird discounts. We promise not to spam you: it really is just once a month. Sign up here.

    How do I buy an advance ticket?

    Tickets for most events can be bought in advance on TicketLeap. You don’t need to print or save anything: your name and the number of tickets you bought will be on a guest list when you show up. We recommend buying online for major touring bands and nights like Disco Night and Goth Night, before they sell out.

    Can I get a refund on a ticket?

    For refunds, please email us at least 24 hours in advance, so we can release your tickets before sales are reported to distributors for the day.

    Can I reserve specific seats?

    Almost all of our events are standing room only, except for movies and poetry slams. It isn’t possible to reserve specific seats for any event.

    I left something in the theatre

    The theatre is only open for events, but you can come to Richards’ Goat next door any time during open hours to pick up things you left. We do our best to make sure they make it into the lost and found before the next show.

    I’m a musician or DJ looking for a place to play

    Please email us your request with details and press links, at richardsgoat@gmail.com.
