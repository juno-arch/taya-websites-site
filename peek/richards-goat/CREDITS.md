# Richards' Goat Tavern & Tea Room + The Miniplex mockup: photo and art credits (Oct 12 2026)

Every photo and drawing is Richards' Goat's own, from their Facebook page (facebook.com/richardsgoat) and their two websites (richardsgoat.com, miniplexevents.com), taken at the largest size offered. No stock, no stand-ins. Their Instagram grid (@richardsgoat, @miniplex_events) is almost all event flyers with words on them, so none of those were used. Customer photos on Yelp and Google were left out on purpose (not theirs, and some show patrons). No photo on the page shows a patron's face.
