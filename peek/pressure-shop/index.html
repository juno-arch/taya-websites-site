Do you take walk ins?

We’re primarily by appointment, so your barber’s ready when you get here. (walk ins when a chair is open?)

Clipper cut or precision cut?

The regular clipper cut is a clean clipper look with a trim on top. If your style needs real scissor work and styling, like a pompadour, a fringe or a shag, book the precision style cut so there’s time to do it right.

Do you cut kids’ hair?

Yes, we’re kid friendly, and plenty of families bring their boys in. (a kids’ price, if you have one)

What if I need to cancel?

Cancel or move your appointment at least 20 hours ahead. Later than that, the full price of the service is charged.

How do I pay?

Visa, Mastercard, Discover, debit cards and cash.
