About Amber Mother + Crone· from $600

Thrifting since before I could even walk

I grew up with four older sisters, which means hand-me-downs were my first wardrobe and secondhand has always felt like home.

I want to honor my body the same way that I honor the seasons, including what I choose to cover it with. Not just for fashion’s sake, but collecting things that truly awaken something in me. That’s what I knot into every piece, and what I look for in every pile.

Amber
