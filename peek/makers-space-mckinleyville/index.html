Drop-in pottery painting Every build

Pick a piece. Paint it. We fire it.

Come paint your own pottery during drop-in studio hours at the cozy McKinleyville studio. Choose from a variety of pottery pieces and a rainbow of colors to create something truly unique.

  1. Pick a piece

    Choose from the many pieces of bisqued pottery, then get a brief run down on painting rules and techniques.

  2. Paint

    Find a seat and start painting.

  3. Sign the bottom

    Write your name on the bottom and sign the contact form.

  4. Pick it up

    Firing typically takes 1 to 2 weeks, and you are contacted as soon as it’s ready.

Pieces are priced to include the basic glazes and the firing, with no added firing fees. Specialty glazes are $2 to $5, depending on the size of the piece.
