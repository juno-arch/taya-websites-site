First time? Every build

Come sweat with us

Old School is an exciting, hardworking yoga class, and the magic of it is that beginners and advanced students practice the same posture, each at their own level. There’s an exact position for everyone, and the teachers will help you find yours.

  1. Come a little earlyFind a spot on the oak floor and settle in. how early is best?
  2. Bring a mat, a towel and waterBlocks help too. Forgot a towel? Rent one for $5.
  3. Go at your own paceNinety minutes in the heat. Rest whenever you like; the posture will be there next time.
  4. Pay $15 at the doorOr pick up a 10 class pack for $120 and use it over three months.

Before you come in

ItI’ve never done hot yoga. Can I come?rsquo;s my first time doing hot yoga. Can I come?

Yes. Every class is for beginners to advanced. Everyone practices the same posture, each at their own level, and the teachers help you find yours.

How long is a class?

Ninety minutes, every class.

What kind of yoga is it?

Hatha yoga in a heated room. It has its roots in the 26 + 2 series, and Sean shapes the practice in his own way.

What should I bring?

A yoga mat, a towel and water. Yoga blocks are highly recommended. You can rent a towel for $5.

Do I need to sign up ahead?

No, just drop in. $15 a class, or $120 for 10 classes, used within 3 months.
