    The oldest bar in town, still the hometown one

    A historic room, renovated back to its 1937 bones: barn wood, brick, big beams, a pool table and a bar that fits the whole town. Smoke free, everyone welcome.

    Since 1937

    Eighty-some years, one address

    1. 1937Rebuilt on this corner after a fire, where the Old Bohemia Club stood. The beams still show the char.
    2. 2005Mel and Claudia Dick buy the 219.
    3. 2013Major renovations begin.
    4. 2016The Niner goes smoke free, in December.
    5. 2018Named Idaho’s Best Small Town Community Bar.
    6. 2025The Chamber’s Business of the Month, in June.
