# Guinevere's Candles mockup: photo credits (Oct 12 2026)

Every photo is Guinevere's Candles' own: from their Shopify shop (flowercandles.com), their public Instagram (@guineverescandles), and the photos they gave the McKinleyville Chamber of Commerce for their member page. No stock, no stand-ins. Many of their product photos already have backgrounds added in a photo app (Pixelcut); they are used exactly as they publish them.

Left out on purpose: photos with words on them (their slogan banners, the Instagram images with "www.flowercandles.com" or a discount code written across them, the "How does it burn? Awesomely." post, the Arcata Fairy Festival and Humboldt Artisans flyers), the booth photo with their wooden name sign (cropped to the left side, without the sign), and the two photos with people in them (Wendy at Whole Earth Festival, the two of them at the booth).
