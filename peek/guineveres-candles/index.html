Our story Every build

Over 30 years of candlelight

Guinevere’s Candles began in the mountains above Bridgeville, making the candles that lit the homes of off-grid families. Today we pour in McKinleyville, still by hand, still with flowers from our own gardens.

We’re a woman-owned small business on the coast of Northern California. Every candle is hand poured with love and attention to detail, with all natural ingredients, and they burn straight down with minimal dripping.

  • Flower candles: pillars, spheres, hearts, tear drops and mushrooms
  • Magic mushrooms, frogs, snails and birds
  • Merlin candles in palm feather wax, which shatters in the mold into its own pattern
  • Mason jar candles in clean scents, and essential oil candles in coconut wax
  • Fire starters, made from 100% recycled wax

FAQ Tended + In Bloom· from $600

Good to know about flower candles

These are your own answers from your FAQ page, plus two from your product pages.

What are flower candles?

Decorative candles with flowers set in the wax. When the candle is lit, the heat melts the wax and lights up the flowers, for a beautiful and enchanting display.

Will mine look like the picture?

Close, but not exactly. Each candle is as unique as the real flowers that adorn it, and every design is made by hand, so yours will be one of a kind.

Are they handcrafted?

Yes, every flower candle is handcrafted with care and attention to detail. We take pride in making each one unique and high quality.

Where do your flowers come from?

Most of our flowers are grown locally or gathered in the mountains of Northern California. We don’t import flowers, and we take care to avoid flowers sourced from China.

Are they eco friendly?

We use eco friendly materials wherever we can. Our wax is often sourced from renewable resources, and our preserved flowers are ethically harvested.

How long do they burn?

A long time. They burn straight down with minimal dripping: a tiny mushroom over 10 hours, a tall pillar over 80, and a large pillar over 150. See every burn time.

Are flower candles safe to burn?

Yes, when used responsibly. Set your candle on a heat resistant surface, away from anything that can catch, and don’t leave it unattended while it’s lit.

How do I care for mine?

Keep it out of direct sunlight and away from extreme temperatures. Trim the wick before each use, and let it burn long enough for the whole top to melt into a pool of wax.
