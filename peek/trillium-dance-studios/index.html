          Twenty years of the Trillium family

          Chloe, Haylee and Allie, photographed by Scurfield Photography.

          Erin McKeever

          Owner & Artistic Director

          Born and raised in Humboldt County, Erin founded the studio in 2006 as Dance Class with Erin Fernandez. It has grown into Trillium: more than 150 students, a team of teachers, three studios off the Plaza and two performing companies. She has created and produced more than 35 large scale productions and loved every minute of it.

          Erin taught ballet and jazz on the faculty of Humboldt State’s Department of Theater, Film & Dance for seven years, has taught and performed in Spain with the Estada Internacional de Danza, and holds Dance Teacher Magazine’s certification for dance educators.

          For parents Tended + In Bloom· from $600

          Good to know before class

          What color leotard does my ballet dancer wear?

          Each ballet level has its class color, with pink tights, pink leather ballet slippers and hair in a neat bun with pins.

          • Pre Ballet: light pink
          • Intro: soft blue
          • Beginning: lavender
          • Ballet 1: marine
          • Ballet 2: mulberry
          • Ballet 3: royal
          • Ballet 4: burgundy
          • Ballet 5: teal
          • Ballet 6: plum
          • Ballet 7: navy
          • Ballet 8+ and Teen: black

          Class color leotards are at the studio or in our Nimbly store.

          Which level is right for my dancer?

          Placement is based on age, ability and dance experience, and dancers usually spend a full year (two sessions) in each level. Email us and we’ll help you find the perfect class.

          Where do I park?

          It’s all city parking, and there’s a loading zone right in front of the studio for drop off and pick up.

          Can I watch class?

          Parents are welcome to watch the last class of each month.

          What goes in the dance bag?

          A water bottle, extra hair supplies and an extra pair of tights. Please write your dancer’s name inside both shoes, and arrive 5 minutes early to get ready.

          What if we miss a class?

          Tuition stays the same when a class is missed, and it can’t be refunded, so please make class a priority. The last class before a show and every rehearsal are needed to dance in the show.

          I’m an adult. Do I need experience?

          Not at all. Beginners are welcome, the dress code is flexible, and there’s no registration fee.
