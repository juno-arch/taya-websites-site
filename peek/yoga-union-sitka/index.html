What’s on Every build

Eight kinds of class, one door

Yoga Union is a nonprofit, dedicated to making yoga accessible to all. Come to a beginner class, a flow, a slow floor practice or an evening of sound. Mats and props are there to borrow.

Beginner, Pay As You Can

Fridays · 8:30 to 9:30 am · Beth

A beginner and gentle class. Pay what you can, from $1 to $10, or use a punch.

Gentle Yoga

Fridays · noon to 1 pm · Lisa

Yoga that is accessible to all students: poses for core strength and balance, moving with the breath, with a little breath work and meditation.

Gentle Flow

Mondays · 6 to 7 pm · Jean

Instructional movement and breathwork, good for beginners and up.

Vinyasa Flow

Tuesdays with Melonie · Wednesdays with Zoe · 6 to 7 pm

Breath and movement linked into one flowing sequence. Wednesdays are an all-level power vinyasa at a steady pace: new students and returning students are welcome.

Hatha Vinyasa

Sundays · 4 to 5:15 pm · Skye

Breath-to-movement flows that build warmth and energy, with moments to slow down and settle into poses for longer. For intermediate students.

Yin Yoga

Saturdays · 10 to 11 am · Jade

A restful hour: a slow, meditative floor practice, with poses held for 3 to 10 minutes. Open to all. Dress warmly.

Somatic Sound Journey

Thursday evenings · 6 to 7 pm · Laura

Gentle movement and breath awareness, then rest while tones from a variety of sound instruments fill the room. All levels, and no yoga or music experience needed.

Sound Healing sessions

Special evenings · Jean

Gentle movement and sound with Jean, a Level 1 Sound Healing Certified Practitioner. $15 plus tax, or one punch. Each session has its own sign-up form.

    Good to know Tended + In Bloom· from $600

    Before your first class

    What if I haven’t done yoga before?

    You are welcome. Try one of the beginner or level 1 classes, like the Pay As You Can class on Friday mornings.

    What if I’m on a tight budget?

    There is a scholarship program for anyone for whom full price would keep them from coming. Fill out a scholarship form, which you can pick up at the studio before or after class. Scholarships are $53 for people on public assistance or AmeriCorps, Jesuit or other volunteers, and $74 for others on tight budgets.

    How do I pay?

    At the studio, with a discount for cash or check, or by credit card. You can also pay on the website.

    Do you have mats?

    Yes, with lots of other props. Bringing your own mat is encouraged. Please clean any borrowed mats and blocks when you’re done.

    What if I’m late?

    Try to arrive a little early. If you do come late, please be as quiet as you can.

    Where is the studio?

    Downtown Sitka, on the Sitka Fine Arts Campus, at Kellog Hall, 115 College Drive. Park at the Hames Center and follow the path to the first building; the steps are below.
