          Questions people ask

          Quick answers before you visit.

          Do you have gluten free and vegan options?

          Yes. We bake a gluten free bread on Fridays after 10 am. It isn’t baked in a certified gluten free bakery, so it may contain traces of gluten and isn’t recommended for people with celiac disease. Our Country Sourdough, Kalamata & Herbs and baguette are vegan, and the smoothies are blended with coconut milk.

          Can I order ahead for pickup?

          Yes. Pickup orders go through our order page, picked up at 327 Seward St, Ste 2.

          Do you deliver?

          We’re a dine in and carry out café, and we don’t deliver.

          Is there Wi-Fi?

          Yes, free Wi-Fi.
