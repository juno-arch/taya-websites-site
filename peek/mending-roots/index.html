Good to know before you come

Something else on your mind? Call or text (707) 440-9035. No question is too small.

Do you take insurance?

We don’t take insurance. You can check whether your flexible spending account covers your session, or whether your insurance lets you submit a claim yourself.

What should I wear for structural bodywork?

Comfortable clothes you can move in. Structural bodywork is done fully clothed.

Can I add the sauna or red light to my massage?

Yes. Add 30 minutes of infrared sauna for $25, or 20 minutes of red light for $15, to any massage or bodywork session.

Is the sauna safe for me?

Heat and light aren’t right for everyone. If you’re pregnant, take medication, or have a heart or blood pressure condition, please check with your doctor before booking.

I take medications. Can I still work with an herbalist?

Yes. Yemaya is trained in how herbs and medications interact, and her intake form asks about everything you take.

What if I need to cancel?

For herbal consultations, please give 24 hours’ notice. Late cancellations are $50, or $15 for phone sessions. For everything else, let us know as soon as you can. your policy here

Does Jennifer see men?

Jennifer’s practice is for women. She sees men by referral, or if you’re already her client.

Where do I park, and which door do I use?

We’re in the building on the corner of H Street and Harris Street. Come in from Harris. There’s off-street parking in front and behind.

Is my first InBody scan really free?

It is. After that a scan is $25, or 4 for $60 and 8 for $120, used within six months.

How long is a postural alignment course?

Most people come for about 16 sessions over about six months, with exercises to do at home in between.
