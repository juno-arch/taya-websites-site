Jennifer, in her own words

Hello, I am Jennifer Teixeira LMT owner of Humboldt Women's Massage.

I facilitate resilience, renewal, and rejuvenation in the midst of aging, honoring the sacred essence of life. I am highly skilled in working with a variety of musculoskeletal dysfunctions and I have over 20 years experience working as a Massage Therapist.

​

I have invested in over 1000 hours of training with some of the best schools in the world and have been practicing Massage for the past 20 years. This extensive training and experience have given me an in-depth knowledge of the different massage techniques and methods I use to provide my clients with the most effective and personalized treatment. My ultimate goal is to ensure that each client leaves my massage table feeling rejuvenated, relaxed, and satisfied. The massage I offer is not just a physical experience but also an emotional and mental one. That's why I take a holistic approach to massage therapy, considering each client's individual needs. So, if you're looking for a massage that's not just a temporary fix but a long-lasting solution to your pain and discomfort, you can trust me to provide you with the best possible treatment.

When I was 7 months pregnant with my son I had a moment where it had become painful to walk, and the pain was in my hips. I couldn't work and I could barely walk. It was suggested by a friend that I get a massage for my hips. After the massage I was sore, but the next day I felt freedom of movement in my hips and the stagnation had been alleviated. I was hooked! Massage has been a passion of mine since.

I studied at the National Holistic Institute in Emeryville California, and eventually found myself in Makawao, Maui at Hale Ho'omana learning more about this ancient form of healing so that I can bring that Healing to you.

Massage is not just for the back and shoulders; the hips carry all of your upper body as well. My goal is to include the whole body in a Customized Massage that will help you feel more flexible and invite the energy to flow unburdened in your body.
