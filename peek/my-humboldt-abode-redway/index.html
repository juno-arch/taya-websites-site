    What happens at a first massage or Reiki session?

    Every massage and Reiki session begins with a complimentary intake consultation, so Samantha can hear what your body needs before you begin.

    What is forest bathing?

    Shinrin-Yoku is the practice of slowing down among the trees and letting the forest settle your mind. It’s a one hour session.

    Can I book a treatment if I’m not staying?

    Yes. Most guests come on retreat, and Samantha loves having neighbors in the treatment room too.

    For the Studio, check in is 4 to 10 pm with a self check in, and check out is by 10 am. (the Bungalow’s times, if they differ)
