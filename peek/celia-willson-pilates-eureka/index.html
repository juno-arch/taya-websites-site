    About Celia Every build

    Meet Celia

    Celia’s love of movement began in 2003, when she was first introduced to yoga while living in Hawaii and attended classes daily. She came to Pilates in 2019 and knew right away that this resistance training was what her body had been asking for.

    She teaches alignment based classes and believes that by safely and consistently bringing yoga and Pilates into our lives, we gain strength, find happiness, and move through our days with more ease.

    When we find exercise that we enjoy, that is fun and that feels good, it becomes easy to prioritize.Celia
    • NCPT classical and contemporary Pilates teacher
    • STOTT Pilates, Level 1 and Level 2
    • Advanced classical Pilates, all equipment
    • Scolio-Pilates, Modular 1 and 2
    • E-RYT 500 Yoga Alliance teacher
    • Anusara 200 hour, TheraYoga 300 hour
    • Yoga Nidra certified
    • Barre certified
    • Women’s health and nutrition coach
