Little questions

How long do custom orders take?

Custom orders are available with a 4 to 6 week turnaround, made to fit.

Is the cost of supplies included in alterations?

Supply cost is not included in the alterations prices. A plus after a price means it starts there.

Can I bring my own fabric or an old garment?

Please do. We’re big on upcycling natural fibers, so bring whatcha got and we’ll turn it into something worth wearing again.

Do I need an appointment?

For a fitting or consultation, message, call or book a time. The shop is open Monday, Tuesday and Thursday through Saturday, 12 to 7, and Sundays are by appointment.
