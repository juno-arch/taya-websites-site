Stitchy, witchy, and local

StitchCraft is run by Jess and Brad Doudna, who opened it in downtown Saratoga as a one-stop shop for sewing and leather work, bulk herbs and teas, incense, books and a little magick.

Come in with a hem, a favorite jacket, or a list of teas, and stay for a look around.
