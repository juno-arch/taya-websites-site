Good to know Every build

Before you bring it in

Is the cost of supplies included?

Supply cost is not included in the alterations prices. A plus after a price means it starts there.

Do I need an appointment?

For a fitting or consultation, message, call or book a time. The shop is open Monday, Tuesday and Thursday through Saturday, 12 to 7, and Sundays are by appointment.

Not sure what it needs?

Message or call, or send a photo through the question form, and the shop will write back with what it takes. Or just bring it in.
