Welcome

A place to rest, and be held

In these times of intersecting crises and world (re)making, A Restful Space offers an intimate, compassionate place to rest, grieve, and reconnect in tender community.

Together we practice rest and slowness, gently decondition from grind culture, metabolize emotions, and support our tender hearts so we can keep them open. Warm, locally crafted herbal tea is prepared for every offering.

Offerings Every build

Three ways to come and rest

Restorative Yoga & Yoga Nidrā

Small weekly classes of long-held poses, 10 to 15 minutes or more, all seated or lying down and fully supported by blankets, bolsters and blocks. Yoga Nidrā is yogic sleep: a guided rest for the whole nervous system.

Weekly class$25 · sliding scale

Private Deep Rest sessions

Ninety minutes in two to four fully supported poses, oriented around the heart. Reiki and tuning forks on or near the body, crystal singing bowls, breath, meditation and sometimes poetry, on an infrared heated mat with jade and tourmaline stone. Warm herbal tea after.

90 minutes$90 to $130 · two people $60 to $80 each

Community Grief Circle

Once a month, a circle of seven gathers to acknowledge, honor and metabolize personal, collective and ecological grief, held with care and closed with breath, song and sound bath.

Monthly, 3 hours$36 · sliding scale

Seasonal workshops and retreats

Lunar Rest-orative Yoga series, equinox and solstice practices with sound bath and journaling, and Rest & Grief Retreats, each with a partial scholarship spot.

Online courses, wherever you are

Yoga for Ecological Grief, now housed in the Wisdom School of Embodied Philosophy, and Breathing for Palestine, a sliding-scale fundraising course with free enrollment on request.

Community Grief Circle Every build

Grief, held in community

Gather in tender community to acknowledge, honor and metabolize personal, collective, systemic and ecological grief. Together we practice deep listening and compassionate witnessing, without the need to fix or solve.

  1. Arriving. Gentle somatic mindfulness and an opening ritual create a trauma-conscious container.
  2. Sharing, only if you wish. You’re invited, never required, to speak anything you would like witnessed and held. Poetry, prose, artwork, photos or song are welcome too.
  3. Closing. We seal the container with breath, meditation, song and sound bath. Warm herbal tea is served.

This is an anti-racist, pro-Palestinian, pro-queer+trans, pro-humanity space committed to collective liberation.

Who holds the space

Laura Johnson, PhD, RYT-500

Laura founded A Restful Space to offer accessible, trauma-conscious, post-capitalist communal rest and grief practice in Humboldt County. A yoga practitioner for more than a decade, she has offered yin and restorative classes, workshops and private sessions here since 2018.

She is also a college geography lecturer, a parent, a writer and an embodied activist, with essays and poetry in Lion’s Roar, Tikkun and Resilience.org, and a children’s book, The Little Book of Rest, inspired by bedtimes with her daughter. She lives on unceded Wiyot land in Arcata.

Gentle questions, plain answers

What people tend to wonder before their first visit.

I’ve never done yoga. Is this for me?

Yes. No yoga experience is necessary, and all bodies are welcome. Every restorative pose is seated or lying down, and your body is completely supported by blankets, bolsters and blocks.

What happens at a grief circle? Do I have to speak?

You’re invited, never required, to share. Some people speak, some bring a poem or a photo, some simply listen. The circle is held with deep listening and compassionate witnessing, with no need to fix or solve.

How does the sliding scale work?

The fair value of a session is near the middle of the scale. Those who can give at the higher end help balance it for those who need the lower end. For a sliding-scale or community care spot, email Laura and ask for what you need.

How do I pay?

Classes and circles are booked through Square. For private sessions, PayPal, Venmo or cash are welcome.
