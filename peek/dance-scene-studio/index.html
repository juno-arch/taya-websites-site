    The company and the team

    Alongside the recreational classes, the Dance Scene offers a complete pre-professional ballet curriculum, with an emphasis on coordination, musicality and technical proficiency.

    Sundance Ballet Company

    The studio’s performance company, classical and contemporary ballet, directed by Carrie Badeaux. Founded in 2012 as a nonprofit DreamMaker program of the Ink People, it produces major productions each year and gives back to the community.

    The Scene Team

    The award-winning, nationally competitive team competes throughout the year. Team dancers take nine classes a week with their instructors and the team coach.

    Good questions, plain answers

    What parents tend to ask before the first class.

    How young can my child start?

    At two! Creative Ballet is a 30 minute class for ages 2 to 3, with rhythm instruments and dance props each week. Tiny Tap starts at 3, and Pre-Ballet at 4.

    Can we try a class first?

    Any class is $20 as a drop-in. A free first class: (your trial policy)

    What does my dancer wear?

    The class color leotard for their ballet level (see Ballet levels), pink tights and ballet shoes, with hair pulled back and secured. Everything is at the boutique, sizes toddler to small adult.

    Can I watch class?

    Yes, through the viewing window or the curtain. To keep class private and focused, no videos or photos of students during class.

    What if we miss a class?

    Make it up within 4 weeks, in a class that fits. Missed classes can’t be refunded or moved to the next month’s tuition.

    How do we pay?

    Cash, check or any major credit card, by the term or by the month. Monthly tuition is due by the first class of the month. Checks can go in the tuition box, with your dancer’s name on the memo line.

    Is there a family discount?

    Yes, family rates apply for up to three immediate family members. (how much)

    Does every dancer perform?

    Yes. Classes run year-round, and every dancer is in the annual June recital. Costume fees: (your costume fee)
