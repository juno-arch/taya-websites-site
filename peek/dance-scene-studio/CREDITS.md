# The Dance Scene Studio mockup photos

Every photo on this page is the studio's own, from their website danceeureka.com (Turbify site; images load by JavaScript from lh3.googleusercontent.com, captured at original size with =s0). No stock photos are used. Captured Oct 4 2026.

## From their "Dance Scene Gallery" page (the Dance Scene Photography Exhibition; originals 2400 to 4600 px)
- hero.webp (+ hero-sm.webp for the link card): a dancer in the surf at sunset, a silhouette, no face. 2470 x 1651 original, exported 2400 wide.
- stage.webp: a ballerina from behind on an empty theater stage (looks like the Van Duzer; the page doesn't say, so the alt text doesn't name it).
- pointe-trio, brick, boardwalk, fence: feet and legs only.
- mustard, sky-stairs, sunset-field, redwoods, tree, mural, rock: small, far figures, no clear faces.
- fisherman.webp: the Fisherman statue silhouetted at sunset (no dancer visible at this size).

## From their "Sundance Ballet Company" page
- rehearsal.webp: company dancers in a brick-walled room, dim, faces small.

## Their logo (homepage banner image, 6913 x 3456)
- ds-full.png: their "The Dance Scene studio" lettering, cut out as an alpha mask (used as a CSS mask for the nav and footer wordmark). Frame lines trimmed.
- ds-dancer.webp: the line-drawn dancer with the floral dress from the left side of their logo, cut out of the black (used on the Visit card and in the easter egg).
