Watercolor Fundamentals

Fridays, 10 to 12Oct 9 to Nov 13, 2026

For anyone just starting their watercolor journey. Mixing harmonious colors, brushwork and glazing, texture, and a sweet little painting to finish.

The six Fridays
  1. Color theory. A color wheel from the primaries, and how to mix.
  2. Techniques. The six basic watercolor techniques, charted.
  3. Textures. Loaded washes, additives, tools and brushwork.
  4. Composition. Perspective, depth and design.
  5. Landscape. An 8 by 10 watercolor landscape.
  6. Still life. Everything together, in one painting.

The light box

Line, then value, then light

Every painting starts as a drawing. In the Monday oil course we set a still life in the light box and build it the way Nella teaches it: line and block in, then value, a first wash of color, glaze over glaze, and last of all the light. The drawing class gives you the first two, watercolor the wash, and oil the rest.

The painter behind the atelier

Nella Rubinstien

Nella is a Healdsburg painter of fine art oil paintings: still life lit like old silver, portraits, and the hills around town. She built the atelier with the community in mind, a beautiful studio space for learning and gathering, set up for every level from a brand new beginner to a highly advanced student.

Good questions, plain answers

What people tend to ask before they pick up a brush on Hudson Street.

I’ve never painted. Is that okay?

Yes. Every class welcomes beginners, and the studio has instructors to help everyone from a brand new beginner to a highly advanced student.

What should I bring?

Just yourself. Materials are included in the six session classes and the Thursday watercolor mornings. Coffee and tea are on us. anything to bring for oil, like a smock

Can I join a session late?

For drawing, yes: you can begin in the second week as well. the other classes

Is there a teacher at figure drawing?

No, it’s a drop in session with a live model and no instructor, $45, 10 to 12:30. Text (707) 791-4028 for upcoming dates.
