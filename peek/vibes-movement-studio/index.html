Our studio Every build

Make yourself at home

Vibes is a boutique movement studio designed to help you build strength, move with intention, and feel at home in your body. The room is bright and filled with natural light, with premium props for every class.

Whether you’re stepping into your first class or have been moving with us for years, you’ll be welcomed exactly as you are. Pre-signup is encouraged, and walk-ins are always welcome: if your day changes, just stop by.

More than anything, we’re building a community where every body belongs.Vibes

      Before your first class

      Do I need to sign up ahead?

      Pre-signup is encouraged, but we do welcome walk-ins. If your schedule changes, feel free to stop by.

      I’m new. Which class should I try?

      Every class is open to all levels, and teachers offer modifications. Try the finder above, or start with the intro offer: three classes for $30.

      How warm is the warm room?

      Inferno Hot Pilates, Heated Sculpt, Heated Pilates, Sculpt and Flow and Power Flow are taught in a heated room. (the room temperature) Bring water and a towel.

      What should I bring?

      (mats and props: bring your own, or borrow one?) The studio is thoughtfully equipped with premium props for every class.

      Do you have student pricing?

      Yes, all year long: a $13 student drop-in and a $120 student 10 class pass, bought in the studio.

      How do I cancel?

      In the Mindbody app or on the Mindbody website, up to 4 hours before class. Cancellations can’t be taken by phone, email or social media. Miss a class you booked and it uses a class from your pass; on an unlimited pass, a no show is $10.

      Where do I park?

      We’re at 613 H Street, Suite A, in downtown Arcata. (your parking note)
