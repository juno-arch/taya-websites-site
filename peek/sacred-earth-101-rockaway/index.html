About Melody Every build

Meet Melody Lane

Melody is the owner of Sacred Earth 101 and the artist behind the paintings and the print on demand pieces. She has painted since 2005: winter landscapes, snowmen and Christmas, Halloween and sci fi, cats, birds, woodland animals and abstracts.

In her words: “I strive to educate and offer supplies for anyone seeking to begin or continue their spiritual journey. I am an extensive rock hound, love cats, and truly enjoy creating things with my hands for other people.”
