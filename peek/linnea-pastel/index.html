The small print, made easy

Deposits hold time that could have gone to someone else, so a few kind rules keep it fair for everyone.

Deposits

Deposits are required to book, and they’re non-refundable and non-transferable. Your deposit holds all of your appointment dates and comes off your total the day your tattoo is finished. If you can pay the rest in cash, it’s greatly appreciated.

Rescheduling

Please give one week’s notice, and use the confirmation text or email you got when you booked. Rescheduling more than once means a new deposit.

Running late

Please arrive on time, or send a message if you’re running late. Fifteen minutes late without a word counts as a no-show, and the deposit is forfeited.

How far ahead can I book?

Booking stays open 60 days out, because I travel often. Want April 1st? Set a reminder for the beginning of February, when April opens. Times also need to be requested 24 hours ahead.

Prepare and aftercare Every build

Before you come, and after you go

Preparing for your appointment

  1. Get a good night’s rest before your appointment.
  2. No alcohol within 24 hours before.
  3. Drink plenty of water and moisturize. Hydrated skin matters.
  4. Wear something comfy that gives easy access to the spot we’re working on, and maybe a sweatshirt in case you get chilly. Please skip perfume, for others with allergies.
  5. Bring water or another non-alcoholic drink, headphones if you’d like your own music, and any snacks you need. Please don’t bring animal products into the studio.
  6. Bring cash for your remaining balance.

Basic mandatory aftercare

  1. Keep your tattoo wrapped overnight. Take the bandage off the next morning with clean hands.
  2. Wash it with warm water and liquid soap for 30 seconds, then rinse with cold for 30 more. Wash 3 times a day until it’s healed.
  3. Around day 3, start moisturizing: a thin layer of plain unscented lotion or cocoa butter, 3 times a day, after washing. No ointments, please.
  4. Don’t pick, scratch or exfoliate while it heals, and don’t soak it (swimming, baths and so on).
  5. SPF protects your tattoos over time.

The re-wrap method (optional)

  1. Follow the basic aftercare.
  2. After washing the next morning, dry it and wrap it in plastic wrap.
  3. Wash and re-wrap before bed and in the morning, for 3 days.
  4. Then carry on with basic aftercare.

The Tegaderm method (optional)

  1. Tegaderm is sold online by companies like Saniderm, DermShield and Second Skin. Put it on after you remove the first bandage, 24 hours after your tattoo.
  2. Wash your tattoo and make sure it’s completely dry. Peel off the paper backing, smooth the sticky side down flat on clean, dry skin, then remove the label side so it’s fully clear.
  3. It’s waterproof and can stay on up to 6 days. Always wash after taking off any bandage.
  4. To remove it, peel up an edge and stretch it outward, away from the tattoo, like a command strip. It lifts slowly.

Blood, plasma and ink pooling under the bandage is normal. If it fills with fluid, do not puncture or drain it. Stop using it if your skin gets irritated.

About Linnea Every build

From Chicago, by way of everywhere

Originally from Chicago, Linnea started working in tattoo shops in 2007 and finished her apprenticeship in 2009. Her work is some sort of retro 80s take on American traditional, mixed with tribal, vintage animals and queer iconography.

While living in North Carolina, around 2019, she found a passion for goldsmithing and designing apparel under the name Pastel, and it became a whole new world: textiles and metals, merged with tattoo illustration.

These days Linnea lives in Las Vegas with her cats, tattoos by appointment in a private studio, and travels monthly for guest spots and conventions all over the world.
