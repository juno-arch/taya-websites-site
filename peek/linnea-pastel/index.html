Booking + general info

Deposits

Deposits are required to book tattoo services. All deposits are non-refundable and non-transferable. Your deposit will be deducted the day that your tattoo has been completed.

Your deposit will be forfeited and you may be denied service under these circumstances:

  • If you fail to give ONE WEEKS notice upon rescheduling your appointment.
  • If you fail to show up to your appointment or are 15 minutes late without any notice.
  • If you reschedule your appointment MORE than once.
Rescheduling

If you need to reschedule your appointment, please do so using the confirmation text or email you received upon booking your appointment.

Running late

Please arrive on time or send a message if you are running late.

How can I get an appointment?

All scheduling must be requested 24 hours in advance or the time slot will appear unavailable. Linnea travels often and cannot accommodate bookings that are multiple months in advance. Booking periods only remain open for 60 days out.

How do I book for a guest spot?

Please message Linnea directly on Instagram (opens in a new tab) to book in for a guest spot or convention.

Location & accessibility

The studio is unfortunately not wheelchair accessible. If you are unable to use stairs, please contact Linnea directly to see if accommodations can be made. There is free parking located directly in front of the building.

Aftercare Every build

Prepare / aftercare

Preparing for your appointment

  1. Be sure to get a good nights rest before your appointment.
  2. Do not drink any alcohol within 24 hours before your appointment.
  3. Drink plenty of water and moisturize!
  4. Wear something comfortable that easily allows access to the area we’re working on, and maybe a sweatshirt incase you get chilly. Due to allergies of others, please don’t wear any perfumes.
  5. Bring water or any other non-alcoholic beverage, headphones if you’d like to listen to your own music, any snacks you may need. Please do not bring any animal products into the studio.
  6. Please bring cash to pay your remaining balance!

Basic mandatory aftercare

  1. Keep your tattoo wrapped overnight. Remove the bandage the following morning with clean hands.
  2. Hand wash your tattoo with warm water and liquid soap for 30 seconds, rinse with cold for an additional 30 seconds. Continue to wash your tattoo for 3 times a day until it is healed.
  3. You can start to moisturize your tattoo around the 3rd day. Apply a thin layer of plain unscented lotion or cocoa butter 3 times a day and wash before application. Please do not use any ointments on your tattoo.
  4. Do not pick, scratch, or exfoliate your tattoos while they are healing. Do not submerge your tattoo in water (swimming, baths, etc) while it is healing.
  5. SPF is recommended to protect your tattoos over time.

Re-wrap method (optional)

  1. After washing your tattoo the following morning, dry, and wrap with plastic wrap.
  2. Wash your tattoo and re-wrap before bed and in the morning for 3 days.
  3. Continue basic aftercare.

Tegaderm method (optional)

  1. Tegaderm may be purchased online from companies like Saniderm, DermShield, and Second Skin. It is recommended to apply after removing the initial bandage 24 hours after receiving the tattoo.
  2. Wash your tattoo and make sure it is completely dry before applying tegaderm.
  3. Remove paper backing and apply sticky side to clean, dry skin. Smooth down as flat as possible. Remove label side so that the bandage becomes completely clear.
  4. Tegaderm is waterproof and may be worn for up to 6 days after application. Make sure to always wash after removal of any bandage.
  5. Removal: peel up edge and stretch outwards away from the tattoo like a 3M/command strip. Bandage will slowly lift up.

Pooling of blood, plasma, and ink under the bandage is normal. If tegaderm fills with fluid, DO NOT puncture or drain your bandage. Discontinue use if irritation occurs.
