How to consign Every build

Bring it in, and let us do the work

  1. Bring up to 15 items a dayClean and ready for sale. If you have more, bring them in the next day.
  2. Head to the back of the storeFind the “Consignment Drop Off” sign. Ask for a copy of the contract to take home, and fill out the info slip that goes with your basket: account number, full name, date, email and phone.
  3. Read the signs at the counterThey show what the racks aren’t taking at that moment, because racks fill up.
  4. Wait for your emailIt can take up to a week to enter your items. Then you’ll get an email of what was entered, and a heads-up on any “no thank you” items, which you have 24 hours to pick up.
  5. Your items go on the floor for 45 daysThey’re marked down regularly, with sales to keep things moving. Stop in about once a month to keep your account current.

Our story Every build

Three racks, 400 square feet, and a whole lot of hope

Over sixteen years ago, my children were just two and four years old, and I was running a taxi business and bartending. I was grateful for the work, but I knew it wasn’t the life I wanted for my family.

In October, I took the leap. I got my business license, rented a small space, and opened the doors with no advertising and no fanfare. Just 400 square feet, three clothing racks, and a whole lot of hope.

In those early days, it felt like we were all in the same boat, my family and this little business, learning as we went. … Every time we grew, our boat grew with us.

This business has always been about more than selling items; it’s about people.

Good to know Tended + In Bloom· from $600

Before you load up the car

How many items can I bring?

Up to 15 items per person in one day. If you have more, bring them in the next day.

What do you take?

Clean, freshly washed and wrinkle free clothing, plus household items, shoes, handbags, scarves and jewelry under the same guidelines. Today’s board shows what the racks want right now, and it changes daily, so if you don’t see what you’re bringing, give the shop a call.

Why did you say “no thank you” to an item?

It isn’t personal. Items with stains, rips, tears, faded spots, fuzz balls, missing buttons, broken zippers, or a smoke, attic or musty smell aren’t right for the racks, and clothing newer than five years is the goal. You’ll get an email about them and have 24 hours to pick them up.

Can I drop off and go?

Please stay for the drop-off, unless it’s a donation. Donations are always welcome during business hours.

How much do I earn?

You receive 40% of the selling price, and furniture is split 50/50. Your items are on the floor for 45 days.

How do I use my store credit?

Bring your ID each time you visit, for using store credit or picking up money from your account. Using a family member’s account is fine once the account owner has authorized you, in person at the store or by email from the original email on file.
