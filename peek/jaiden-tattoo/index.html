    Tattoo care Tended + In Bloom· from $600

    Healing it well

    Proper aftercare is what keeps your tattoo bold and healthy for years. You’ll get a care kit and a sheet after your session; here’s the short version.

    1. Days 1 to 5

      The bandage

      A dermal bandage can stay on 5 to 7 days. Any other wrap comes off within 3 hours unless I say otherwise.

    2. Days 1 to 10

      Wash

      With clean hands, gently wash with warm running water and the soap from your kit, twice a day. Pat dry with a paper towel.

    3. Days 1 to 10

      Heal

      A small amount of salve after every wash, until it starts healing.

    4. Day 10 on

      Moisturize

      When it’s dry and itchy, switch to the cream. Once healed, SPF 50 every time you’re in the sun.

    For 3 weeks, avoidre-bandaging, scratching or picking, sun and tanning beds, pet hair, workouts that stretch the area, and swimming or soaking.

    Questions Tended + In Bloom· from $600

    Before you inquire

    Still unsure about anything? Email me. I want you to feel confident and excited.

    Can I see my design before my appointment?

    No. I design with you there, often freehand on the skin, and we go over the details together at your appointment. Most often there’s nothing prepared to show ahead of time.

    Do you do consultations?

    No. For big projects like sleeves, much of our first session is planning, anywhere from 30 minutes to 2 hours, and that time is included in your session.

    What should I bring?

    A valid ID, snacks and water, headphones or a book, loose black clothes that make the area easy to reach, and a cozy layer. It can get chilly during long sessions.

    Can I book sessions back to back?

    Yes, up to 3 in a row if you’d like. Otherwise, wait at least 2 weeks between sessions. Please think about your own pain tolerance.

    What if I’m traveling to see you?

    Thank you for making the trip, it means a lot! I’m a little more flexible for out-of-state clients if flights get delayed, so just let me know. Grants Pass is a small Southern Oregon town with great food, vineyards and outdoor adventures, and I’m happy to recommend places to stay and eat.

    Do you offer touch-ups?

    Yes, usually free if it’s under an hour, booked within a year with your original booking link. Send me photos of the spot first. Poor aftercare may mean a full charge, and after a year it’s natural aging.

    Can I use numbing cream?

    Yes, but I don’t provide or apply it. If you use one, buy it ahead and research it well: some cause reactions or change how your skin takes ink.

    What if I’m pregnant or breastfeeding?

    I won’t tattoo you while you’re pregnant; reschedule for well after your due date. If you’re breastfeeding, please get your doctor’s okay before booking.

    Do you have any discounted designs?

    Yes! Message me to see them. Each one is priced and sized on its own, and you can peek at them in my “available” story highlight on Instagram.

    Is there a waitlist?

    Yes, for existing clients with ongoing projects. My booking system sends a note automatically when a spot opens.

    My project wasn’t accepted. Now what?

    Make sure it fits my style, but honestly, I just can’t fit every awesome project in. Ask me for artist recommendations, or send it again next booking season.
