Since 2010

Fire and flow, as a way to heal

Bella founded Bella Vita Fire Dance Co. in Humboldt County in 2010. She’s a performing artist, dance teacher, certified fire walking instructor and coach, and her love of dance is channeled through the element of fire.

Her workshops weave together flow props, intentional movement and healing: poi, fans, staff, batons, silks and more. Her latest troupe, the Humboldt Fire Dragons, performs all over the county, and it’s an Ink People DreamMaker project.

Fire walking

Step through the fear

Fire walking is a practice held sacred by many cultures, a tool for transformation and personal empowerment. Bella is certified by the FireWalking Center of California, under Tolly Burkan, and leads walks and fire walking circles for groups, teams and events.

You don’t have to walk to come. The walk is an invitation, offered to anyone who wants to face a fear and step forward into confidence.

  • Personal growth
  • Team building
  • Perspective shifts
  • Motivational breakthroughs
  • Spiritual development

“Through the experience of walking upon hot coals I found within me the courage and the determination to take each intentional step with precise focus.”

Bella, on why she walks
