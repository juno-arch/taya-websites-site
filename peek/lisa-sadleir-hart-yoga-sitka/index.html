About Lisa Every build

Quiet practice, since 2002

Lisa has practiced yoga since 2002 and has taught gentle and restorative yoga in Sitka since early 2007. In 2016 she took the Yoga Development Course at Yasodhara Ashram in British Columbia, and she went on to train in Hatha Yoga, Kundalini & Dreams Yoga and Hidden Language Hatha Yoga.

  • She watches birds every day and counts them for citizen science.
  • She has been a guest on KCAW’s Library Show, talking about Yasodhara books and her path to teaching. More from KCAW (opens in a new tab)
