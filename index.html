Both plans can take bookings and send a newsletter. The difference is who builds the tools: with Tended I set up trusted third-party tools in your name, and with In Bloom your booking, shop, gift certificates, waivers and portal are hand-built by me, on my own server.

Your booking, shop and more, built by me on my server

  • Booking. Clients pick a time on your site, with a deposit if you like, plus confirmation and reminder emails. Busy times on your own calendar stay blocked. Made for one-on-one appointments; group classes stay in your class app.

  • Your own portal. A private place with its own sign-in, with only the parts your business uses.

What it costs

All of it is covered by your $90 a month subscription: booking, the shop, gift certificates, waivers and your portal. There’s no separate app to sign up for or pay each month.

Card payments go through your own Stripe at Stripe’s usual rate (about 2.9% + 30¢ a payment), straight to you. I don’t take a cut. If you ever cancel, I help you move these into accounts of your own.

What apps like these usually cost on their own

  • Booking apps for one person: about $20 to $35 a month (Fresha, Acuity, Vagaro, Booksy). A few are free for one person, like Square’s free plan and Cal.com.
  • Shop builders: about $15 to $39 a month (Big Cartel, Shopify, Squarespace, Wix), and some add 2% on each sale.
  • Waiver apps: about $19 to $22 a month (WaiverForever, Smartwaiver).
  • Gift card tools: often no monthly bill, but about 2.5% to 3.5% of every card sold, on top of the card fee (Square, Gift Up).

Already have booking?

Keep it. Whichever build you pick, your Book button opens the booking app you already use (Vagaro, Square, Fresha, Mindbody, Booksy and the like), so nothing changes for you or your clients. With In Bloom, booking can sit right on your site instead, running on my server as part of your subscription, or you can keep your own app. That’s your choice, and I’ll ask when you pick In Bloom.

Can I start small and add more later?

Yes. Start with what you need now. When you’re ready for more pages, booking set up for you or right on your site, or selling, what you paid counts toward the bigger build, so moving from Planted to Tended, or Tended to In Bloom, doesn’t mean paying twice. Your subscription moves up with it.

How long does it take?

About 2 weeks for Planted, 3 for Tended and 4 for In Bloom. Your launch date starts counting the day your deposit clears, and I email it to you. I need your answers and photos within a week of that; if they come later, your launch date moves back by the same number of days. While I build, I set up what your site needs, like your newsletter or booking, in your name and with your email, then hand you the logins at launch. You’ll get one or two confirm-your-email messages from them; just click them. Your part is small: add me as a manager on your Google profile (about 2 minutes), or if you don’t have one yet, I build it, you do Google’s one quick check, and at launch I make you its owner. Taking payments? You make your own Stripe account, with my help over email.

No rush, but if your content isn’t in 30 days after you say yes, I’ll nudge you kindly, and at 60 days the project closes so neither of us is left hanging. Your deposit covers the work done so far, and if you pick it back up within a year, it counts toward finishing.

What if I want to leave?

It’s easy and free, because everything is already in your name. The accounts I set up for you, like your newsletter on Buttondown or your booking on Cal.com, are made with your email, and their logins have been yours since launch. Your Google profile and your Stripe account (if you take payments) are yours too, with me added as a helper. Your web address is legally yours as well: I hold it for you, with you listed as the owner, and renew it as part of your subscription.

If you ever cancel your subscription, I hand you every file and login within a week, your web address too, and your site stays up the whole time. With In Bloom, your booking, shop, gift certificates and waivers can’t keep running on my server, so I help you move them into accounts of your own.

Your subscription ends at the end of the month you’ve paid for.

Will people find me on Google? (SEO)

Your site is built to be found: fast, phone-first, with clear titles and descriptions for Google, your business details written so Google can read them, and a photo preview when someone texts your link. Every build also sets up your Google Business Profile, the listing on the map. Your subscription includes holiday heads-ups, with your hours updated on your site and your Google profile, and with Tended or In Bloom, what you post lands there too.

What I won’t do is promise you’ll outrank a national chain. Nobody honestly can.

Do I have to write everything?

Nope. You answer a few easy questions about your work by email, and I write your words from that. You read them, tell me anything that doesn’t sound like you, and I fix it.

And if you make your own art, like flash, lettering, patterns or drawings, send it over and I’ll build it into your site: headers, dividers, buttons, backgrounds, even the little animation. It should look like you made it.
