How it works

Four steps, start to finish.

  1. We chat

    45 minutes about your work. I write your words from it, so you don’t have to.

  2. I draft

    A first draft in about a week, on a private link you can open on your phone.

  3. You tweak

    Two rounds of changes. Put everything for each round in one email.

  4. It goes live

    At your own web address, plus 30 days of settling-in tweaks on me.

Ready in about 2, 3 or 4 weeks (Maiden, Mother, Crone) from when your content is in.

Who you’d be working with

Hi, I’m Taya.

I tend gardens here in Humboldt County, and I build websites for the small businesses around me. I made my own garden business’s site first, then one for my friend Juliet, and loved it so much it turned into this.

I’m happy to build for any small local business, here or anywhere. It’s just me, so I only take a few new builds a month, and when you write, I’m the one who writes back. (I’m also the one reading your About page out loud to make sure it sounds like you 😂)

Questions

Every detail, said plainly.

Something missing? Email me and I’ll answer it straight.

Any other costs?

Just one you can count on: your web address, about $12 a year. Your first year is included in your build, and with care it’s covered.

Everything else is optional, only if you want it. Sending email from your own address is about $3 a month. A newsletter is free up to 100 subscribers, then about $9 a month (care covers both, the newsletter up to 1,000 subscribers). Online booking lives in your own Cal.com account, free for one person, and it can take payments through your own Stripe. Taking card payments costs about 3% + 30¢ per payment (it varies a little by payment company).

Hosting is free, forever (and if that ever changed, I’d move your site for free). These are estimates as of September 2026.

What does care include?

I make your changes whenever you ask, by email, and reply within 2 business days. Your web address, sending email from your own address and your newsletter (up to 1,000 subscribers) are all covered. Anything big, like a new page, I’ll quote first.

It’s $69 a month, or $690 a year (two months free), and billing starts after your 30 days of settling in, not at launch. Cancel anytime, and your site stays yours. Fixes for anything I got wrong are always free, with or without care.

How do changes work without care?

Send me one email with everything you’d like changed. I’ll reply with a price before I start ($100 an hour; most small changes, like new hours, a price or a photo, come to about $25 to $50). Nothing starts until you say yes. Fixes for anything I got wrong are always free.

How does paying work?

Half to start (it holds your spot) and half at launch, by card or bank transfer.

Founding clients get half off the build, and care locked at $49 a month or $490 a year.

How long does it take?

About 2 weeks for Maiden, 3 for Mother and 4 for Crone, counted from when your content is in. You’ll see a draft in about a week, then two rounds of changes. More rounds are priced the same way ($100 an hour), and I’ll tell you the price first.

No rush, but if your content isn’t in 30 days after you say yes, I’ll nudge you kindly, and at 60 days the project closes so neither of us is left hanging. Your deposit covers the work done so far, and if you pick it back up within a year, it counts toward finishing.

What if I want to leave?

It’s easy and free. Everything is yours, and your web address is already in your name. I move it all over to you within a week, and your site never goes down. That’s your web address, your site files and your newsletter list.

Monthly care ends at the end of the month you’ve paid for, and with yearly care, unused full months are refunded.

Will people find me on Google? (SEO)

Your site is built to be found: fast, phone-first, with clear titles and descriptions for Google, your business details written so Google can read them, and a photo preview when someone texts your link. Mother and Crone also set up your Google Business Profile, the listing on the map.

What I won’t do is promise you’ll outrank a national chain. Nobody honestly can.

Do I have to write everything?

Nope. We talk for 45 minutes about your work, and I write your words from that. You read them, tell me anything that doesn’t sound like you, and I fix it.
