How it works

Four steps, start to finish.

  1. We chat

    45 minutes about your work. I write your words from it, so you don’t have to.

  2. I draft

    A first draft in about a week, on a private link you can open on your phone.

  3. You tweak

    Two rounds of changes. Put everything for each round in one email.

  4. It goes live

    At your own web address, plus 30 days of settling-in tweaks on me. With care, it carries right on: just keep emailing me.

Ready in about 2, 3 or 4 weeks (Maiden, Mother, Crone) from when your content is in.

Who you’d be working with

Hi, I’m Taya.

I tend gardens here in Humboldt County, and I build websites for the small businesses around me. I made my own garden business’s site first, then one for my friend Juliet, and loved it so much it turned into this.

I’m happy to build for any small local business, here or anywhere. It’s just me, so I only take a few new builds a month, and when you write, I’m the one who writes back. That doesn’t stop at launch. (I’m also the one reading your About page out loud to make sure it sounds like you 😂)

Questions

Every detail, said plainly.

Something missing? Email me and I’ll answer it straight.

Any other costs?

Just one you can count on: your web address, about $12 a year. Your first year is included in your build, and with care it’s covered, so there’s nothing to renew.

Everything else is optional, only if you want it. Sending email from your own address is about $3 a month. A newsletter is free up to 100 subscribers, then about $9 a month (care covers both, the newsletter up to 1,000 subscribers). Online booking lives in your own Cal.com account, free for one person, and it can take payments through your own Stripe. Taking card payments costs about 3% + 30¢ per payment (it varies a little by payment company).

Hosting is free, forever (and if that ever changed, I’d move your site for free). These are estimates as of September 2026.

What does care include?

Think of me as your web person. When something changes, like new hours, a price, a photo or a holiday closure, just email me and I’ll take care of it, usually within 2 business days. I make your changes whenever you ask, with no hours to count and no price to wait on for the little things. Anything big, like a new page, I’ll quote first.

Your web address, sending email from your own address and your newsletter (up to 1,000 subscribers) are all covered, all on one bill. I keep your Google profile fresh too: hours and holidays, new photos, a monthly post, and a review card for your counter.

It’s $69 a month, or $690 a year (two months free). Billing starts after your 30 days of settling in, not at launch, so your free tweaks lead straight into care, with no gap. Cancel anytime, and your site stays yours. Fixes for anything I got wrong are always free, with or without care.

Is it worth it? Most small changes come to about $25 to $50 without care, so care costs about the same as two a month, and it covers your web address, email and newsletter too. If it’s just a few changes a year, paying as you go costs less, and that’s fine too. The two-minute quiz does the math with your own answers.

How do changes work without care?

Send me one email with everything you’d like changed. I’ll reply with a price before I start ($100 an hour; most small changes, like new hours, a price or a photo, come to about $25 to $50). Nothing starts until you say yes. Fixes for anything I got wrong are always free. If you find yourself writing more than once or twice a month, or you’d simply rather not think about it, care is there anytime.

How does paying work?

Half to start (it holds your spot) and half at launch, by card or bank transfer.

Founding clients get half off the build, and half off care for as long as they keep it: $35 a month or $350 a year.

How long does it take?

About 2 weeks for Maiden, 3 for Mother and 4 for Crone, counted from when your content is in. You’ll see a draft in about a week, then two rounds of changes. More rounds are priced the same way ($100 an hour), and I’ll tell you the price first.

No rush, but if your content isn’t in 30 days after you say yes, I’ll nudge you kindly, and at 60 days the project closes so neither of us is left hanging. Your deposit covers the work done so far, and if you pick it back up within a year, it counts toward finishing.

What if I want to leave?

It’s easy and free. Everything is yours, and your web address is already in your name. I move it all over to you within a week, and your site never goes down. That’s your web address, your site files and your newsletter list.

Monthly care ends at the end of the month you’ve paid for, and with yearly care, unused full months are refunded.

Will people find me on Google? (SEO)

Your site is built to be found: fast, phone-first, with clear titles and descriptions for Google, your business details written so Google can read them, and a photo preview when someone texts your link. Every build also sets up your Google Business Profile, the listing on the map, and with care I keep it fresh for you: the right hours on holidays, new photos and a monthly post.

What I won’t do is promise you’ll outrank a national chain. Nobody honestly can.

Do I have to write everything?

Nope. We talk for 45 minutes about your work, and I write your words from that. You read them, tell me anything that doesn’t sound like you, and I fix it.

And if you make your own art, like flash, lettering, patterns or drawings, send it over and I’ll build it into your site: headers, dividers, buttons, backgrounds, even the little animation. It should look like you made it, not like a template.
