How it works

Seven steps to blast off your site

No pressure, no rush, snacks optional.

1

Mockup sent

Tell me about your business and within a week you get a free mockup of your own site, on a private link. Tap anywhere on it to leave notes.

Your part: Look and leave notes

2

Choosing

We talk through which build fits you (the quiz helps). You pick one.

Your part: Pick a build

3

Getting started

One sitting in your portal: a few easy questions, the agreement, then half the build as a deposit, which holds your spot. Your launch date starts counting the day the deposit clears, and I email it to you.

Your part: Answer, sign, pay

4

Setup

I set up your accounts in your name: your web address, and whatever your build uses, like booking or Stripe. Anything tricky comes with a short video.

Your part: Only the parts you have to do

5

Build and review

Your mockup becomes the real site, with your words written from your answers, and you can ask for changes any time. Two days before launch I send a preview of the whole site. Tell me “yes, go” or “not yet”, plus any changes you’d like. If it’s not yet, I’ll tell you whether your date still works or we pick a new one.

Your part: Look it over and say go

6

Launch, then settling in

Your site goes live at your own web address on your launch date. For the next 30 days, tweaks are on me. Your subscription starts after that.

Together: 30 days of settling in

7

Care

Your subscription keeps it healthy: hosting, a monthly check-in, updates as you post, and changes by message, usually within 2 business days.

Your part: Tell me what’s new

How long

Ready in about 2, 3 or 4 weeks (Pocket, Color, Advance) from the day your deposit clears. I need your answers and photos within a week of it; if they come later, your launch date moves back by the same number of days.
