Our agreement

Between Taya of Web Faery (Humboldt County, California) and you. Version of October 10, 2026.

  1. What I’ll make. Your build (Planted, Tended or In Bloom, as named on your signed copy): Planted: One page with who you are, what you offer and how to reach you (a tap to call, text or email, and a Book now button to the booking app you already use, if you have one), planted and kept healthy. Tended: A full site with a contact form, newsletter sign-up and a Book now button that opens your booking page (the app you use now, or a free Cal.com), tended as the seasons change. In Bloom: Everything in Tended, plus booking right on your site, payments or a small shop (up to about 20 items) in your own accounts: your site in full bloom, doing business for you. Every build comes with your words written for you from your answers, a phone-first design, the Google basics (clear titles and descriptions, business details Google can read, a photo preview when someone texts your link), your Google profile set up, a slim top banner for news, a one-page brand sheet (your colors and fonts, and your logo if you have one, ready for any designer), your web address’s first year, 30 days of settling-in tweaks, and a one-page “Your site” hand-off sheet.
  2. The price. Planted $600, Tended $1,200, or In Bloom $1,800 for the build (while a founding spot is open, half off the build; a trade or a gift is the price as we agreed). Your own price is written on your signed copy. Half to start (your deposit), which holds your spot, and half at launch (the balance). By card or US bank account.
  3. How we’ll work. You send your content: the answers on your getting-started page and your photos. The build clock starts the day all your content is in. I send a first draft in about a week, on a private link. You get two rounds of changes; each round is one batch, with everything in it: notes on your draft, or one email. Any more rounds, I quote first, and nothing starts until you say yes. Your site is ready in about 2, 3 or 4 weeks (Planted, Tended, In Bloom) from when your content is in.
  4. If life gets busy. If your content isn’t in 30 days after you say yes, I’ll nudge you gently. At 60 days the project closes, so my calendar stays fair to everyone waiting. Your deposit covers the work done so far, and if you pick it back up within a year, it counts toward finishing.
  5. Launch. Your site goes live at your own web address, I hand you the logins to the accounts I set up for you, and the second half is due. Then 30 days of settling-in tweaks are included.
  6. After launch. Your subscription, at your build’s monthly price ($12, $45 or $90 a month), starting after your 30 days of settling in. It covers hosting, your web address and backups, and if you post on Instagram or Facebook, I can follow along and put your updates on your website and Google profile (I don’t post on your socials for you), or you can tap it on your site or email me your changes, as often as you need (changes usually go up within 2 business days). If anything breaks, or I got something wrong, I fix it. Big new things, like a new page or a new feature, I quote first. Your subscription doesn’t cover Stripe’s card fee (about 2.9% + 30¢ per payment, taken from your own sales), paid plans of apps you already use (like Square, Vagaro or Acuity), ads, or a newsletter list over 1,000 people.
  7. What’s yours. Everything: your site, your words, your photos, your web address (with you as its legal owner, even while I hold it and renew it for you as part of your subscription), your Google profile, your newsletter list, and your booking and payment accounts. Any account I set up for you, like your newsletter or booking, is made in your name, with your email, and its own strong password, which I hand you at launch for you to change. A Google profile I build for you is yours at launch too: I make you its owner. After that, where I need a way in, I’m added as a helper. Taking payments? Stripe needs your own ID, tax number and bank account, so you make that account yourself, with my help over email, and add me as a Developer. I won’t ask for a password you already use. If you ever cancel your subscription, your site is still yours: I hand over every file and login within a week, your web address too, and your site stays up the whole time. With In Bloom, your booking, payments or shop can’t keep running on my server without a subscription, so I help you move them into your own accounts. Your subscription is monthly, and it ends at the end of the month you’ve paid for.
  8. Hosting. Hosting is part of your subscription, so there’s nothing else to pay for it. Most sites live on GitHub Pages; an In Bloom site that takes payments or runs a shop lives on Cloudflare Pages. If either one ever changes its rules or prices, moving your site is part of your subscription too. If you ever cancel it, your files are yours to host anywhere you like.
  9. Your part. Send only photos, logos and words you have the right to use, and tell me if anything on your site is wrong. While I build, click the one or two confirm-your-email messages from the accounts I set up for you.
  10. Honest limits. I build carefully for Google, but no one can promise a ranking or a number of new clients. Outside services (Google, your booking app, your payment company, your newsletter app, Porkbun) have their own rules and prices, which can change; the prices here are estimates as of October 2026.
  11. Upgrading later. If you outgrow your build, what you paid counts toward a bigger one.
  12. Privacy. What you share here is only for building your site. I don’t share or sell it.

These are the full words of this version, the same for everyone who signs it. Your own build and price are on the copy I email you when you sign. This page stays exactly as it is; when the words change, a new version gets its own page.
