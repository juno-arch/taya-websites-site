Questions

Got questions? Great.

I like questions. Here’s what folks usually ask. Anything else, email me and I’ll answer.

How does paying work, and are there other costs?

Half to start (it holds your spot) and half at launch, by US bank account. My first 5 founding clients get half off the build.

After launch, there’s just your subscription: $12, $45 or $90 a month, matching your site. It starts after your 30 days of settling in, and if you ever cancel, your site is still yours and I hand you every file and login. Your web address’s first year comes with your build. After that, your subscription pays for these, so there’s nothing to renew:

  • Pocket: hosting, backups and your web address.
  • Color: all that, plus email from your domain address and your Instagram feed on your site.
  • Advance: all that, plus your booking, shop, gift certificates and waivers, running on my server.

Your subscription doesn’t pay for Stripe’s card fee (about 2.9% + 30¢ per payment, taken from your own sales), paid plans of apps you already use (Square, Vagaro, Acuity and the like), ads, or the paid plan of your newsletter tool, Buttondown. New to online booking? With Color, I set you up with Cal.com, free for one person and in your name, and with Advance, booking runs right on your site, on my server. Would you like email from your domain address? It comes with Color and Advance.

These are estimates as of October 2026.

What does your subscription include?

Your subscription is what keeps your site healthy and growing, so every site comes with one. If you ever cancel it, your site is still yours and I hand you every file and login.

Pocket, $12 a month:

  • Hosting, your web address, security and backups
  • Your magic site, yours for good: whenever something small needs changing, you just message me through it
  • A “your changes are up” email the moment it’s live
  • A monthly check-in about your business, so you don’t have to think about what to update
  • New reviews added as they come in

Color, $45 a month: everything in Pocket, plus:

  • Post once, show up everywhere: when you post on Instagram or Facebook, I make your new post show up on your website and your Google profile too. I only post what you’ve made or said yes to.
  • Contact form and newsletter sign-up
  • Your Instagram feed on your site, filling itself in as you post
  • Email from your domain address, like you@yourbusiness.com, covered
  • A small shop, up to 5 products, with buy buttons (it runs through your own Stripe account, and Stripe’s normal card fee applies)
  • Gift cards, through Gift Up in your own account (their small fee applies)
  • A DoorDash button on your site for food and drink businesses, linking to your DoorDash page
  • A seasonal refresh: new photos and wording as the year turns

Advance, $90 a month: everything in Color, plus:

  • Your booking, shop, gift certificates and waivers, running on my server
  • Your own portal, built around your business, with its own sign-in
  • Fuller direct ordering for food businesses: DoorDash’s free online ordering built into your site, beyond the Color button
  • A flyer for one event a month, made and posted for you
  • A yearly refresh: new photos from your year, your story updated, every page checked on phones

Every plan’s subscription also includes:

  • I follow your socials and update your site as you post, or you email me
  • Changes usually within 2 business days
  • I watch over your site: if it goes down, I know before you do, and if anything breaks, I fix it
  • Holiday heads-ups, with your hours updated on your site and your Google profile
  • If a host changes its rules, moving your site is on me
  • Private visitor counts, no tracking

New hours, a price, a photo, new wording, as often as you need. Big new things, like a new page or a new feature, I quote first.

Every account is in your name. The ones I set up for you, like your newsletter or booking, are made with your email, and I hand you the logins at launch. Where I need a way in after that, I’m added as a helper. What your subscription pays for, and what it doesn’t, is under paying and other costs.

Already have booking?

Keep it. Whichever build you pick, your Book button opens the booking app you already use (Vagaro, Square, Fresha, Mindbody, Booksy and the like), so nothing changes for you or your clients. With Advance, booking can sit right on your site instead, running on my server as part of your subscription, or you can keep your own app. That’s your choice, and I’ll ask when you pick Advance.

No booking app yet? With Color, I set you up with Cal.com, free for one person and in your name: clients pick a time, and you both get reminders. With Advance, booking comes built right into your site. Or keep it simple with Pocket: people call, text or email you.

If your link or your app ever changes, just email me and I’ll update it.

Can I start small and add more later?

Yes. Start with what you need now. When you’re ready for more pages, booking set up for you or right on your site, or selling, what you paid counts toward the bigger build, so moving from Pocket to Color, or Color to Advance, doesn’t mean paying twice. Your subscription moves up with it.

How long does it take?

About 2 weeks for Pocket, 3 for Color and 4 for Advance. Your launch date starts counting the day your deposit clears, and I email it to you. I need your answers and photos within a week of that; if they come later, your launch date moves back by the same number of days. Two days before launch I send you a preview of the whole site, and you tell me “yes, go” or “not yet”. Anything big and new I’ll quote first.

While I build, I set up what your site needs, like your newsletter or booking, in your name and with your email, then hand you the logins at launch. You’ll get one or two confirm-your-email messages from them; just click them. Your part is small: add me as a manager on your Google profile (about 2 minutes), or if you don’t have one yet, I build it, you do Google’s one quick check, and at launch I make you its owner. Taking payments? You make your own Stripe account, with my help over email.

No rush, but if your content isn’t in 30 days after you say yes, I’ll nudge you kindly, and at 60 days the project closes so neither of us is left hanging. Your deposit covers the work done so far, and if you pick it back up within a year, it counts toward finishing.

What if I want to leave?

It’s easy and free, because everything is already in your name. The accounts I set up for you, like your newsletter on Buttondown or your booking on Cal.com, are made with your email, and their logins have been yours since launch. Your Google profile and your Stripe account (if you take payments) are yours too, with me added as a helper. Your web address is legally yours as well: I hold it for you, with you listed as the owner, and renew it as part of your subscription.

If you ever cancel your subscription, I hand you every file and login within a week, your web address too, and your site stays up the whole time. With Advance, your booking, shop, gift certificates and waivers can’t keep running on my server, so I help you move them into accounts of your own.

Your subscription ends at the end of the month you’ve paid for.

Will people find me on Google? (SEO)

Your site is built to be found: fast, phone-first, with clear titles and descriptions for Google, your business details written so Google can read them, and a photo preview when someone texts your link. Every build also sets up your Google Business Profile, the listing on the map. Your subscription includes holiday heads-ups, with your hours updated on your site and your Google profile, and with Color or Advance, what you post lands there too.

What I won’t do is promise you’ll outrank a national chain. Nobody honestly can.

Do I have to write everything?

Nope. You answer a few easy questions about your work by email, and I write your words from that. You read them, tell me anything that doesn’t sound like you, and I fix it.

And if you make your own art, like flash, lettering, patterns or drawings, send it over and I’ll build it into your site: headers, dividers, buttons, backgrounds, even the little animation. It should look like you made it.

Anything else? Ask away.
